# Definition

The **kuṇḍalinī** (*kulakuṇḍalinī*, "coiled serpentine") is the **parāśakti**
lying dormant at the **Svayambhūliṅga** in the ultimate state of crudification.
Ánanda Sútram 4-7: *"Sthūliibhavane nidritā sā kuṇḍalinii."* It is the **force of
fundamental negativity** (4-8, *"Kuṇḍalinii sā mūliibhūtā rṇātmikā"*).

# Explanation

At the **last expressional point** — the fringe of the Bhavānī
[śakti](/cosmology/stages-of-prakrti.md), the ultimate state of crudity — the
*parāśakti* (the introversial, pervasive force) lies in a **quiescent state as the
jīvabhāva** (finite subjectivity). Coiled like a serpent at the
[Svayambhūliṅga](/cosmology/shambhu-svayambhu-linga.md), it is the **force of
fundamental negativity** (*ṛṇātmikā*), the polar counterpart of the *fundamental
positivity* at the Shambhūliṅga.

Being the **introversial** force, the kuṇḍalinī is the unit-level seat of the
return movement [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md): in sādhanā this
dormant force is the one to be awakened and raised.

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — the negative force against Avidyāmāyā

The 1959 cosmology (*The Creation of the Universe*, II-8) develops the sūtra's
cosmological frame:

- **Kuṇḍalinī = the "theoretical negative force" of the unit body.** Since mokṣa
  requires retracing against the singular positive force of the Macrocosm's
  thought-projection, "the path of non-qualified spiritual practice which can reach
  final mokṣa is always the path of negativity. The theoretical negative force with
  its fundamental negativity, which tends towards the final merger into Puruṣa for
  attaining mokṣa, is called **kulakuṇḍalinī — the 'coiled serpentine.'**"
- **It is different for each individual** — being the negative force of the unit
  body — and its starting point is in the **mūlādhāra**, that portion of the body
  from which the crudest matter (*kṣititattva*) is controlled.
- **The arena is the kāma-pīṭha.** Just as the Cosmic *kāmabīja* (point of the
  positive resultant) lies on the back of **Shambhūliṅga**, the negative
  kāmabīja of the living being lies on the back of **Svayambhūliṅga**. The yoga
  sādhaka raises this "fundamental negativity" aggressively against the force of
  Avidyāmāyā — "This path of non-compromising aggressive spirituality is the only
  way to ultimate oneness with Infinite Consciousness."

# In *Neohumanism in a Nutshell* (1987) — ascent along the kulakuṇḍalinī to the guṇātrikona

*Physical Propulsion, Psychic Propensities and Spiritual Attainment* (nh-2-11, 1 Jan
1987, Tiljala, Calcutta) gives the final stage of psychic-spiritual attainment:

- **Contact with the kaoṣikī śakti.** "In the last stage of this movement, that is, in
  the spiritual sphere, one comes in contact with the kaoṣikī śakti, the primordial
  force of creation." This is reached "through the lokatrikona which was established
  through pramā siddhi" — whose three sides are pramā saṃvṛddhi, pramā ṛddhi and pramā
  siddhi — approaching "the vertex of the guṇātrikona," "the particular vertex of the
  unbalanced triangle, from which the kaoṣikī śakti came out moving in a straight
  linear order, not in a systaltic way."
- **The ascent.** "In this final stage of spiritual practice, the kaoṣikī śakti will
  move in a straight line in the opposite direction, along the upward movement of
  kulakuṇḍalinī, and finally one will become one with the guṇātrikona. This is the
  state of perfect consummation of human existence; this is the glory of supreme
  spiritual attainment."

## Relationships

- the parāśakti dormant at the [Svayambhūliṅga](/cosmology/shambhu-svayambhu-linga.md) (fundamental negativity)
- a quiescent form of [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) as finite subjectivity (jīvabhāva)
- the introversial force — unit-level seat of [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md), raised in sādhanā
- *Idea and Ideology*: the negative force raised against [Avidyāmāyā](/cosmology/vidya-avidya.md), starting at [mūlādhāra](/biopsychology/cakra.md) in the kāma-pīṭha, polarized by [Shambhūliṅga / Svayambhūliṅga](/cosmology/shambhu-svayambhu-linga.md) — the path of non-qualified mokṣa
- *NHN 2/11*: final stage = contact with the *kaoṣikī śakti* ("the primordial force of creation") at the unbalanced vertex of the guṇātrikona; the *kaoṣikī śakti* then moves "along the upward movement of kulakuṇḍalinī" until "one will become one with the guṇātrikona" (cf. [Pramā](/philosophy/prama.md))

# Notes — fidelity

- In Ánanda Sútram ch. 4 the kuṇḍalinī is introduced **cosmologically** (force of
  fundamental negativity), not yet as a cakra-by-cakra practice manual. Keep this
  definition; the practical raising is treated in Sarkar's other works.
- II-8 is consistent and more explicit: kulakuṇḍalinī is *per-individual*, starts at
  mūlādhāra, resides in the kāma-pīṭha, and opposes Avidyāmāyā. Note the Tantric
  vocabulary (kāmabīja, kāma-pīṭha, Shambhūliṅga/Svayambhūliṅga) is Sarkar's own.
