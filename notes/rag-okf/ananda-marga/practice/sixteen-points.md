# Definition

The **Sixteen Points** are Sarkar's sixteen-fold code of personal discipline binding on
every Ananda Margi — a single regimen spanning physical hygiene, diet, fasting, regular
spiritual practice, doctrinal loyalty, and organizational participation. They are issued
in *Ānanda Mārga Caryācarya* Part 2 (c. 1975, Patna) and named in the
[Conduct Rules](/practice/conduct-rules.md) as a binding obligation ("Strictly adhering
to the code of 16 Points").

# The sixteen points

As given verbatim in Caryācarya 2/7:

1. After urination, wash the urinary organ with water.
2. Males should either be circumcised or keep the foreskin pulled back at all times.
3. Never cut the hair of the joints of the body.
4. Males should always use kaopiina (laungoṭā).
5. Do vyāpaka shaoca as directed.
6. Bathe according to the prescribed system.
7. Take only sāttvika (sentient) food.
8. Observe fast as prescribed.
9. Do sādhana regularly.
10. Observe uncompromising strictness and faith regarding the sanctity of the Iṣṭa (Goal).
11. Observe uncompromising strictness and faith regarding the sanctity of the Ādarsha (Ideology).
12. Observe uncompromising strictness and faith regarding the sanctity of the Supreme Command.
13. Observe uncompromising strictness and faith regarding the sanctity of the Conduct Rules.
14. Always remember the content of your oaths.
15. Regular participation in the weekly dharmacakra at the local jāgrti should be considered mandatory.
16. Observe C.S.D.K. (Conduct Rules, Seminar, Duty, Kiirtana).

# Explanation

The code moves outward in widening circles: **points 1–9** govern the body and individual
practice (cleanliness, *vyāpaka shaoca*, sentient/*sāttvika* food, prescribed fasting, and
daily [sādhana](/practice/sadhana.md)); **points 10–13** are the **four sanctities** —
loyalty to the [Iṣṭa](/practice/ishta.md) (the personal goal/Goal), the Ādarsha (Ideology),
the Supreme Command, and the [Conduct Rules](/practice/conduct-rules.md); **points 14–16**
fix organizational life — one's oaths, mandatory weekly *dharmacakra* (collective practice
at the local *jāgrti*), and **CSDK** (Conduct rules, Seminar, Duty, [Kiirtana](/practice/kiirtana.md)).

## Relationships

- a binding obligation under the [Conduct Rules for Sādhakas](/practice/conduct-rules.md) (rule 4); a sibling code to the [Fifteen Shiilas](/practice/fifteen-shiilas.md)
- point 9 mandates daily [sādhana](/practice/sadhana.md); point 16 (CSDK) includes collective [kiirtana](/practice/kiirtana.md)
- points 10–13 enshrine loyalty to the [Iṣṭa](/practice/ishta.md), the Ādarsha (Ideology), the Supreme Command and the [Conduct Rules](/practice/conduct-rules.md)
- point 7 (sentient/sāttvika food) and point 8 (fasting) connect to the dietary/physical practice code of Caryācarya Part 3

# Notes — fidelity

- **Primary, doctrinally authoritative source = Caryācarya 2/7 (Sarkar's own text).** This
  is distinct from the later disciple compilation *The Sixteen Points: Our Fundamental
  Duty* by Ac. Shraddhananda Avadhuta — a **secondary** explanatory booklet, not Baba's
  words; it is not used to ground this node.
- The enumeration is reproduced verbatim (these are directives). "Dharmacakra" (weekly
  collective practice) is referenced as plain text — no concept node exists for it yet.
