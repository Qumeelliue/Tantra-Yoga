# Practice

Spiritual practice and conduct — sādhanā, Tantra, kiirtana, yama-niyama, and the
Sixteen Points. Entries use `type: Practice`.

# Entries

* [Samādhi](samadhi.md) - Absorptive merger of the unit mind: savikalpa (Macrocosmic Mahat) and nirvikalpa (the Ātman).
* [Bhakti (Devotion)](bhakti.md) - Devotion as ideation on God, not ritual or flattery; petitionary prayer is a source of confusion.
* [Guru (Sadguru)](guru.md) - The longing for liberation draws the sadguru; ultimately Brahma alone is the guru.
* [Obstacles as Helping Force](obstacles.md) - On the path of sādhanā, obstacles are friends — the counter-effort carries one to the goal.
* [Kuṇḍalinī (Kulakuṇḍalinī)](kundalini.md) - The dormant parāśakti at the Svayambhūliṅga; the introversial force raised in sādhanā.
* [Tantra](tantra.md) - The practical cult of Sadāśiva — liberation from staticity; in Sarkar's framing identical to sādhanā and to yoga.
* [Iṣṭa (Object of Ideation)](ishta.md) - The chosen personal God / object of ideation and adoration, inseparable from dharma.
* [Mantra](mantra.md) - Sound whose meditation liberates; awakened (mantra caetanya) by the guru; the iṣṭa-mantra and guru-mantra.
* [Kiirtana](kiirtana.md) - Singing the siddha mantra "Bábá Náma Kevalam" with the lalita mármika dance — the chief aid to sādhanā.
* [Sādhanā](sadhana.md) - Spiritual practice: reuniting Ātman with Paramātman by eliminating saṃskāras; the hub of Tantra/mantra/kiirtana, incl. Madhuvidyā.
* [Yoga](yoga.md) - Unification (saṃyoga) of jīvātman with Paramātman; the three branches jñāna/karma/bhakti; "yoga ≈ tantra."
* [Mokṣa & Liberation](moksa.md) - Permanent liberation from all bondages; the five grades as cakra-feelings; fulfilled only through devotion.
* [Dhyāna (Meditation)](dhyana.md) - The concentration process (physico-psychic → psycho-spiritual); four stages; abhidhyāna and ārādhanā.
* [Bhāva & Mahābhāva](bhava.md) - The graded states of spiritual ecstasy; mahābhāva (tactual presence of Parama Puruṣa) as a kind of savikalpa samādhi.
* [Sevā (Service)](seva.md) - Service as spiritual activity (four kinds); serving creation serves Him; karma yoga, the form devotion takes in action.
* [Tāṇḍava & Kaoṣikī](tandava.md) - The two devised dance-exercises: Tāṇḍava (Sadāśiva's "fight against death," for men) and Kaoṣikī (Sarkar's 1978 all-round health dance for everyone).
* [Dīkṣā (Initiation)](diksa.md) - Spiritual initiation in two phases (Vaedikii / Tāntrikii); diipanii + mantrāghāta + mantra caetanya; the "twice-born" (dvija) second birth.
* [Mysticism](mysticism.md) - The never-ending endeavour to link finite with Infinite; the first phase of non-attributional devotion; "the search for the Great by the little."
* [Yama & Niyama](yama-niyama.md) - The ten-fold cardinal moral code; first two limbs of aṣṭāṅga yoga and the prerequisite "holy water" of sādhanā.
* [Grace (Kṛpā)](grace.md) - Descending divine action: grace rains on all, felt only by removing the "umbrella of vanity"; union depends on grace, not intellect.
* [Fifteen Shiilas (Paiñcadasha Shiila)](fifteen-shiilas.md) - The fifteen rules of behaviour — character and social conduct of the sādhaka; rule 10 binds one to Yama-Niyama (Caryācarya 2/5).
* [Sixteen Points](sixteen-points.md) - The sixteen-fold code of physical, dietary and spiritual discipline mandatory for Ananda Margis; the four sanctities, weekly dharmacakra and CSDK (Caryācarya 2/7).
* [Conduct Rules for Sādhakas](conduct-rules.md) - The four binding rules integrating the Shiilas, the Caryācarya, the four sanctities and the Sixteen Points (Caryācarya 2/6).
* [Dharmacakra (Collective Spiritual Practice)](dharmacakra.md) - Weekly congregation for collective Iishvara Praṇidhāna (the Saṃgacchadhvaṃ mantra); its large-scale form, Dharma Mahācakra, is held in the Guru's presence with Tāṇḍava/Kaoṣikī (Caryācarya 1/9, 1/11).
* [Āsana & Prāṇāyāma (Physical Yoga Practices)](asana-pranayama.md) - Posture and breath-control as physical supports for sādhanā — rectifying glands/vṛttis so the mind can concentrate; taught graded, only under an ācārya (Caryācarya 3/8, 3/10).
* [Fasting (Upavāsa)](fasting.md) - Voluntary fasting = "to remain in proximity to Iishvara"; compulsory on ekādashii; drains excess shukra so the mind rises to the higher vṛttis — a fast day is "an excellent time for sādhanā" (Caryācarya 3/3).
* [Three Species of Human Being & the Ācāras](three-species.md) - Pashu / viira / deva elevated by pashvācāra → vīrācāra/ciinācāra → divyācāra; the saṃyoga definition of yoga.
* [Vidyā Tantra & Avidyā Tantra](vidya-avidya-tantra.md) - The six actions of Vidyā Tantra (benevolent) vs the six of Avidyā Tantra (māraṇa, vaśīkaraṇa, uccāṭana, sammohana, stambhana, śāntikarma).
* [Tāntrika Samādhi (Tanmātrika Samādhi)](tantrika-samadhi.md) - Samādhi on the tanmātric point — the silver line between the relative and absolute worlds; its negative form (prakṛtiliina).
* [Subjective Approach through Objective Adjustment](subjective-approach.md) - Hands in worldly duties, mind on the Supreme; the minimum qualification is a human body and mind.
* [The Grades of Samādhi (Tattva Kaomudii)](grades-of-samadhi.md) - Bhāva samādhi (the four lower cakras), dharmamegha samādhi (at the anāhata), and the tanmātrika/rāganugā/abhedajñāna series.
* [Mudrā (Externalization of Inner Feeling)](mudra.md) - Gesture and posture expressing the mind's desires; the third mode of study; Shiva as creator of the fine arts.
* [Sām̐giita (Song, Dance & Music)](samgiita.md) - Singing, instruments and dancing together; the six rāgas and thirty-six rāgiṇīs; the taal derivation from Tāṇḍava and Lalita.
* [Proto-Psycho-Spirituality](proto-psycho-spirituality.md) - The dynamic "running" of the mind toward the Supreme while serving universal welfare; circum-rotarian union with the Hub.
* [Be a First-Class Person](first-class-person.md) - A/B/C-class people by the consistency of thought, word and deed; "speak what you think, do what you speak."
* [The Inner Pilgrimage (Ātma Tīrtha)](atma-tirtha.md) - Átmá is the Supreme, the only tiirtha; external pilgrimage is the error of the támasa.
