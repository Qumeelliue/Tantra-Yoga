# Definition

**Āsana** means *"a position in which one feels comfortable"* — *Sthirasukhamāsanam*
(Caryācarya 3/8). **Prāṇāyāma** is *"the process by which the prāṇas in the body are
controlled"* (3/10). Both are physical practices taken up as **supports for
[sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md)**, taught in graded stages and **only under an
[ācārya](/practice/guru.md)** — *"No one should practise prāṇāyāma without the permission of
an ācārya."*

# Explanation

**Āsana — gland → vṛtti → mind.** Āsanas keep the body healthy, but *"are not prescribed for
the general cure of diseases"* — only those ailments that obstruct meditation. Their deeper
role is psycho-physical: mental expression works through the *vṛttis*, whose predominance
depends on the body's **glands** and hormones; a glandular defect excites particular vṛttis,
so that one *"cannot concentrate … because [the] mind becomes extroverted."* By rectifying
the glands, *"āsanas are an important part of sādhanā."* Of *"more than 50,000 āsanas,"* the
ācārya teaches each person only those they need; many are named after animals whose
structure furthers a glandular secretion (e.g. *kūrmakāsana*, the tortoise posture, aids
withdrawal of mind from the external world). For dhyāna and dhāraṇā the proper postures are
**padmāsana, siddhāsana and viirāsana**.

**Prāṇāyāma — control of the ten vital airs.** The ten *prāṇāh* — *prāṇa, apāna, samāna,
udāna, vyāna, nāga, kūrma, krkara, devadatta, dhanaiñjaya* — are controlled to **increase
apperceptive power and concentration**; it is *"meant for sādhakas"* (risky for others). In
*Sādhāraṇa Prāṇāyāma* one ideates on the first syllable of the **[Iṣṭa](/practice/ishta.md)
mantra** while inhaling — *"that infinite vital energy is entering … from the infinite
Brahma"* — and on the remaining syllable while exhaling, returning that energy to Brahma.
Like dīkṣā, prāṇāyāma is **graded**: *Sādhāraṇa* (taught by an ācārya), *Sahaja* and
*Visheṣa* (by a *purodhā*), and *Āntar*. Specific therapeutic prāṇāyāmas exist but must not
be mixed with the prāṇāyāma of sādhanā.

# Relationships

- physical supports for [sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md); the bodily limb of [yoga](/practice/yoga.md), rectifying glands so the mind can concentrate (cf. the [vṛttis](/biopsychology/citta.md) / [indriya](/biopsychology/indriya.md))
- taught graded and only under an [ācārya](/practice/guru.md)/purodhā — introduced alongside the [dīkṣā](/practice/diksa.md) yoga grades (Sādhāraṇa/Sahaja/Visheṣa Yoga)
- prāṇāyāma ideation centres on the [Iṣṭa mantra](/practice/ishta.md) and infinite [Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md); some āsanas press the [cakras](/biopsychology/cakra.md) (mūlādhāra, svādhiṣṭhāna)
- preceded by *vyāpaka shaoca* and sentient (sāttvika) food — see [Sixteen Points](/practice/sixteen-points.md) 5–7

# Notes — fidelity
