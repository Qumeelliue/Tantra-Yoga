# Definition

**Proto-psycho-spirituality** is the dynamic "running," "this forward movement" of
the mind advancing psycho-spiritually toward the Supreme while working for universal
welfare: "Since it is the mind which advances towards spirituality, it is called
'psycho-spirituality'. And it is called 'proto' in the sense that it is a flickering
entity – it is like a flickering flame, not like a steady flame." Its structure is
the systalsis: "the mobility portion within the systalsis of proto-psycho-spirituality
is purely psychic; and the blissful staticity within the systalsis is purely
spiritual." It enables one to recognize and expose the "demons in human framework":
"It is not enough for one to personally unmask them; one must open the eyes of others
also."

# Explanation

**The running, not the stance.** "Supposing some boys want to run. If they simply
stand still on the hard earth, will that be considered running? They will have to
actually run. This running, this forward movement, is known as 'proto-psycho-
spirituality'." It is a dynamic force — "If they lose their mobility and sit down on
the ground, that will not be considered running; it will only encourage the
psychology of lethargy."

**Flickering, not complete.** "It is a flickering entity – it is like a flickering
flame, not like a steady flame. It is not something unruffled or fixed; it is
expressive of movement. Hence it is not complete psycho-spirituality but
proto-psycho-spirituality." The systalsis blends strata: "the mobility portion within
the systalsis of proto-psycho-spirituality is purely psychic; and the blissful
staticity within the systalsis is purely spiritual. So it is a happy blending of
psychic and spiritual strata."

**The circum-rotarian universe and its single Centre.** "This universe of ours, as
you know, is circum-rotarian (that is, rotating around its own nucleus). Nothing is
outside its circumference, so it is not necessary to search for its nucleus outside."
"This circum-rotarian universe is controlled by one Centre both collectively and on a
unitary basis. Thus each and every grain of dust, each and every blade of grass – all
are being equally controlled by that Supreme Hub." "If, by some means, an individual
can make his or her individual psycho-spiritual hub coincide with that circum-rotarian
spiritual Hub, then that individual will feel oneness with every grain of dust, with
every blade of grass – that will be his or her psychology."

**Base — sama-samája tattva.** "To train the mind, a base is required. In this case,
the base is sama-samája tattva": "I sincerely believe that all humans have the right
to food, clothes, accommodation, education and medical treatment. It is not enough for
me to accept their rights in principle; as an honest person I should make the utmost
effort to see that they have attained their rights."

**Demons in human framework.** The exploiters are "veritable demons in human
framework" — clever, cunning, "exceptionally clever and cunning," who "by inculcating
various sentiments, misguide not only thousands, but millions, of people to the path
of malevolence." Those who practise proto-psycho-spirituality "can easily recognize
those demons in human framework. Then, after recognizing them, it is their duty to
expose them to others also."

**Human chameleons and the metamorphosed sentimental strategy.** The exploiters
"switch over to a sentiment different from the one which they were previously using"
— "maybe from communalism to nationalism or from nationalism to communism." This
"switch-over is called 'metamorphosed sentimental strategy'." Examples: Maharshi
Kapila, who "quickly invented a new concept of Janya-Iishvara [Proto-God]" when his
Niriishvara-Sáḿkhya faced embarrassment; and Shankaracharya, who taught that
everything is Máyá yet "composed hymns to the River Ganges" — "a very fine example of
a metamorphosed sentimental strategy." The "human chameleons" seek "the peaceful
transference of political power" while "they will retain the same exploitative
machinery. The only difference will be that the white rulers will have been replaced
by black rulers." But "according to natural law, no truth is suppressed forever."

## Relationships

- the social base and companion ideal is [sama-samája tattva](/prout/sama-samaaja-tattva.md), which guarantees the [minimum requirements](/prout/minimum-requirements.md) to all
- opposes the "socio-sentiment" and "geo-sentiment" that [Neohumanism](/philosophy/neohumanism.md) condemns; the metamorphosed sentimental strategy is the tool of [Pseudo-Humanism](/philosophy/pseudo-humanism.md)
- its circum-rotarian Hub is the one Centre of the [cosmic mind](/cosmology/cosmic-mind.md), and coinciding one's hub with it is the [subjective approach](/practice/subjective-approach.md)
- the "running" is a form of [sādhana](/practice/sadhana.md) and [bhakti](/practice/bhakti.md) toward the Supreme
- fights the sentiment-exploitation built into the [samaja-cakra](/prout/samaja-cakra.md) and upheld by the [five fundamental principles](/prout/five-fundamental-principles.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- **Terminology kept as Sarkar's:** "systalsis," "circum-rotarian," "proto-psycho-
  spirituality," "metamorphosed sentimental strategy" and "demons in human framework"
  are his own terms, kept verbatim without paraphrase.
- **"Proto" is explicit:** the "proto" marks a flickering, unfinished, moving state —
  "not complete psycho-spirituality" — not a lesser or partial spirituality in the
  ordinary sense.
- **Two strata preserved:** the psychic mobility and spiritual staticity within the
  systalsis are kept as Sarkar's exact distinction.
- **Could not verify:** the historical characterizations of Maharshi Kapila and
  Shankaracharya are Sarkar's interpretations within the discourse, reproduced as
  such; no independent verification attempted here.
