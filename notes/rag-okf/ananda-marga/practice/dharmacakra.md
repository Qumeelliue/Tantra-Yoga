# Definition

**Dharmacakra** is *"a congregation of spiritual aspirants for collective performance of
Iishvara Praṇidhāna and spiritual discussions"* (Caryācarya 1/9). Aspirants sit in rows
(women and men separately), perform [Iishvara Praṇidhāna](/practice/ishta.md) at the
scheduled time, then join the collective meeting; latecomers take their seat silently.

# Explanation

Each session opens with the **Saṃgacchadhvaṃ** mantra (Ṛgveda), recited three times:

> *Saṃgacchadhvaṃ saṃvadadhvaṃ saṃ vo manāṃsi jānatām, / Devābhāgaṃ yathāpūrve
> saṃjānānā upāsate. / Samānii va ākūtih samānā hrdayānivah, / Samānamastu vo mano
> yathā vah susahāsati.*
> — "Let us move together, radiate the same thought-wave, know our minds together; let us
> share our wealth without differentiation, like sages of the past… let our minds be as one
> mind, so that we … become one with the Supreme."

**Directions (Caryācarya 1/9):** dharmacakra is held **every Sunday** (and on festival days)
at a time set by the local *ācārya*. **When in good health one must attend the weekly
dharmacakra**; if unavoidable duties prevent it, one performs Iishvara Praṇidhāna at the
*jāgrti* later that day, or — failing that — **forgoes one principal meal** at the week's
end. All sit on common seats in socially acceptable dress. Non-Margis with ardent spiritual
longing may attend as spectators by prior permission of the jāgrti manager, but **only
Ananda Margis may ask or answer questions**; a *Tattvasabhā* (spiritual conference) is
arranged for inquisitive non-Margis.

**Dharma Mahācakra (Caryācarya 1/11).** The dharmacakra *"held on a large scale on special
occasions"* is the **dharma mahācakra** (a smaller large-scale form is *dharma
mahāsammelana*) — *"a spiritual congregation held only in the physical presence of Reverend
Mārga Guru."* It must provide for [Tāṇḍava and Kaoṣikī](/practice/tandava.md) dances, and a
public procession with Tāṇḍava is mandatory.

## Relationships

- the collective form of [Iishvara Praṇidhāna](/practice/ishta.md) / [sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md); mandated by [Sixteen Points](/practice/sixteen-points.md) (point 15: weekly dharmacakra mandatory) and the CSDK of point 16
- the **Dharma Mahācakra** requires [Tāṇḍava and Kaoṣikī](/practice/tandava.md) and is held only in the Guru's physical presence
- complemented by collective [Kiirtana](/practice/kiirtana.md); held at the local *jāgrti*
