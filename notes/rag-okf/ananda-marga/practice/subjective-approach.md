# Definition

**Subjective approach through objective adjustment** is Ananda Marga's method: while
moving toward the Supreme Entity, one still does "all [one's] worldly duties in the
social, economic and all other spheres" — "your hands should be engaged in worldly
duties, and your mind should be moving towards the Supreme Entity." (1979, AFPS 4/4.)

# Explanation

- **The minimum qualification for salvation.** In the Shiva–Pārvatī dialogue
  (nigama = the questions, āgama = the answers; the two together = Tantra), Pārvatī
  asks what qualifications are needed for salvation. Shiva answers: **only a human
  body and a human mind** — "no racial bar, no colour bar, no regional bar":
  *"Ātmajñānamidam Devi param mokṣaekasādhanam; sukrtaermānavaḥ bhūtvā jñānīcen
  mokṣamāpnyāt"* — self-knowledge is the greatest means to salvation.
- **Knowing = subjectivization of external objectivity.** The mind has three movements:
  extro-internal (external object moving inward), intro-external (sentiment expressed
  outward), and the movement toward the Spirit. To "know" is the subjectivization of
  external objectivity into the mind's objective compartment; **self-knowledge** is the
  mind moving toward the spirit.
- **How can the mind know the Omniscient?** Parama Puruṣa is the subject, the unit mind
  the object; the mind cannot make Him its object. "There lies the secret": always
  remember that whatever you do and think "is being witnessed by Him." The devotee's
  "very clever policy" — with a pinnacled mind one brings the Supreme Entity "within
  the periphery of [one's] minds," so "the path of devotion or the path of spirituality
  is for those who are extremely cunning and clever. And those who think that Parama
  Puruṣa is not seeing them are the most foolish amongst the fools."
- **The stages of mind in creation.** Inert mind (the quinquelemental world), instinct-
  guided mind (protozoa, plants), a bit of independent mind (developed metazoa, and dogs/
  monkeys via contact with humans), and the developed human mind that can feel "whatever
  one does… is always being witnessed by Parama Puruṣa." Humans are "the most developed
  stage in the process of introversion" — "blessed beings."

# In the *Discourses on Neohumanist Education* (1969, 1982) — devotion's culmination and the East–West adjustment

The *Discourses on Neohumanist Education* expand the phrase into a complete social
philosophy:

- **Devotion culminates "by subjective approach through objective adjustment."**
  (DNE 24, *Devotional Sentiment and Neohumanism*, 21 Feb 1982.) "human beings
  reach the culmination of devotion by subjective approach through objective
  adjustment" — the *introversial aspect* is "the subjective approach," the
  *extroversial aspect* is "the objective adjustment." "the actual task of human
  beings is to maintain a subjective approach – that is, they will advance
  psycho-spiritually towards the Supreme Consciousness, inspired by Neohumanistic
  ideals – while at the same time they must strive for the expansion of humanistic
  principles and thus establish a social structure based on universalism.
  Otherwise, their inner psycho-spiritual rhythms will not be able to properly
  maintain equilibrium with narrow sentiments such as geo-sentiment" (cf.
  [Neohumanism](/philosophy/neohumanism.md), [Bhakti](/practice/bhakti.md)).
- **The East–West adjustment.** (DNE 10, *Talks on Education – Excerpt C*, 1969.)
  "The East is predominantly subjective in outlook, whereas the West has a mainly
  objective bent of mind. The East, throughout its development, has maintained a
  subjective approach, whereas western countries put great stress on objective
  development. Too much emphasis on either one of these approaches is not
  conducive to the all-round growth of the society. We can build up an ideal
  society only on the basis of a happy adjustment between the subjective and the
  objective approaches. Here is the greatness of Ananda Marga ideology" (cf.
  [Education](/philosophy/education.md), [Synthesis & Analysis](/philosophy/synthesis-and-analysis.md)).

# In *Neohumanism in a Nutshell* (1979) — the ideal life as the happy blending

*An Ideal Life* (nh1-3, 2 January 1979) states the same method as the ideal life
itself:

- **The happy blending.** "a real sadhaka [spiritual aspirant] will properly discharge
  their duties in the relative world, and will at the same time attend properly to their
  duties in the spiritual world; and between the two worlds, there will be a happy blending.
  In the external world there should be an objective adjustment, and at the same time there
  should, in the psychic world, be a subjective approach."
- **The withdrawal chain.** "The subjective approach is to first withdraw the crude mind
  gradually from the external world and merge it in the subtle mind, and then to merge the
  subtle mind in unit consciousness. When unit consciousness is merged in Cosmic
  Consciousness, a human being is said to have attained the supreme fulfilment in life."
- **The tragedy of the day.** "In the present world, human beings face such tremendous
  difficulties in maintaining their objective adjustment that they cannot afford to give any
  time for their subjective approach. This is the greatest tragedy of the day! That is why I
  would like to advise you to build a healthy human society as early as possible."

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** the minimum qualification is a human body and mind; "the path of
  spirituality is for those who are extremely cunning and clever"; the witnessing
  reminder is the secret. Keep sharp.
- The **nigama/āgama = Tantra** identification here matches [Tantra](/practice/tantra.md)'s
  prose — a corroborating source.
- **Could not verify in the cited passage:** a developed "objective adjustment" scheme
  beyond the hands/mind split (the phrase is the title; the method is the witnessing
  remembrance).
- **NHN 1/3 (1979) corroborates the method:** the "happy blending" of the two worlds and
  the withdrawal chain (crude mind → subtle mind → unit consciousness → Cosmic
  Consciousness) are stated verbatim in *An Ideal Life*, eleven months before AFPS 4/4.
