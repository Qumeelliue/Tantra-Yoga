# Definition

The **Conduct Rules for Sādhakas** are the four binding rules that summarize a sādhaka's
obligations, issued in *Ānanda Mārga Caryācarya* Part 2 (c. 1975, Patna). They function
as an integrating index: each rule points to one of the other discipline-codes.

# The four rules

As given verbatim in Caryācarya 2/6:

1. Observing Paiñcadasha Shiila (15 Shiilas) in daily life.
2. Following directions in connection with physical, mental, spiritual and social aspects of life given in Caryācarya (Parts 1, 2 and 3).
3. Observing uncompromising strictness and faith regarding the sanctity of Iṣṭa, Ādarsha, the Supreme Command and the Conduct Rules.
4. Strictly adhering to the code of 16 Points.

Sarkar adds that, beyond these, **separate conduct rules** exist for householder ācāryas,
whole-timers, local full-timers, local part-timers, tāttvikas, ācāryas and avadhūtas.

# Explanation

The Conduct Rules bind the discipline-system together: rule 1 invokes the
[Fifteen Shiilas](/practice/fifteen-shiilas.md); rule 2 invokes the whole of Caryācarya;
rule 4 invokes the [Sixteen Points](/practice/sixteen-points.md). Rule 3 names the **four
sanctities** — the [Iṣṭa](/practice/ishta.md), the Ādarsha (Ideology), the Supreme Command,
and the Conduct Rules themselves — the same four enshrined in Sixteen Points 10–13. The
"Conduct Rules" are thus both an item *within* the Sixteen Points (point 13; and the "C" of
**CSDK**, point 16) and the summarizing frame *around* the Shiilas and the Points.

## Relationships

- rule 1 requires the [Fifteen Shiilas](/practice/fifteen-shiilas.md); rule 4 requires the [Sixteen Points](/practice/sixteen-points.md)
- appears reciprocally as Sixteen Points 13 (sanctity of the Conduct Rules) and as the "C" of CSDK (Sixteen Points 16)
- rule 3's four sanctities centre on the [Iṣṭa](/practice/ishta.md) (the personal Goal) and the Ādarsha/Ideology
