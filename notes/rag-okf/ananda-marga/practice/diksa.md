# Definition

**Dīkṣā** is spiritual initiation — a term Sarkar insists the English word
"initiation" cannot carry (whose true Saṃskṛta equivalent is *abhiṣeka*, not dīkṣā).
He derives it from the Tantric verse *"Diipajiṇānaṃ yato dadyāt kuryāt pāpakṣayaṃ
tatah; Tasmād diikṣeti sā proktā sarvatantrasya sammatā"* — "the process which
produces the capacity to realise the inner import of mantra and which expedites the
requital of the saṃskāras." The first syllable *dii* comes from *diipanii /
diipajñāna* (the torch-light of inner knowledge); *kṣa* from *(pāpa)kṣayam* (the
waning of sin / reactive momenta) (AV 6, AV 25).

# Explanation

**Two phases.** Per the science of yoga, dīkṣā has two phases (AV 6, AV 25).
**Vaedikii dīkṣā** "is not a cult" but a prayer to Parama Puruṣa to show the path —
the Gāyattrii Mantra (properly the *Savitṛ Ṛk*, "guide our intellect onto the path
of bliss") is its exemplar. Today it survives "only as a prayer for Tantric
initiation," i.e. as a first step (AV 5). **Tāntrikii dīkṣā** "actually is a cult" —
the operative process of liberation.

**The three sub-phases of Tāntrikii dīkṣā.** AV 6 enumerates exactly three:
**diipanii** (the torch-light that reveals the path through "the cimmerian darkness
of Māyā"); **mantrāghāta** (the mantra's acoustic root striking and arousing the
coiled [kuṇḍalinī](/practice/kundalini.md)); and **mantra caetanya** (the roused
force moving toward supreme positivity) — together freeing the aspirant from
bondages on the physical and psychic planes.

**Twice-born (dvija).** In *Twice Born* (AV 30) Sarkar reads *Janmanā jāyate
shūdrah, saṃskārāt dvija ucyate*: by birth all are *shūdra*; upon initiation (the
renewal, *saṃskāra*) one becomes *dvija*, "born twice" — the first birth physical,
the second spiritual. "Unless and until one is initiated one cannot be treated as a
human being."

**Preceptor and the Iṣṭa mantra.** *The Three Stages of Spiritual Progress* (AV 4)
specifies that Tāntrikii dīkṣā occurs "when one is eligible to be taught Iṣṭa mantra
and guru mantra," and that **Iishvara alone is the Guru** who "makes arrangements
for imparting diikṣā." *Tantra and Sādhanā* (SS 8) stresses the indispensable first
step: selection of a competent preceptor and worthy disciple — "the preceptor's
initiation is the sowing of seeds" on the field of the disciple's heart.

# In Caryācarya — the practical grades of initiation

Caryācarya 1/2 sets out the graded initiation system, complementing the doctrinal
Vaedikii/Tāntrikii distinction above. At **age five** a child may receive **Nāma Mantra**
(the preliminary process) from a parent/guardian — sitting in padmāsana, taught *"to feel or
imagine that everything around them … is Brahma."* At **twelve** one takes **Sādhāraṇa
Yoga** from an *ācārya*, and at **sixteen or after**, **Sahaja Yoga**; **Visheṣa Yoga**, the
highest, is given by **purodhās alone** to adept Sahaja-Yoga initiates with ardent desire
and time to practise. **Prārambhika Yoga** is an elementary process (above Nāma Mantra) for
those whom Sādhāraṇa Yoga does not yet suit; Sādhāraṇa and Prārambhika exclude āsanas, hence
carry no dietary restriction.

Initiation (other than Nāma Mantra) is given before the **pratiika** (emblem); **no fee** is
ever charged; Nāma Mantra is given to **no more than four** at a time (preferably one). Each
initiate lacking a Sanskrit name is given one with the suffix **deva**, *"used in all
worldly dealings"* — while giving *"equal respect and full scope to all languages."* These
yoga grades are the practical counterpart of the Tāntrikii dīkṣā process; the physical
[āsanas](/practice/asana-pranayama.md) introduced with the higher grades follow the rules of
Caryācarya Part 3.

## Relationships

- Vaedikii dīkṣā is the prayer-phase (the Gāyattrii / Savitṛ Ṛk); Tāntrikii dīkṣā confers the working [mantra](/practice/mantra.md) and the [Iṣṭa](/practice/ishta.md) mantra used in ideation
- belongs to the [Tantra](/practice/tantra.md) tradition and is the prerequisite gateway to [sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md); its sub-phases *mantrāghāta* and *mantra caetanya* arouse the [kuṇḍalinī](/practice/kundalini.md)
- the [guru](/practice/guru.md) / preceptor is essential — "Brahmaeva Gurureka nāparah," "Iishvara alone is the Guru" (AV 25, AV 4)
- dīkṣā expedites the requital of [saṃskāras](/biopsychology/samskara.md) (*pāpakṣayam*), the waning of accumulated reactive momenta
- *Caryācarya* sets the practical grades — Nāma Mantra → Prārambhika → Sādhāraṇa → Sahaja → Visheṣa Yoga (Visheṣa by purodhās only; no fee); the [āsanas](/practice/asana-pranayama.md) of the higher grades follow Caryācarya Part 3

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** the count of TWO main phases (Vaedikii / Tāntrikii) and THREE
  sub-phases of Tāntrikii dīkṣā (*diipanii*, *mantrāghāta*, *mantra caetanya*) is
  stated explicitly in AV 6 — not inferred. Likewise "dīkṣā ≠ initiation; *abhiṣeka*
  is the true equivalent of 'initiation'."
- The etymological verse is preserved verbatim; AV 6 sources *dii* from "diipanii,"
  AV 25 from "diipajiṇāna" — same teaching, slightly different wording.
- **Could not verify:** no read passage states the mantra must come from a *living
  human* preceptor (as opposed to "Iishvara as Guru"); the guru-necessity is grounded
  in "Iishvara alone is the Guru" and the Tantric preceptor–disciple relationship.
  Do not assert "a living guru is required" as a direct quote.
