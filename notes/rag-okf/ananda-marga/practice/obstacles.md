# Definition

**Obstacles are the helping forces that establish one in the goal.** Ánanda Sútram
3-10: *"Bādhā sā yuṣamānā shaktih sevyaṃ sthāpayati lakṣye."*

# Explanation

On the path of *sādhanā* (spiritual practice), obstacles (*bādhā*) are **no foes
but friends** — they only render service to the aspirant. It is *because of* the
obstacles that the battle against them rages, and **this counter-effort alone
carries the sādhaka to the cherished goal.** Resistance is thus structurally
necessary to progress, not an accident to be lamented.

# In the *Discourses on Neohumanist Education* (1979) — the four complexes

*Rise Above All Complexes* (DNE 32, 2 Oct 1979) names the psychological obstacles
that must never be encouraged (fully treated in the
[Psychological Complexes](/biopsychology/psychological-complexes.md) node):

- **The four complexes.** "You must never encourage any superiority complex,
  inferiority complex, fear complex, defeatist complex, etc." — no inferiority
  ("You are the very children of Parama Puruṣa"), no superiority ("this entire
  universe is the creation of the Supreme Father"), no fear ("When the Supreme
  Entity, the Supreme Father Himself, is your constant companion, then why should
  you fear anyone?"), no defeatism ("The path of your journey towards the Supreme
  Entity is wide open. Hence, there is no question of defeat for you").
- **Equanimity — the fourth requirement.** "Lord Śiva said: Caturtho
  samatābhavo [The fourth requirement for success is equanimity of mind]" (Śiva
  Saṃhitā): "In order to attain success in life, especially in spiritual life,
  the fourth indispensable factor is balance of mind, samatābhavo." Without it "no
  progress in any sphere is possible, not even in the spiritual sphere."
- **Nothing is fixed.** "Nothing is fixed, nothing is stationary. Everything is on
  the move, everything has to move. Cessation of movement means death."

## Relationships

- reframes obstacles on the path of sādhanā toward [Samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md)
- *DNE 32*: the four complexes — superiority, inferiority, fear, defeatist — are psychological obstacles that must never be encouraged; "Caturtho samatābhavo" (equanimity, the fourth Śiva Saṃhitā requirement for success); "Cessation of movement means death" (cf. [Psychological Complexes](/biopsychology/psychological-complexes.md), [Sādhana](/practice/sadhana.md))
- the struggle is aided by the [guru](/practice/guru.md)
- counter-effort serves the aim of [Dharma](/philosophy/dharma.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- The claim is **strong and literal**: obstacles are *helping forces*, not merely
  "tolerable." Keep the positive valuation rather than a neutral "challenges" gloss.
