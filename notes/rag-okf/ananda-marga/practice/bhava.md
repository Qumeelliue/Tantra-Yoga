# Definition

**Bhāva** is, in the Vaeṣṇava framework Sarkar adopts, one of the graded states of
**spiritual ecstasy** experienced during [sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md) — the
state in which the sādhaka feels the divine world around him *together with its
source*. **Mahābhāva** is the supreme such state, in which the sādhaka feels the
**tactual presence** of Parama Puruṣa, "even within His embrace."

# Explanation

- The three-fold graded scheme — **dashā → bhāva → mahābhāva** — is taken
  explicitly from Vaeṣṇava philosophy; the states arise as the propensities are
  withdrawn from crude objects toward [Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md):
  - **dashā** — feeling "the thrill of divine existence around him," falling down;
  - **bhāva** — feeling the divine world *and its source*, great bliss, falling;
  - **mahābhāva** — feeling "the closest proximity of Parama Puruṣa, even within
    his embrace."
- **Mahābhāva is a kind of savikalpa [samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md):** conscious,
  subconscious and unconscious minds fuse into one, the eyes become fixed and red,
  every nerve-cell and pore feels the divine touch.
- The state attained is **conditioned by [saṃskāras](/biopsychology/samskara.md)**
  — hence one in mahābhāva may be restless or calm, may laugh or weep; devotion
  fills the mind "to the brim."
- **Cosmological pair** (of Parama Puruṣa Himself): *liilābhāva* = His **expressed**
  ([Saguṇa](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)) stance carrying on creation
  through saiñcara/pratisaiñcara ("One Puruṣa becomes many" — the
  [Liilā](/cosmology/lila.md)); *nityabhāva* = His **original**, unexpressed
  (Nirguṇa) stance. Both together realize His *madhura bhāva* (sweet stance).
- **Brahmabhāva** is a practical technique: *superimpose Brahmabhāva on objects and
  thoughts* — they "cease to be physical or mental, they become spiritual" — the
  means to escape *karma bandhana* (cf. the Buddhist Bhusuku's constant ideation,
  yielding *dharmameghānanda*).

## Relationships

- mahābhāva is a form of savikalpa [Samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md); the states intensify [Bhakti](/practice/bhakti.md)
- arise in [Sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md), conditioned by [Saṃskāra](/biopsychology/samskara.md)
- directed toward [Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md) / the [Iṣṭa](/practice/ishta.md); aroused via the [cakras](/biopsychology/cakra.md)
- *liilābhāva* expresses [Saguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md) and the [Liilā](/cosmology/lila.md) of creation

# Notes — fidelity

- **Samatābhāva** (the 1979 discourse) carries a **different sense** — *mental
  equipoise* (Shiva's fourth principle of success) — NOT a graded devotional state.
  Kept separate from the dashā/bhāva/mahābhāva ladder.
- Sarkar attributes the dashā/bhāva/mahābhāva scheme specifically to **Vaeṣṇava
  philosophy** (his stated framing).
- *Liilābhāva* here is a property **of Parama Puruṣa** (His creative stance), not a
  sādhaka-ecstasy — do not conflate with the experiential ladder.
- **Could not verify in the cited passages:** the classical named moods *dāsya,
  sakhya, vātsalya, mādhurya* (not used in the discourses read).
