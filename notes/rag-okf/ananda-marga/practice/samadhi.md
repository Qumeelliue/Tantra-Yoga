# Definition

**Samādhi** is the absorptive merger of the [unit mind](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md)
back toward its source:

- **Savikalpa samādhi** (*saguṇāsthiti*): citta → aham → mahat, the
  mahattattva becoming the Macrocosmic "I". Ánanda Sútram 1-21.
- **Nirvikalpa samādhi** (*nirguṇāsthiti*): the mahat itself merges into the
  Ātman / [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md) (Citishakti) — an objectless, mindless
  state beyond mind. Ánanda Sútram 1-22, 1-23.

# Explanation

The merger of an effect into its cause is *pralaya* / *praṇāśa*. Nirvikalpa is
verbally inexpressible and beyond the three mental stages (conscious /
subconscious / unconscious); its **proof** (1-24) is the lingering bliss that
trails on after the mind returns (driven by unserved *saṃskāras*). This is the
culmination of the introversial [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md) and
the goal of sādhanā.

# In the *Discourses on Neohumanist Education* (1971) — samādhi as the mind's failure to symbolize

*Expression and Symbolization* (DNE 37, 22 May 1971) frames samādhi through the
limits of symbolization:

- **Samādhi when symbolization of Parama Puruṣa fails.** "When because of the
  depth of the inner feelings, your mental waves seek symbolization but do not
  succeed, the mind itself ceases working. Such a state is called Samādhi. To
  explain Parama Puruṣa at such a time is even more impossible."
- **"Yato vaco nivartante ..."** "It is not possible for mental faculties to
  symbolize Him. They fail to describe Him. That is why it has been said: Yato
  vaco nivartante aprapyamansa saha, Anandam Brahmano bidvana ma vibheti
  kutascana — 'When you want to say something about Parama Puruṣa ... words
  cannot be uttered because the vocal organ itself fails. The mind fails in
  symbolizing Him.'" (Cf. [Symbolization](/philosophy/symbolization.md),
  [Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md).)

## Relationships

- merger of the [Unit Mind](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md) strata
- *DNE 37*: samādhi arises when the mental waves seek to symbolize [Parama Puruṣa](/cosmology/brahma.md) and fail — "the mind itself ceases working"; *"Yato vaco nivartante ..."* (cf. [Symbolization](/philosophy/symbolization.md))
- savikalpa → Macrocosmic Mahat; nirvikalpa → [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md) (Ātman)
