# PROUT (Progressive Utilization Theory)

Sarkar's socio-economic theory — the social cycle (varṇa), sadvipra leadership,
economic democracy, and the maximum-utilisation principle. Entries use
`type: SocialTheory`.

# Entries

* [PROUT (Progressive Utilization Theory)](prout.md) - The overarching socio-economic theory; seed text = ch. 5 of Ánanda Sútram.
* [Five Fundamental Principles of PROUT](five-fundamental-principles.md) - The five principles of utilization and distribution (5-12…5-16).
* [Samāja Cakra (Social Cycle)](samaja-cakra.md) - The rotation of the four classes — Shūdra, Kṣatriya, Vipra, Vaeshya — punctuated by revolution.
* [Sadvipra](sadvipra.md) - Moralist-spiritualists in the nucleus who control the cycle and subdue exploiters.
* [Social Change — Evolution, Revolution & their Reversals](social-change.md) - The five modes: krānti, viplava, vikrānti, prativiplava, parikrānti.
* [Diversity Is the Law of Nature](diversity.md) - Diversity, not identity, is Prakṛti's innate tendency; forced equality fails.
* [Minimum Requirements & Surplus Distribution](minimum-requirements.md) - Minimum requirements to all; surplus to the meritorious; the minimum standard ceaselessly raised.
* [Economic Democracy](economic-democracy.md) - Economic power vested in the common people; minimum requirements guaranteed to all — "not political democracy."
* [Decentralized Economy](decentralized-economy.md) - Production for consumption, not profit; local control of resources, cooperatives, full local employment.
* [Socio-Economic Groupifications](socio-economic-groupifications.md) - Self-sufficient socio-economic units merging progressively toward one integrated world unit.
* [Cooperatives](cooperatives.md) - Coordinated cooperation in farmers, producers and consumers cooperatives — "We want cooperatives, not communes."
* [Exploitation — the Forms of Exploitation](exploitation.md) - Exploitation in all strata — economic, social, psychic, spiritual — with dogma as its hidden instrument.
* [Block-Level Planning](block-level-planning.md) - The basic unit of decentralized planning: ascending order from the block, with short-term (≤6 mo) and long-term (≤3 yr) plans.
* [Master Units (Cakranemi)](master-units.md) - Ananda Marga's self-sufficient miniature communities meeting the five minimum requirements.
* [Sadvipra Boards (Benevolent Dictatorship)](sadvipra-boards.md) - The future government: elected sadvipra boards (Supreme, Legislative, Executive, Judicial, sub-boards).
* [Ideal Constitution](ideal-constitution.md) - Requirements and reforms for a just constitution; the Charter of Rights for a world constitution.
* [Cardinal Socio-Political Principles](cardinal-socio-political-principles.md) - No retrenchment without alternative employment, no forcible conversion, no mother-tongue suppression.
* [Three Types of Occupation (Jīvikā)](three-types-of-occupation.md) - Physical, mental and spiritual occupations; spiritual = engagement in the field of Dharma.
* [Four Vargas & Devotion](four-vargas.md) - The four aims of life (kāma, artha, dharma, mokṣa) prepared for by itihāsa, attainable together through devotion.
* [Artha & Paramārtha](artha-paramartha.md) - Temporary relief from the triple afflictions (artha/money/meaning) vs permanent cessation (paramārtha).
* [Social Justice](social-justice.md) - Co-ownership of the world's resources as a birthright; recognizing (not granting) rights; "the human family includes everyone."
* [Sama Samaaja Tattva (Social Equality)](sama-samaaja-tattva.md) - The sincere conviction that all have the right to food, clothes, accommodation, education and medical care — with utmost effort to realize it.
