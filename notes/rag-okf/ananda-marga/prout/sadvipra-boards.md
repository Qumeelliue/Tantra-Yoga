# Definition

**Sadvipra boards** are the future form of government in PROUT — the **rule of
sadvipras** ("the ideal form of leadership"), in which elected boards of sadvipras
constitute a **benevolent dictatorship**. PROUT supports **restricted democracy**
today as a transitional system, "because in the future a better system of government
will be evolved." (1969, PiaN 18/2.)

# Explanation

- **Why not democracy.** "Democracy can not solve human problems because in a
  democracy one particular class gets the scope to dominate the society while the
  other classes are deprived of their freedom." Sadvipras alone "can represent the
  genuine aspirations of the people"; they fight ceaselessly against corruption,
  exploitation, immorality and fissiparous tendencies.
- **Who is a sadvipra here.** Those who follow the principles of [Yama & Niyama](/practice/yama-niyama.md)
  — "the principles of spiritual morality" — and are devoted to the Supreme
  Consciousness; recognised "by their exemplary conduct, selfless service, dutifulness
  and moral integrity."
- **The board structure** — political power centralized (but "not centralization in one
  person or one institution"), economic power decentralized:
  1. **Supreme Board of Sadvipras** — the elected supreme body for framing policy and
     supervising the other boards;
  2. **Legislative Board of Sadvipras** — frames laws per PROUT and the Supreme
     Board's policies;
  3. **Executive Board of Sadvipras** — implements laws, supervises the bureaucracy
     and the sub-boards;
  4. **Judicial Board of Sadvipras** — frames rules/procedures for judges and
     judiciary personnel;
  5. **Sub-boards of Sadvipras** — experts in the respective branches of
     administration; their lists pass from the Executive Board through the
     Legislative Board to the Supreme Board, which is the **final authority**.
- **Establishment requires method, not force.** Sadvipra rule "cannot be established
  by blind physical force or idle intellectual extravaganza" — it requires "the
  systematic and rational application of PROUT by the collective effort of many highly
  intelligent people."

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** "the benevolent dictatorship of Sadvipras is an absolute
  necessity"; democracy is "not an ideal system," PROUT supports only "restricted
  democracy" as transitional. Keep this sharp — do not soften "benevolent
  dictatorship."
- The **five boards** with their exact functions are stated in full — preserve the
  structure; the Supreme Board is explicitly the final authority.
- **Terminology:** "benevolent dictatorship" and "rule of Sadvipras" are Sarkar's
  own terms for this system.
