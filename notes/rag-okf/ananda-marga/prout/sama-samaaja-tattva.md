# Definition

**Sama Samaaja Tattva** — the theory of social equality — is the sincere conviction
and spirit that "all humans have the right to food, clothes, accommodation, education
and medical treatment," joined with the utmost effort to see those rights attained:

> "Whatever others might say, I sincerely believe that all humans have the right to
> food, clothes, accommodation, education and medical treatment. It is not enough for
> me to accept their rights in principle; as an honest person I should make the utmost
> effort to see that they have attained their rights. This is the spirit of
> sama-samája tattva."

# Explanation

**The base for training the mind.** "To train the mind there must be a proper base.
Suppose some boys want to practise running; they need a place for that. Similarly, to
train the mind, a base is required. In this case, the base is sama-samája tattva."

**From conviction to running.** The spirit alone is not enough — "Supposing some boys
want to run. If they simply stand still on the hard earth, will that be considered
running? They will have to actually run." This running, the dynamic practice on the
sama-samája tattva base, is [proto-psycho-spirituality](/practice/proto-psycho-spirituality.md).

**Against socio-sentiment.** The conviction is to be spread so that people can
recognize the "human chameleons" and their "metamorphosed sentimental strategy":
"through seminars should make people aware of the importance of sama-samája tattva
and proto-psycho-spirituality to fight against socio-sentiment (because socio-sentiment
is many times more harmful than geo-sentiment)."

## Relationships

- the five rights of sama-samája tattva are exactly the [minimum requirements](/prout/minimum-requirements.md) of society
- it is the social-ethical base for the [five fundamental principles](/prout/five-fundamental-principles.md) of PROUT
- the "running" built on this base is [proto-psycho-spirituality](/practice/proto-psycho-spirituality.md)
- its universal concern for all humans is [neohumanism](/philosophy/neohumanism.md) in social practice
- it is opposed to the [exploitation](/prout/exploitation.md) of the [samaja-cakra](/prout/samaja-cakra.md) and to the sentiment-manipulation of [pseudo-humanism](/philosophy/pseudo-humanism.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- **Base, not goal:** the source presents sama-samája tattva specifically as the
  *base* for training the mind (running-ground analogy), with proto-psycho-spirituality
  as the actual movement — this two-step framing is preserved.
- **"Through seminars" kept:** the diffusion mechanism named in the passage is
  seminars; reproduced as stated.
- **Five rights exact:** food, clothes, accommodation, education and medical treatment
  — kept in Sarkar's order.
- **Could not verify:** whether the *five rights* here are to be identified with a
  fixed enumeration elsewhere in PROUT literature; the passage itself does not
  enumerate further.
