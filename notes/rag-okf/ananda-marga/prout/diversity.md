# Definition

**Diversity, not identity, is the law of nature.** Ánanda Sútram 5-8:
*"Vaecitryaṃ prākrtadharmah samānaṃ na bhaviṣyati."*

# Explanation

Diversity is the **innate characteristic of the Supreme Operative Principle** —
[Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md). No two objects in the universe are identical: no
two bodies, two minds, two molecules or two atoms. This variety is Prakṛti's
inherent tendency.

Therefore those who want to **make everything equal are sure to fail**, for they
work against Prakṛti's nature. All things are equal **only in the unmanifest state**
of the Supreme Operative Principle (see
[Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)); to insist on
making all manifest things equal is, in effect, to "think of the destruction of
everything."

This is the cosmological ground of PROUT's economics: distribution must honour
**merit and difference**, not impose a flat identity (hence surplus to the
meritorious in [minimum requirements](/prout/minimum-requirements.md)).

## Relationships

- diversity = the innate tendency of [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) (Supreme Operative Principle)
- equality holds only in the unmanifest state — [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)
- grounds merit-based distribution in [Minimum Requirements](/prout/minimum-requirements.md) and [PROUT](/prout/prout.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- This is an argument **against forced/mechanical equality**, not against social
  justice — PROUT still guarantees minimum requirements to all. Keep both halves;
  do not present it as a defence of inequality per se.
