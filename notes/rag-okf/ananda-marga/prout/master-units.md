# Definition

**Master Units** are Ananda Marga's planned self-sufficient communities — "the
nerve centres of the society" and **miniature forms of Ananda Marga** (and of
Ánanda Nagar), started "to develop the fate of the backward and downtrodden
classes." They expand all possible services — education, culture, economics,
spiritual upliftment — first for human beings, then for all living beings,
"irrespective of caste, creed, colour, religion and national barriers." The
Saṃskṛta name is **Cakranemii** — "the nucleus of the cakra (wheel)." (1989, PiaN
19/6.)

# Explanation

- **Self-sufficiency is a must.** "I want all Master Units to be economically
  self-sufficient in all respects, because spiritualists should not depend on the
  wealthy class for money." Master Units are to be started in each district and block
  of the world, a minimum of **five acres** each; they expand and "gradually terminate
  in the maxiature form and cover the whole universe."
- **The five primary requisites** correspond to the five
  [minimum requirements](/prout/minimum-requirements.md):
  1. **Food** — local raw materials produced by agriculture and scientific farming,
     feeding agro-industries (dairy, horticulture, sericulture);
  2. **Clothing** — production of fibres and fabrics (ladies' fingers, pineapple,
     sugar beet, banana, basil, cotton, sisal);
  3. **Education** — primary and post-primary schools now (higher education later);
  4. **Medical** — general and special medical units, emphasising alternative
     treatments;
  5. **Housing** — schemes to construct houses for extremely poor people.
- **Common points** for all Master Units: schools, hostels (junior/senior/higher),
  children's homes, medical units, cottage industries, dairy farms, plantations.
- **Special features:** flour mill, bakery, seed bank, a *Sulav Biija Vitaran Kendra*
  (cheap seed distribution centre), a free plant distribution centre (with a precise
  seedling-rooting method for large sweet fruit), sericulture and silk weaving,
  bio-gas plants, butter production, apiculture, an Ideal Farm Training Centre, and a
  sanctuary. **Only bio-fertilizers** (compost, cow dung, neem paste/spray) — "chemical
  fertilizers must be avoided."
- **Active representation from all departments of Ananda Marga** is required — those
  who fail in representation "will be lost in non-existence."
- The programme is "a combination of oriental sublimity and western dynamicity."

## Relationships

- the institutional embodiment of [PROUT](/prout/prout.md) and all-round [Sevā](/practice/seva.md) — "PROUT, all-round service and Master Units are the ways of life"
- meet the [Minimum Requirements](/prout/minimum-requirements.md) in miniature — the five requisites
- exemplify [Decentralized Economy](/prout/decentralized-economy.md) (local production for local needs)
- rest on spiritual philosophy as their hub — cf. [Sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md), [Dharma](/philosophy/dharma.md)
- related to the [Socio-Economic Groupifications](/prout/socio-economic-groupifications.md) ideal of self-sufficient units

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** Master Units must be "economically self-sufficient in all
  respects" and are the "biggest structure of Ananda Marga"; chemical fertilizers are
  forbidden, only bio-fertilizers.
- The chapter is a **programme of the Ananda Marga institution**, not a PROUT theory
  node per se — kept in prout/ because it embodies the PROUT minimum-requirements
  scheme in practice.
- **Could not verify in the cited passage:** any quantitative production targets or a
  timeline for the "few months or a few years" elevation claim — that phrase is
  Sarkar's framing.
