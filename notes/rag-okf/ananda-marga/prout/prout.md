# Definition

**PROUT** — the **Progressive Utilization Theory** — is Sarkar's socio-economic
theory. Its self-statement closes Ánanda Sútram 5-16:
*"Pragatishiila upayogatattvamidaṃ sarvajanahitārthaṃ sarvajanasukhārthaṃ
pracāritam"* — "This is the Progressive Utilization Theory, propounded for the
happiness and all-round welfare of all."

# Explanation

Chapter 5 of [Ánanda Sútram](/works/ananda-sutram.md) (sixteen sūtras, 1961) is
the **seed text** of PROUT. By the editors' note, aphorisms **5-12 to 5-16
correspond to the [Five Fundamental Principles](/prout/five-fundamental-principles.md)**
that Sarkar first gave in English in 1959. The chapter joins together:

- the rotation of the [Samāja Cakra](/prout/samaja-cakra.md) (social cycle) through
  the four classes, and the [Sadvipra](/prout/sadvipra.md) who control it from the
  nucleus;
- the modes of [social change](/prout/social-change.md) (evolution, revolution and
  their reversals);
- the law that [diversity](/prout/diversity.md), not identity, is the rule of
  nature;
- the economic guarantee of [minimum requirements](/prout/minimum-requirements.md)
  with surplus to the meritorious;
- the [Five Fundamental Principles](/prout/five-fundamental-principles.md) of
  utilization and distribution.

Its kernel: **maximum utilization and rational distribution** of all crude, subtle
and causal potentialities, varying with **time, space and person** (see
[Relative Truth & the Three Relative Factors](/cosmology/relativity.md)), always of
*progressive* nature.

# In *Neohumanism in a Nutshell* (1978) — the physical-sphere necessity of PROUT

*The Physical, Psychic and Spiritual Strata* (nh1-2, 12 December 1978) grounds
PROUT's necessity in the physical-sphere lacuna:

- **The sole purpose.** "With this end in view, with the sole purpose of removing physical
  misery and affliction, and keeping in view the higher goals of human life, the Prout(1)
  philosophy has been formulated."
- **The noumenal cause.** "this lacuna in the physical sphere, and the consequent disparity,
  is the noumenal cause of all phenomenal troubles. Due to this noumenal cause, peace in the
  psychic sphere is being disturbed again and again."
- **The alternative.** "There was no alternative but to propagate the Prout philosophy. Had
  it not been done, maybe human sorrows and sufferings would have been perpetrated for
  centuries together."

# Notes — fidelity

- PROUT is presented as the **social application of the same spiritual philosophy**,
  not a stand-alone economics. Keep the linkage to dharma and cosmology; do not
  reduce it to a secular ideology.
- **NHN 1/2 (1978)** gives the explicit statement of PROUT's purpose ("the sole purpose
  of removing physical misery and affliction") and its noumenal-cause argument — a dated
  late-1970s corroboration of the 1959/1961 formulation.
