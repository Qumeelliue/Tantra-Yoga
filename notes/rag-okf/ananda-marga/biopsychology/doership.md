# Definition

**Doership** distinguishes the entity that *acts* (*kartā*) from the entity that
merely *witnesses* (*sākṣī*). Ánanda Sútram 2-15:
*"Puruṣah akartā phalasākṣiibhūtah bhāvakendrasthitah guṇayantrākashca"* —
"Puruṣa does no action directly, but is the witnessing entity of actions and
reactions; located at the nucleus of Saguṇa Brahma, He is the controller of the
guṇas."

# Explanation

The strata of agency form a precise hierarchy:

- **[Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md) — non-doer (akartā), witness (phala-sākṣī).**
  When energy is begotten in an object-body through the flow of the Operative
  Principle, its controller is the *kartā*; but Puruṣa controls not that energy,
  rather the **[guṇas](/cosmology/guna.md)** at whose nucleus He is established.
  He is therefore **not subject to** the guṇas but their sovereign (cf.
  [Saguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)).
- **[Buddhi](/biopsychology/buddhi.md) / [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md) —
  not the doer**, but remains *associated with objects* (2-16,
  *"Akartrii viṣayasaṃyuktā buddhih mahadvā"*).
- **[Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) — the doer (kartā)** and the direct
  enjoyer/endurer of the fruits of action (2-17).
- **[Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md)** then takes the form of those results (2-18)
  — the substrate on which [karma](/philosophy/karma.md) is recorded.

## Relationships

- witness / non-doer = [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md), controller of [Guṇa](/cosmology/guna.md)
- non-doer-but-associated = [Buddhi](/biopsychology/buddhi.md) / [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md)
- doer & enjoyer = [Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md)
- grounds the mechanics of [Karma & Requital](/philosophy/karma.md)
