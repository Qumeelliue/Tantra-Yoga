# Definition

A **tanmātra** is a sensible or super-sensible **inference / generic essence** — a
microscopic fraction that *represents* the [bhūtas](/cosmology/five-fundamental-factors.md),
is *carried by* them, and is *created by* the vibrations of clash within them.
Ánanda Sútram 2-22:
*"Bhūtalakṣaṇātmakaṃ bhūtabāhitaṃ bhūtasaungharṣaspandanaṃ tanmātram."*

# Explanation

Stirrings in object-bodies (from internal and external pressures) travel as
**waves** through subtler media to the gates of the
[indriyas](/biopsychology/indriya.md), thence through nerves and their secretions
to appropriative points of the brain. There the simulative
[citta](/biopsychology/citta.md) adopts the vibrative form of the external body —
bringing citta into contact with **sound, touch, form, taste or smell**. These
five appropriative waves are the tanmātras.

A bhūta is **recognized by the tanmātras it carries** (2-23,
*"Bhūtaṃ tanmātreṇa pariciiyate"*), in a cumulative ladder:

| Fundamental factor | Tanmātras carried |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| Ethereal (ākāśa) | sound |
| Aerial (marut) | sound, touch |
| Luminous (tejas) | sound, touch, form |
| Liquid (ap) | sound, touch, form, taste |
| Solid (kṣiti) | sound, touch, form, taste, smell |

An object's category is judged by the **crudest** tanmātra it carries.

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — waves, pauses, and the perceptual process

The 1959 cosmology (*Bhūtatattva, Tanmātratattva and Indriyatattva*, II-3) grounds
the sūtra in a full wave-theory of perception:

- **Tanmātra = a microscopic fraction of a wave.** *Tat + mātra* = "that" +
  "minutest quantity": a tanmātra is a microscopic fraction of the bhūta carried by
  waves. Every bhūta "is in an eternal flow"; its very existence "is just a pattern
  of waves." The heterogeneity of tanmātras (different wavelengths) gives the
  varieties of the perceptible world.
- **Sentient and static pauses.** Waves pass through objects via "adjustment of
  wavelengths at the two pauses of the waves" — the **sentient pause** (crest: where
  upward momentum ends) and the **static pause** (trough: where downward movement
  ends) — the crest and trough of physical science. Subtler waves pass through
  cruder ones.
- **Out-going and in-coming tanmātras.** The wave radiated by an object is the
  "out-going tanmātra"; the wave received by the sensory nerves is the "in-coming
  tanmātra." Reflection/refraction determines perception (cf.
  [Kośa](/biopsychology/pancakosa.md)): when out-going tanmātras are reflected the
  object comes within sensation/perception/conception; when refracted, partially;
  when neither, not at all.
- **The perceptual process.** The object vibrates → waves strike the gateways of
  the organs → a sympathetic vibration in the sensory nerve is conveyed to the
  brain-seat (*e.g.* the *cakṣu indriya*) → citta takes the form of the vibration
  and the ego feels "I am seeing." A defect in any sensory nerve deprives the ego
  of that perception — "objective perception is always attributed with the merits
  and demerits of the sensory nerves."
- **The immanent capacity of an object to discharge tanmātras is constant** — the
  solid transmitting all five perceptions with equal intensity does not match the
  ethereal's intensity of sound alone; the ethereal, carrying only sound, has the
  *collective* intensity of all five.
