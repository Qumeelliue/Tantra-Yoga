# Definition

**Mahattattva** is the pure "I"-feeling — it evolves out of
[ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) as, under *vidyā shakti*, rajoguṇa
sheds and **sattvaguṇa** predominates. Ánanda Sútram 1-15.

# Explanation

It is the **subtlest stratum** of the [unit mind](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md).
Its surplus over the aham is [bodhi](/biopsychology/bodhi.md) (intuition). In
the introversial merger ([samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md)) the sequence is
citta → aham → mahat.

## Relationships

- evolves-from [Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) (sattvaguṇa of [Guṇa](/cosmology/guna.md))
- subtlest stratum of the [Unit Mind](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md)
- surplus over aham = [Bodhi](/biopsychology/bodhi.md); merges in [Samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md)
