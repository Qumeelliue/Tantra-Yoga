# Definition

**"The human body is a biological machine."** Bio-psychology is the science of
the mind-body relationship: glands and plexi secrete hormones that change
thoughts, instincts and propensities, while the lymphatic glands supply **lymph
("the initial hormone")** as the raw material to those glands; the most refined
product is **shukra**. (DNE 29.)

# Explanation

**Glands, plexi and hormones.** Over-secretion and under-secretion of hormones
"affect nerve cells, and thus change thoughts. Instincts also change." Plexi
are "the bio-psychological secrets of living"; in inanimate objects there is no
secretion of hormones from plexi. The glands are "factories"; the lymphatic
glands supply their raw material, and "the surplus lymph goes to the brain and
provides food for the nerve cells in the cranium." The dependence of the other
glands on the lymphatic glands is greater than the reverse. "Lymph itself is a
hormone, and is converted into other hormones by different glands. Lymph is the
initial hormone."

**Shukra — the cream of all creams.** "The final stuff is shukra [which has
three stages: lymph, spermatozoa and seminal fluid]. It is the most developed
stuff – the cream of all creams." Chlorophyll accelerates the production of
lymph but is not the initial stuff; the initial stuff is the energy and
vitality acquired from the quinquelemental factors (water, air, light, etc.).
"Men should have proper control over the conversion of lymph into semen. This
is part of Brahmacarya sadhana [meditation on the Supreme Entity]."

**The pituitary and pineal.** "The pituitary gland is psycho-spiritual in
nature, and the pineal gland is spiritual in nature." The right wing of the
pituitary plexus controls "the qualities, attributions and quanta of the
leftistic propensities, which are a little more than 400 in number"; the left
wing controls the rightistic propensities, "which are also a little more than
400 in number. The total is a little less than 850. When both sides of the
pituitary plexus are fully developed and fully utilized, one attains apexed
intellect." The pituitary plexus is very important in spiritual progress.

**Four dimensions of Yoga psychology.** "There are four dimensions of Yoga
psychology – from the muladhara cakra to the manipura cakra; from the manipura
cakra to the vishuddha cakra; from the vishuddha cakra to the ajina cakra; and
above the ajina cakra. These dimensions of psychology correspond to the four
phases of meditation." Most glands function within the first and second
dimensions; the pituitary concerns the third, the pineal the third and fourth.

**Microvita and grace.** "Parama Purusa applies microvita to different glands
and sub-glands." Negative microvita do not function in the pituitary and pineal
glands; positive microvita function through all plexi. "As a general rule the
grace of Parama Purusa is expressed through positive microvita in different
plexi." Hence "Guru krpahi kevalam [The grace of the Guru is everything]."

**Meditation — a still subtler science.** (DNE 35.) "Regarding thinking with
concentration, that is, meditation – it is another, still subtler, science.
Meditation should be performed knowing fully well the location of different
glands and sub-glands and also the different cells in the human brain," and one
must be acquainted with "their respective systems of hormone secretion,
otherwise their meditation will not produce any fruit. So for this system of
meditation, knowledge of biology is essential." Memorizing, too, is best
achieved by associating the object with psychic pabula of a similar nature and
avoiding "the use of different sub-glands producing inimical hormones."

**Warning on occult powers.** "You should only desire to be one with the
Supreme; there must not be any longing to attain these occult powers – it is
bad." Occult phenomena (subtler telepathy, clairvoyance, omniscience, even the
transmission of spirit amongst contemporary corporal structures) exist in pure
spiritual strata, but aspiring to them is discouraged.

# Notes — fidelity

- All core claims (gland→thought, lymph as initial hormone, shukra as cream of
  all creams, ~850 propensities, apexed intellect, four dimensions =
  four phases of meditation) are direct teaching preserved verbatim.
- The "biological machine" line and the four-dimensions bracketed passage are
  quoted exactly; the latter is the editors' gloss of the four dimensions,
  reproduced as recorded.
- **Guru krpahi kevalam** is quoted as "The grace of the Guru is everything."
- The warning against longing for occult powers (DNE 35) is direct and kept in
  full.
- Could not verify in the cited passages: a full list of the ~850 named
  propensities per plexus, or the specific "eighth stage" of lymph→semen
  control; only the totals and the category (leftistic/rightistic) counts are
  given here.
