# Biopsychology

The layers and dynamics of mind — kośas, cakras, vṛttis (propensities),
kuṇḍalinii, and saṃskāra. Entries use `type: Concept`.

# Entries

* [Unit Mind (Microcosm)](unit-mind.md) - The individual mind: citta + ahaṃtattva + mahattattva, evolving across life forms.
* [Citta](citta.md) - Objective "done-I" mind, from ectoplasmic particles (mind born of matter).
* [Ahaṃtattva](ahamtattva.md) - Doer "I" (rajoguṇa-dominant), evolved from citta.
* [Mahattattva](mahattattva.md) - Pure "I"-feeling (sattvaguṇa-dominant), subtlest stratum.
* [Buddhi](buddhi.md) - Intellect: surplus of aham over citta.
* [Bodhi](bodhi.md) - Intuition: surplus of mahat over aham.
* [Prāṇa](prana.md) - Vital energy: resultant force forming a nucleus in the body.
* [Sukha](sukha.md) - Happiness as the contact of sympathetic waves; attachment to it is the primary vṛtti.
* [Tanmātra](tanmatra.md) - The five generic essences (sound→smell) carried by the bhūtas; the matter of perception.
* [Indriya](indriya.md) - An organ as a four-part composite: gateway, nerve, nerve-fluid, brain-seat.
* [Doership (Kartā & Witness)](doership.md) - Who acts vs. who witnesses: Puruṣa (witness) → aham (doer) → citta (record).
* [Pañcakośa (Five Sheaths)](pancakosa.md) - The five koṣas (kāmamaya → hiraṇyamaya) and the three bodies; micro-mirror of the saptaloka.
* [Saṃskāra & Rebirth](samskara.md) - Reaction in potentiality (deferred vipāka); unserved saṃskāras drive rebirth into a parallel womb.
* [Death & the States of Mind](death.md) - Waking/dream/sleep/death across the three minds; the bodiless mind has no doership or pleasure-pain.
* [Ghosts & Hallucination](hallucination.md) - Ghosts do not exist; the cittāṇu in concentrated thought projects positive/negative hallucination.
* [Cakra (Psycho-spiritual Plexus)](cakra.md) - The plexuses controlling the fifty propensities (vṛttis) and the glands, from mūlādhāra to sahasrāra.
* [The Two Human Approaches](two-human-approaches.md) - Extro-internal vs intro-external movement; ectoplasmic hallucination; Parama Puruṣa's two imperfections and the devotional approach.
* [Micropsychic Longing (Ābhoga)](micropsychic-longing.md) - "The urge that pulls forward"; the four dimensions of micropsychic longing; psychic pabulum and apexed psychology.
* [Bio-Psychology](bio-psychology.md) - The gland/plexus–mind relation; lymph → shukra; the ~850 propensities; "the human body is a biological machine."
* [Memory — Cerebral & Extra-Cerebral (Smrti)](memory.md) - Memory as the re-creation of the perceived; the citta as its abode; dhruvāsmrti; extra-cerebral memory and jātismara.
* [Rise Above All Complexes](psychological-complexes.md) - The four complexes (superiority/inferiority/fear/defeatist); equanimity (samatābhāva); every person is a child of Parama Puruṣa.
* [Stages of Language Expression (Parā–Śrutigocarā)](stages-of-language-expression.md) - Language in six stages from the seed of the idea (Mūlādhāra) to articulated speech; only the last two differ across languages.
