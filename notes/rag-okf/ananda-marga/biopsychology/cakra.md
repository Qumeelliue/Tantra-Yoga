# Definition

The **cakras** (psycho-spiritual plexuses) are the controlling stations that
govern the mind's **fifty fundamental propensities** (*vṛttis*) and are associated
with the body's glands. Spiritual practice withdraws the lower propensities and
guides the flow **upward** toward the highest controlling point, which when
pinnacled **touches Parama Puruṣa**.

# Explanation

- The mind has **fifty fundamental propensities**: forty-nine bind it to matter and
  one (*parā*, spiritual knowledge) connects it to spirit; practice discourages the
  forty-nine and encourages the one.
- The propensities are distributed across the cakras, each controlling a fixed set:
  **mūlādhāra** (4: dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa), **svādhiṣṭhāna** (6), **maṇipura**
  (10), **anāhata** (12), **vishuddha** (16), **ājñā** (2: *aparā* mundane and
  *parā* spiritual knowledge).
- The fifty work internally and externally (×2) and move in ten directions (×10),
  giving the **one thousand** vṛttis controlled by the *multi-propensive plexus* —
  the **sahasrāra** (thousand-petalled lotus, identified with the pineal gland).
  Its pinnacled controlling point is *Parama Pada* / the state of **Nivṛtti**,
  touching [Parama Puruṣa](/cosmology/brahma.md).
- **Glandular mechanism:** psychic waves first vibrate the pituitary plexus, a
  hormone is secreted, lower glands are influenced, and bodily change follows — so
  the aspirant must "rectify the defects of the physical glands." (E.g. the
  vishuddha cakra at the throat controls the vocal cord.)

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — vrtti, the thousand, and the seed of saṃskāra

The 1959 cosmology (*Mind, Prāṇendriya and Vṛtti*, II-4) supplies the systematic
vrtti-cakra account:

- **Vṛtti = "the way of expression of mind."** Mind, "a state in the process of
  [Brahma Cakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md)," expresses through "inter- and
  intra-ectoplasmic occupations" (love, hatred, fear) called *vrttis*. The *seed*
  of every vrtti lies in the brain; the *first expression* occurs in the
  subsidiary glands — "sub-stations of the organs" whose main controlling station
  is in the brain. The gland-seated vrttis that create external waves are
  *instincts*.
- **Fifty vrttis / one thousand.** "Generally there are one thousand vrttis in the
  human structure; in their development and expression on the ordinary crude level
  they are fifty in number." The seeds of the thousand lie in the pineal gland —
  hence **sahasrāra** ("thousand"). Subsidiary glands control 48 vrttis; the
  **pituitary** controls two — *saṃkalpātmaka* (leading to *parāvidyā*, knowledge
  of the Great) and *vikalpātmaka* (*aparāvidyā*, mundane) — completing the
  50×2×10 = 1000 (fifty, internal and external, ten directions).
- **The seed of saṃskāra is within the vrttis.** "Within the scope of these
  vrttis lies the seed of saṃskāra — good or bad"; yogis controlling the sahasrāra
  attain **nirvikalpa samādhi**, a state "beyond the approach of all the vrttis,"
  the exhaustion of all saṃskāra and previous momentum — **mokṣa** "union with the
  Transcendentality."

# In *Neohumanism in a Nutshell* (1979) — the six stages of language expression on the cakras

*Human Society Is One and Indivisible – 3* (nh2-6, 17 June 1979) maps the first
three stages of language expression onto the lower cakras (cross-ref the new
[Stages of Language Expression](/biopsychology/stages-of-language-expression.md) node):
"Parā Shakti, the seed of all expression, lies in the Muladhara Cakra"; Pashyantī
"lies in the next higher Cakra, that is the Svādhiṣṭhāna"; in Madhyamā, "In the
Maṇipura Cakra there is an urge to transform the idea into sound form."

## Relationships

- the controlling stations of the [Unit Mind's](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md) propensities (*cittavṛtti* of [Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md))
- aroused along the path by the [Kuṇḍalinī](/practice/kundalini.md)
- the thousand vrttis are expressed through the [indriyas](/biopsychology/indriya.md)
- equilibrium across the propensities is [Pramā](/philosophy/prama.md); the pinnacle touches [Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md) in [Samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md)
- vrtti-seed of [Saṃskāra](/biopsychology/samskara.md): controlling the sahasrāra ends all vrttis and all saṃskāras = [Mokṣa](/practice/moksa.md)
- *nh2-6 (1979)*: Parā at Mūlādhāra, Pashyantī at Svādhiṣṭhāna, Madhyamā at Maṇipura — the six stages of language expression mapped onto the cakras (cf. [Stages of Language Expression](/biopsychology/stages-of-language-expression.md), [Vishva-Bhāṣā](/philosophy/vishva-bhasa.md))

# Notes — fidelity

- **Idea and Ideology** gives the *saṃkalpātmaka / vikalpātmaka* pair (with
  parā/aparāvidyā) and the **50×2×10 = 1000** arithmetic explicitly — this is the
  chapter's own derivation, distinct from the editor-compiled per-cakra heading
  lists flagged above. The "48 subsidiary + 2 pituitary" split is stated here too.
- The per-cakra propensity headings in the primary discourse carry an editorial
  note — they were "added by us from information given by the author elsewhere":
  the propensity lists are Sarkar's; the cakra bracketing is editor-compiled (still
  from the author).
- **Could not verify in the cited passages:** the full seven-cakra chart (only six
  are named with counts, plus the sahasrāra as the seat of the thousand); a
  complete gland-per-cakra mapping (only pituitary and pineal, plus
  vishuddha→vocal-cord); the *suṣumnā* canal teaching; element/colour/bīja-mantra
  correspondences (implied by names like "terranean/fluidal/igneous" but not taught
  as such here).
- **nh2-6 (1979) adds a cakra-language mapping:** Parā at Mūlādhāra, Pashyantī at
  Svādhiṣṭhāna and the Madhyamā urge at Maṇipura are grounded verbatim in the language-
  expression account (cf. [Stages of Language Expression](/biopsychology/stages-of-language-expression.md)).
