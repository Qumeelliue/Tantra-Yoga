# Definition

"Nothing is fixed, nothing is stationary. Everything is on the move, everything
has to move. Cessation of movement means death." To rise above all complexes is
to refuse to encourage any **superiority, inferiority, fear or defeatist**
(hopelessness) complex, for **"You are the very children of Parama Puruṣa.
Hence you are not inferior to anyone."** (DNE 32.)

# Explanation

**Four complexes.** "You must never encourage any superiority complex,
inferiority complex, fear complex, defeatist complex, etc." Each is answered in
turn: no inferiority complex, for one belongs to "the highest and noblest
family"; no superiority complex, for "this entire universe is the creation of
the Supreme Father"; no fear complex, for "When the Supreme Entity, the Supreme
Father Himself, is your constant companion, then why should you fear anyone?" —
neither human beings, nor any theory, nor any doctrine, nor any dogma; no
defeatist or hopelessness complex, for "The path of your journey towards the
Supreme Entity is wide open. Hence, there is no question of defeat for you."

**Dogma defined.** " 'Dogma' means an idea around which some line is drawn,
beyond which it is prohibited to go. 'You will have to accept this doctrine, you
cannot go beyond it.' This sort of restrictive idea is called a dogma. You
should never be afraid of any dogma of this sort." The complex of fear must
never grip the mind: "You are not alone in this world ... This entire universe
is pervaded by Parama Puruṣa. We are always sitting on His very lap, we are
always sleeping on His lap."

**Equanimity — the fourth requirement.** "Lord Śiva said: Caturtho
samatābhavo [The fourth requirement for success is equanimity of mind]." From
the Śiva Saṃhitā: the first requirement is firm determination ("I must
succeed"), the second reverence, the third guru pūjā (constant remembrance of
one's spiritual master), **the fourth equanimity of mind**, the fifth control of
the senses, the sixth a balanced diet — "The seventh – There is no seventh
requirement." "In order to attain success in life, especially in spiritual
life, the fourth indispensable factor is balance of mind, samatābhavo. In other
words, one must maintain the state of psychic equilibrium. In its absence, no
progress in any sphere is possible, not even in the spiritual sphere." There
must be "a perfect balance" between extro-internal and intro-external psychic
movement; the mind moves from the physical sphere to the psychic, the
psycho-spiritual, and finally the purely spiritual sphere, maintaining balance
throughout.

**Human existence is an ideological flow.** "The starting-point of this flow is
the supreme positivity, and after passing through the supreme negativity, you
will again return to the supreme positivity. In other words, you move forward in
your evolution from the stage of crude animality towards subtler humanity. Your
final destination is extremely glorious, crowned with supreme effulgence. So
where is the scope for you to be afraid of defeat of any sort?" Nothing is
insignificant: "every one of you is a VIP – a very important person."

## Relationships

- the four complexes are the psychological obstacles that a [Sādhaka](/practice/sadhana.md) must overcome (cf. [Obstacles](/practice/obstacles.md))
- samatābhāva (equanimity) is the fourth of the seven Śiva Saṃhitā requirements for success
- refusal of complexes is an expression of [Neohumanism](/philosophy/neohumanism.md)'s universal self-worth
- fear of dogma blocks [Moralism and the liberation of intellect](/philosophy/neohumanism.md); dogma is further treated in [Dogma and Human Intellect](/works/discourses-on-neohumanist-education/038-dogma-and-human-intellect.md)
- the ideological flow (supreme positivity → supreme negativity → supreme positivity) is the [Existential Flow](/philosophy/existential-flow.md) of the unit mind
- the balance of extro-internal and intro-external movement is the psychic stance of [Sādhana](/practice/sadhana.md) (cf. [Dhruvāsmṛti](/biopsychology/memory.md))
- unshakeable self-worth is the temperament of [Bhakti](/practice/bhakti.md) — "you are the very children of Parama Puruṣa"
- equipoise of mind is the precondition of [Samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md)
- rising above the defeatist complex requires choosing [Śreya over Preya](/philosophy/shreya-preya.md)
