# Definition

The living being is a **composite of five koṣas** (layers of mind), "like a
plantain flower" with its petals. Ánanda Sútram 3-1:
*"Paiñcakoṣātmikā jaeviisattā kadaliipuṣpavat."* Each subtler layer is revealed
only after removing the cruder one.

# Explanation

In [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md), after the
[citta](/biopsychology/citta.md) comes into being, mind manifests progressively
through five sheaths (crudest → subtlest):

| Koṣa | Stratum | Witnessing puruṣa |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **kāmamaya** | conscious mind (crude) | Prājña |
| **manomaya** | subconscious mind (subtle) | Taijasa |
| **atimānasa** | supra-mental mind | (causal) |
| **vijñānamaya** | subliminal mind | (causal) |
| **hiraṇyamaya** | subtle causal mind (subtlest) | Vishva |

The **annamaya koṣa** — the physical body, a property of
[saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) — is the crude receptacle. Grouped: kāmamaya
+ manomaya = the *crude* and *subtle* minds; atimānasa + vijñānamaya + hiraṇyamaya
= the **causal (unconscious) mind** collectively.

These map onto **three bodies**:

- **sthūla deha** (crude body) = the annamaya koṣa;
- **sūkṣma deha** (subtle body) = the five koṣas kāmamaya → hiraṇyamaya;
- **sāmānya / kāraṇa deha** (supra-causal body) = the
  [mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md) and
  [ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md), between the hiraṇyamaya koṣa and
  merger into [Puruṣottama](/cosmology/paramashiva.md).

The macrocosmic counterpart of this scheme is the seven
[lokas](/cosmology/saptaloka.md).

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — kosas as crudification of citta

The 1959 cosmology (*Kośa*, II-5) gives the generative account the sūtra
compresses:

- **The five kosas are stages of citta's crudification.** "This process of
  crudification of citta, having a coordination of Mahattattva and Ahamtattva, is
  divided into five stages known as kosas" — from subtlest to crudest:
  **hiraṇyamaya, vijñānamaya, atimānasa, manomaya, kāmamaya**. The **kāmamaya** is
  the crudest stage, "where citta is metamorphosed into the crudest stuff."
- **Their functions.** *Kāmamaya* deals with the five bhūtas — the "crude mind"
  (*sthūla manah*); *manomaya* has recollection and contemplation (*smaraṇa* and
  *manana*) — the "subtle mind" (*sūkṣma manah*); the remaining three, the
  rudimental stages of these, are collectively the **causal / astral mind**. (The
  "conscious / sub-conscious / unconscious" nomenclature for crude/subtle/causal
  mind "does not appear to be correct.")
- **The three bodies.** The unit's *annamaya* kośa is its crude body (*sthūla
  deha*); kāmamaya → hiraṇyamaya constitute its subtle body (*sūkṣma deha*); above
  hiraṇyamaya, till merger into Puruṣottama, the unit possesses a body that cannot
  be termed causal — the last phase is **sāmānya deha**.
- **Macrocosmic kośas.** The Cosmic Mind's kāmamaya includes the unit's annamaya;
  the five bhūtas are "within the psychic scope of the Macrocosm," so its kāmamaya
  is "often termed the crude body of Paramātman," the remaining portions its subtle
  body, and its Ahamtattva/Mahattattva its astral/causal body. "By a process of
  correct sādhanā, the sādhaka will feel that there is one causal mind in the
  universe" — the unit's causal portion is not separate from the Cosmic causal
  mind; only "the seed of past action" differentiates the microcosm.
- **Witnessing entities.** Puruṣottama witnesses each macrocosmic kośa under
  different names: **Virāṭa/Vaishvānara** (causal), **Hiraṇyagarbha** (subtle),
  **Iishvara** (crude); the microcosmic counterparts are **Vishva, Taejasa,
  Prājñā**. (These are functional names of the one witness, not separate deities.)

# DNE cross-reference (DNE 36 = II-5)

DNE 36 (*Kośa*, 31 May 1959, Jamalpur) is the **same chapter** as *Idea and
Ideology* ch. 5 (*Kośa*, II-5) — the two files are separate electronic editions
of one discourse. The passages above (citta-crudification into five kośas,
crude/subtle/causal mind, the three bodies, the one causal mind with the
Macrocosm, and the witnessing entities) are corroborated verbatim in DNE 36.

# Notes — fidelity

- **Sarkar's five kosas here are stages of citta crudification** (kāmamaya crudest
  → hiraṇyamaya subtlest), NOT the classical Pañcakośa order of Ānandamaya-etc.
  The classical annamaya appears only as the *physical body* (a property of
  saincara), not as a kośa of the mind in this scheme. Preserve his ordering and
  do not normalise toward the Upaniṣadic list.
- The witnessing names (Virāṭa/Hiraṇyagarbha/Iishvara; Vishva/Taejasa/Prājñā) are
  **functional names of the one Puruṣottama**, as in [Saptaloka](/cosmology/saptaloka.md).
