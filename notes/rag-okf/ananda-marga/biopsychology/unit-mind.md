# Definition

The **unit mind** (microcosm) is the individual mind, composed of three strata —
[citta](/biopsychology/citta.md), [ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) and
[mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md) — which manifest progressively as
life forms evolve. Ánanda Sútram 1-18…1-20.

# Explanation

- Undeveloped organisms (creepers, shrubs): **citta only** (1-18).
- More developed: **citta + aham** (1-19).
- Developed organisms and humans: **mahat + aham + citta**, all three (1-20).

The unit mind is the microcosmic counterpart of the Cosmic Mind, unfolding along
the introversial [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md) of the
[Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md).

## In the Discourses on Neohumanist Education (DNE 13, 16 Jun 1979) — unequal domains, clash and cohesion

DNE 13 (*The Ever-Expanding Domain of the Microcosm*) stresses that "the domains
of all microcosms are not equal" — some, "according to their destination or
goal," even lose their psychic dimensions and "are gradually becoming smaller
and smaller." Growth occurs "through clash and cohesion": the human body "became
more and more complex with increasingly complex permutations and combinations of
the five elements (páiṋcabhaotika)" yet "did not increase its physical power;
rather, on the contrary, it lost some of its physical strength. As the nervous
system developed, the hands and legs, teeth and nails lost some of their
strength, and the eyesight also became weaker." The spiritual limit remains:
**"no true progress can occur in the psychic or in the physical realm of the
five elements (páiṋcabhaotika); progress occurs only in the spiritual sphere."**

## In the Discourses on Neohumanist Education (DNE 36, 31 May 1959) — the composition and divisions of the unit mind

DNE 36 (*Kośa*) locates the unit mind where **Mahattattva, Ahamtattva and citta
are all present**: "Hence in the stage of citta we get Mahattattva and Ahamtattva
as well." Its divisions are **crude, subtle and causal mind** — kāmamaya kośa is
the "crude mind" (sthúla manah), manomaya the "subtle mind" (súksma manah), and
atimānasa + vijiṋānamaya + hiraṇmaya the "causal" or "astral" mind. Sarkar adds
that "the psychological nomenclature of 'conscious', 'sub-conscious' and
'unconscious' minds for the crude, subtle and causal minds does not appear to be
correct." The unit causal mind has no separate existence from the Cosmic causal
mind: "By a process of correct sādhana, the sādhaka will feel that there is one
causal mind in the universe" — only "the seed of past action" differentiates the
microcosm from the Macrocosm.

## Relationships

- comprises [Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md) + [Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) + [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md)
- strata evolve across life forms; microcosm within [Pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md)
- merges toward source in [Samādhi](/practice/samadhi.md)
- *DNE 13*: domains of microcosms are **unequal**; growth through clash and cohesion; the body complexified while physical strength declined; progress occurs only in the spiritual sphere
- *DNE 36*: the unit mind = Mahattattva + Ahamtattva + citta; crude/subtle/causal divisions (the conscious/subconscious/unconscious nomenclature "does not appear to be correct"); one causal mind with the Macrocosm — only "the seed of past action" differentiates ([Sādhana](/practice/sadhana.md))
