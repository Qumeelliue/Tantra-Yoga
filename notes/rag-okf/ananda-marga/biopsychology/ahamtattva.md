# Definition

**Ahaṃtattva** is the "doer / owner I" — it evolves out of [citta](/biopsychology/citta.md)
when, under *vidyā shakti* (the introversial pull toward [Paramaśiva](/cosmology/paramashiva.md)),
tamoguṇa wanes and **rajoguṇa** becomes dominant. Ánanda Sútram 1-14.

# Explanation

It is the part of the mind-body where rajoguṇa predominates — the **middle
stratum** of the [unit mind](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md), between citta and
mahat. Its surplus over citta is [buddhi](/biopsychology/buddhi.md).

## Relationships

- evolves-from [Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md) (rajoguṇa of [Guṇa](/cosmology/guna.md))
- below [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md); stratum of the [Unit Mind](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md)
- surplus over citta = [Buddhi](/biopsychology/buddhi.md)
