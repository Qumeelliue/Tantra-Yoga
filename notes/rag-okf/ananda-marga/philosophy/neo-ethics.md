# Definition

**Neo-ethics** is the ethics indispensable for wiping out **imperialism** in the
present age, resting on two points: (1) **the Macrocosmic Entity must be accepted as
the supreme desideratum in human life**, and (2) **there should be happy adjustment
and balanced blending between carbonic and non-carbonic pabula**. Its application
"will lead to multi-lateral salvation of human society by removing economic
exploitation, political suppression, religious indoctrination, cultural imposition
and social subordination." (1987, PiaN 9/9.)

# Explanation

- **Microvita and the carbonic fallacy.** "Carbon atoms come from microvita" — not the
  reverse; billions of microvita solidify into one carbon atom. Every carbonic
  structure, animate or inanimate, has a mind (manifest or dormant). **Psychic pabulum
  of carbonic nature moves the mind toward the crude; of non-carbonic nature, toward
  subtlety.** Carbonic pabula maintain the physical structure; non-carbonic strengthen
  the psychic and aid spiritual/supra-psychic motivation. Defective carbonic intake
  adversely affects "the psychic triangle of forces" (the Ahalyā fable).
- **Imperialism is a psychic ailment.** Detachment from non-carbonic pabula and
  engrossment in carbonic pabula has two ill-effects: the mind drifts toward crude
  matter, and it "thinks in terms of devouring others' carbonic pabula" — the
  psychological explanation of imperialism. Externally it takes many forms — capitalism,
  state capitalism, communism, nationalism, communalism, parochialism, provincialism,
  socialism, caste-imperialism, male chauvinism, lingualism — "all the same psychic
  ailments in various forms and figures."
- **Imperialism is anti-human** — against the spirit of Neohumanism, detrimental to
  pramā-saṃvṛddhi/ṛddhi/siddhi; it generates war and divisive forces, attracts
  **negative microvita** ("poisonous radiation of black force"), and cultivates a
  psychology of slavery, inferiority complex, pseudo-culture and psycho-economic
  exploitation, sometimes nihilism and cynicism.
- **The Guru's role.** Only the **Supreme Guru** — who "has been using microvita to
  accelerate the spiritual growth of individuals" — can teach the technique of using
  microvita and lead living beings in the non-carbonic world: *Gurukṛpā hi kevalam* —
  "The Guru's grace is everything."

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** the two-point neo-ethics is "a panacea for the present
  imperialistic social ills"; imperialism is "rooted deep in the human psyche" and
  takes the named forms, all "the same psychic ailments."
- **Microvita are the cause of atoms** — Sarkar's novel ontology; do not normalise
  toward standard chemistry. Keep "carbon atoms come from microvita."
- **Could not verify in the cited passage:** a method for "balancing" pabula beyond the
  careful food-selection statement; the Guru-technique of microvita is asserted, not
  described in detail.
