# Philosophy & Ethics

Abstract principles and ethics — Neohumanism, microvita, dharma, and the sūtras
of Ánanda Sútram. Entries use `type: Principle` or `type: Concept`.

# Entries

* [Dharma](dharma.md) - To long for and run after the Great; the indispensable practice (dharma sādhanā) of every human.
* [Karma & Requital](karma.md) - Action as transmutation of citta; requital is citta regaining its state — no heaven, no hell.
* [Neohumanism](neohumanism.md) - Love and service extended to all beings, animate and inanimate, on a devotional-spiritual basis.
* [Pramā (Dynamic Equilibrium)](prama.md) - Psychic equilibrium and equipoise across the physical, psychic and spiritual spheres.
* [Struggle (Clash & Cohesion)](struggle.md) - "Struggle is the essence of life"; all progress through clash (saṃgharṣa) and cohesion; obstacles as friends.
* [Viveka (Conscience / Discrimination)](viveka.md) - The discriminating faculty (fivefold viveka paiñcaka) that chooses shreya over preya and guides the otherwise blind mind.
* [Shreya & Preya (the Good vs the Pleasant)](shreya-preya.md) - The Kaṭha-Upaniṣadic pair: permanent welfare (shreya/Paramārtha) vs fleeting pleasure (preya); the choice of the wise.
* [Idea & Ideology (Bhāva · Artha)](ideology.md) - "Idea" is psycho-spiritual parallelism (bhāva), "ideology" its conception; word-meaning is psycho-physical parallelism (artha) — the book's title concept.
* [Mahāpuruṣa & the Rejection of Avatāravāda](mahapurusa.md) - Elevated beings as gradual products of saincara/pratisaincara, not incarnations; the avatāra theory rejected as illogical and exploitative.
* [Sin, Crime & Law (Pāpa · Pātaka · Pratyavāya)](sin-crime-law.md) - The grades of sin (pātaka/atipātaka/mahāpātaka), pratyavāya; crime vs the legal code; ripus controlled, pāshas resisted.
* [Neo-Ethics of Multi-Lateral Salvation](neo-ethics.md) - The two-point ethics against imperialism (Macrocosmic Entity supreme; carbonic/non-carbonic pabula balanced).
* [Women's Rights](womens-rights.md) - Women and men equally divine with equal rights; ending psycho-economic exploitation through education and security.
* [Social Values & Human Cardinal Principles](social-values.md) - Human value takes precedence over social value; punishment for rectification, never revenge.
* [Suppression, Repression & Oppression](suppression-repression-oppression.md) - The three psychic calamities (pradamana/avadamana/damana) inflicted by capitalism and communism.
* [Moralism & Society](moralism.md) - Society as "moving together" on a systaltic force; morality = the initial inspiration toward the Cosmic state, distinct from religion.
* [Education (PROUT's Scheme)](education.md) - Awakening a thirst for knowledge; teachers as samāja gurus; play/stories/idealism; a world language.
* [Pseudo-Humanism & Socio-Sentiment](pseudo-humanism.md) - Humanism as socio-sentiment maximitis (enlarged nationalism); pseudo-humanistic strategy; every creature's existential value.
* [Prāṇa Dharma (National & Individual Soul-Character)](prana-dharma.md) - The internal psychic discipline of a life and the soul-character of a nation, struck at by foreign rule, capitalism and communism.
* [The Faculty of Knowledge (Jñāna Mārga)](faculty-of-knowledge.md) - Knowing as subjectivization of objectivity; the Shaeva cult ("What is this?") vs the prapatti mārga.
* [Existential Flow & Its Culminating Point](existential-flow.md) - The mind as a psychic flow; pravrtti redirectable into nivrtti; the culminating point = Parama Puruṣa, universal love personified.
* [Sāhitya (Literature as Service)](sahitya.md) - Literature as moving with the trends of life and hita (welfare); "Art for service and blessedness"; yuga vs tatastha sahitya; the sahityika as seer (kavi).
* [Parā-Jñāna & Aparā-Jñāna](para-apara-jnana.md) - Spiritual (paravidya) vs mundane (aparavidya) knowledge; prápta vs ápta vákyá; "know all – know one."
* [Genius & Technician](genius-and-technician.md) - "A genius is not made – only a technician is"; firm determination (Phaliṣyatiiti viśvāsah); civilizations fail from lack of coordination.
* [History (Itihāsa)](history.md) - History as the collective cultural record — the weal and woe of the masses, not of kings; biased history as dogma.
* [Symbolization](symbolization.md) - Expression through relative symbolization; latent symbols as samskaras; liberation when symbolization ends at the Ātman.
* [Gurukul](gurukul.md) - The guru-centred school; catuspathi → gurukul; public help not government aid; "Vishvavidalaya is a misnomer."
* [Synthesis & Analysis](synthesis-and-analysis.md) - Analysis divides, synthesis unites toward a singular goal; vijiṋána as Brahma-vijiṋána; science cannot change samskaras.
* [Aesthetic Science (Nandana Vijināna)](aesthetic-science.md) - The love of beauty as the fundament of the aesthetic science, uplifting humanity to the subtler worlds.
* [Art & Science](art-and-science.md) - Art as movement toward finer sentiments; science as rational expression governed by cause and effect; Shiva the first scientist.
* [The Inner Pilgrimage (Ātma Tīrtha)](../practice/atma-tirtha.md) - Átmá is the Supreme, the only tiirtha; external pilgrimage is the error of the támasa.
* [Emanation (Utsāraṇa) & Distortion (Vivartana)](emanation-utsarana.md) - Natural expansive change in a systaltic crest-and-trough flow (vidhāta-as-system) vs the corrupt, arrested change of distortion.
* [Vishva-Bhāṣā (World Language & Mother Tongues)](vishva-bhasa.md) - All languages "bound on a single thread"; the dignity of every mother tongue; a replaceable world language.
* [Reality, Intellectuality & the Supremacy of Spirituality](reality-and-intellectuality.md) - "That which we call reality is not reality" — neither reality nor intellectuality is absolute; only spirituality, moving linearly, is the absolute stance.
* [The Strata of Life (Four Strata & Trifarious Expression)](strata-of-existence.md) - Existence divided into physical/physico-psychic/psychic/psycho-spiritual strata; Neohumanism the cult whose goal is perfect spirituality.
* [Liberation from Staticity](liberation-from-staticity.md) - Liberation in every sphere — economic/political/social, psychic, and intellectual (where intuition is "the eleventh organ or the sixth sense").
