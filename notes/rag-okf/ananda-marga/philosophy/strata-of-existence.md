# Definition

**"Human life – and not only human life but the life of other living beings also
– is divided into four main strata"** — physical, physico-psychic, psychic and
psycho-spiritual. Everything moves within them; the final stage, pure
spirituality, also moves, but "free from all bondages of relativity." Neohumanism
is the cult — "the movement of all human expressions, all human manifestations –
and not only human expressions and manifestations, but all expressions, all
manifestations, of all living beings, including flora and fauna" — while perfect
spirituality "cannot be the cult… it is the goal, the desideratum, the
culminating point of all movements." (31 December 1982, Anandanagar; NH 1/13.)

# Explanation

## The four strata (NH 1/13, 31 Dec 1982)

- **"Human life – and not only human life but the life of other living beings
  also – is divided into four main strata. The first stratum is the physical
  stratum; the second one is physico-psychic; the third one psychic; and the
  final one is psycho-spiritual."** "In these four strata there is movement;
  everything moves, nothing is stationary."
- **The last stage moves beyond velocity.** "Even in the last stage… that is, the
  stage of pure spirituality, there is movement; but we cannot bring it within the
  scope of speed, of velocity, because that movement is free from all bondages of
  relativity."
- **Pseudo-spirituality vs perfect spirituality.** Sarkar rejects "spiritual
  maximitis" ("movement from imperfection to perfection") as the name for the
  intermediate state, preferring **pseudo-spirituality** — "spirituality that is
  not in perfect order, but is to some extent spirituality, having something to
  do with spirituality."
- **Dogma and its seed.** "Thus wherever there is pseudo-spirituality, there is
  the seed of dogma." Pseudo-spirituality fails in all four strata: in the
  physical world people are guided by group interest rather than the Cosmic
  rhythm, and "Dogma affects the free and natural movement of the collective
  world."
- **The cult and the goal.** "So that perfect spirituality cannot be the cult – it
  is the goal, it is the desideratum, it is the culminating point of all
  movements.… The cult is the movement of all human expressions, all human
  manifestations – and not only human expressions and manifestations, but all
  expressions, all manifestations, of all living beings, including flora and
  fauna. This is the cult, this is Neohumanism."

## The three strata / trifarious expression (NH 1/17, 2 Feb 1985)

- **"All expressions in this universe are divided into three strata – physical,
  psychic and spiritual – and not a single stratum among them is unimportant or
  negligible."** All three must be "properly developed, and their speed…
  accelerated."
- **Control hierarchy.** "The psychic world, partly directly and partly
  indirectly, controls the physical world, and the spiritual world, partly
  directly and partly indirectly, controls the psychic world." The apex logic:
  "mind is the subtilized form of matter," and "the spiritual world is the apex
  form of the psychic world."
- **Acceleration from the top.** "If the acceleration of spiritual progress
  becomes tremendous, then not only in the human world, but also in the physical,
  psychic and spiritual spheres of the entire world – and not only of the entire
  animate world, but of both the animate and inanimate worlds – there will be
  tremendous progress."

## The three spheres of human life (NH 1/2, 12 Dec 1978)

- "Human life has three strata, in other words, it functions in three spheres.
  The first one is the spiritual sphere, the second one is the psychic sphere and
  the third one is the crude physical sphere."
- Each sphere's afflictions are addressed differently: spiritual — personal
  effort plus "the blessings of realized personalities and just a wee bit of
  Cosmic grace" (*Mahat krpayaeva Bhagavatkráleśādvā*); psychic — requital of
  unserved saṃskāras eased by grace; physical — the collective distribution of
  wealth, for "this lacuna in the physical sphere, and the consequent disparity,
  is the noumenal cause of all phenomenal troubles."

# Notes — fidelity

- **Two framings, one node.** NH 1/13 gives **four** strata (physical,
  physico-psychic, psychic, psycho-spiritual); NH 1/17 gives **three** (physical,
  psychic, spiritual); NH 1/2 gives **three spheres** (spiritual, psychic,
  crude-physical). Sarkar uses both counts without reconciling them — keep them as
  distinct framings rather than merging them into one.
- **Could not verify** in the cited passages any single canonical statement
  unifying the four-strata and three-strata enumerations.
- **"Spiritual maximitis" is a coined term** for movement from imperfection to
  perfection; Sarkar explicitly prefers the term pseudo-spirituality. Keep his
  preference.
- **Neohumanism is the cult; perfect spirituality is the goal** — do not blur
  this distinction; pseudo-spirituality is the intermediate, imperfect stage.
