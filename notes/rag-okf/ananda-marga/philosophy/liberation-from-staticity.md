# Definition

**"In fact whatever is in this universe – crude matter, subtle idea or
consciousness – everything needs to be liberated."** "In the absence of
liberation, the natural expression of an object or an individual does not take
place." Liberation from the bondage of staticity is needed in the crude world
(economic bondage, political slavery, social dependence), from psychic slavery,
and from the dogma-blockade on the intellect — whose intuition is "subtler than
the mind" and "should be called the eleventh organ or the sixth sense." (28
January 1980; NH 1/10.)

# Explanation

- **Why liberation is indispensable.** "If we want to see the full expression or
  the total unfoldment of the qualities and capacities that are latent in
  anything, the liberation of that object or that individual is indispensable."
  "In every sphere of life liberation is necessary; liberation from the bondage
  of staticity is a must."
- **Not liberation *from* the body.** "It is not possible to attain liberation
  from this crude physical body; liberation from the physical body means death."
- **Crude world — economic, political, social.** "The effort to attain liberation
  from economic bondage, political slavery and all sorts of social dependence in
  the physical world, is the liberation from staticity in the crude world." "If a
  certain country is held in bondage to another country, then that subjugated
  country will have to wage war in order to attain liberation from the chains of
  slavery." Wherever there is worldly subservience — "economic, political or
  social" — "the proper development of the innate qualities, or the genius of
  human beings, remains remote."
- **Psychic sphere — slavery and exploitation.** "Subtler than matter is the
  mind. From psychic slavery, also, the mind should be liberated. We notice in
  human society various forms of psychic pressure, numerous ways of
  exploitation." "The internal urge to attain liberation in every sphere of life
  is a natural wont of each and every human being."
- **Intellect as the essence of mind.** "The essence of the human mind is known
  as intellect." "Intuition cannot be precisely called the essence of mind. In
  fact, intuition is subtler than the mind, and a very special aspect of human
  existence. Intuition should be called the eleventh organ or the sixth sense."
- **The unstained yardstick of discrimination.** "Discrimination between holy and
  unholy, permanent and impermanent, pure and impure in the intellectual sphere
  has a certain yardstick of its own. This yardstick should remain unstained."
- **Dogma is the blockade.** "What is dogma? Dogma is a preconceived idea which
  forbids human beings to outstep the limits of that idea or object. In this
  situation the human intellect cannot freely function." "Where there is dogma,
  even ten to twenty percent of the human intellect cannot be properly utilized."
- **The order of liberation.** "So we need the liberation of the human mind, and
  even before this we need the liberation of the human intellect." The slogan:
  "in all corners of the world and in all directions, one and only one slogan
  should echo and re-echo from all mouths, 'Dogma – No more, no more!'"

# Notes — fidelity

- **Three spheres, kept distinct:** crude (economic/political/social),
  psychic (slavery/exploitation), intellectual (dogma-blockade). Do not merge
  them into one generic "freedom".
- **Mundane liberation ≠ mokṣa.** This is liberation *within* the world — from
  staticity in the crude, psychic and intellectual spheres — complementing, not
  replacing, [Mokṣa](/practice/moksa.md)'s spiritual liberation. Keep the two apart.
- **"Eleventh organ / sixth sense" kept exact** for intuition; do not restate as
  mere "higher intellect".
- **Direct teaching:** the yardstick "should remain unstained"; dogma's
  ten-to-twenty-percent claim; the slogan "Dogma – No more, no more!"
- **War for freedom is stated plainly** — subjugated countries "will have to wage
  war" to be freed from "the chains of slavery." Preserve this as Sarkar's
  wording.
