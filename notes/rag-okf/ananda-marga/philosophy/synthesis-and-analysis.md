# Definition

**Synthesis and analysis** are the two outlooks available to human society. The
analytic outlook "compels people to see the differences and bring divisions among
different nations" — Aryans/Negroes/Mongolians, Buddhist factions — breeding
factionalism that "will prevail until human beings accept a singular goal in the
process of synthesis." Only the path of synthesis brings peace: "There is no other way
than this." (DNE 13, 16 June 1979.)

# Explanation

- **The analytic outlook divides.** "an outlook which is not synthetic but only
  analytic, compels people to see the differences and bring divisions among different
  nations. In the one human race there are Aryans, Negroes, and Mongolians. Among the
  Aryans there are Mediterraneans, Nordics, Alpines – also there are subdivisions of
  Latins and non-Latins. An analytic perspective brings divisions, and divisive
  tendencies create impediments to peace. The only way out now to save human beings
  from destroying tranquillity is to adopt the path of synthesis; there is no other way
  than this."
- **Factionalism until a singular goal.** "The spiritual goal is one. But in the
  absence of this unitary goal, factionalism emerged. Factionalism does not mean only
  groupism; it is also a conflict of doctrines. And it will prevail until human beings
  accept a singular goal in the process of synthesis." The Buddhist example: two groups
  of bhikkus — Sthaviravádii (southern) and Mahásáḿghika (northern), later
  "Hiinayána" and "Maháyána" — then "innumerable branches and sub-branches… always
  engaged in vindicating their own doctrines and maligning others'."
- **"Peace, peace" is a hollow sham.** "Peace may come for the time being, but it will
  not be permanent; like an ash-covered spark, unrest will again flair up." "Continually
  mouthing the empty slogan, 'peace, peace,' is nothing but a hollow sham."
- **Transcending time, space and person.** "The human entity exists within the
  boundaries of time, space and person. While remaining within the scope of time, space
  and person, people should endeavour to go beyond, to transcend these limitations and
  march on towards the Infinite. There is no other alternative to reach the goal of the
  Infinite than following the path of synthesis, than evolving a synthetic consciousness.
  Thus one will have to adopt the way of mysticism, to break free from the bondages of
  limitation and merge with the Infinite. There is no alternative."
- **Vijiṋána is not English "science" (DNE 3).** "The Sanskrit word vijiṋána, however,
  is not synonymous with the English word 'science', because in Sanskrit vijiṋána stands
  for Brahma vijiṋána [intuitional science], or adhyátma vijiṋána [the science of
  spirituality]." Yet "the steady cultivation of science must go on and that such
  cultivation will never be an impediment to human progress."
- **Science cannot change saḿskáras.** "science can never change people's saḿskáras.
  Therefore, for the development of their personalities, there is no alternative for
  human beings other than to perform sadhana."
- **The singular goal and society.** "The establishment of a classless society is only
  possible for those who accept Parama Puruśa [Supreme Consciousness] as the goal of
  their lives, for those whose entire mental power is ceaselessly directed towards one
  supreme goal."

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** analysis divides, synthesis unifies; factionalism lasts until a
  singular goal is accepted; the path of synthesis is the only path to peace and the
  Infinite.
- The Aryans/Negroes/Mongolians taxonomy and the Buddhist-faction history are Sarkar's
  own framing (1979) — reproduce, not as independent scholarship.
- **Could not verify:** DNE 13's lecture overlaps with the 1959 *Prout in a Nutshell*
  printing of "Synthesis and Analysis" (PiaN 4/2); only the DNE chapters were
  consulted, and the DNE 3 text carries no explicit date in its frontmatter (the body
  closes "27 July 1958 RU, Gorakhpur").
