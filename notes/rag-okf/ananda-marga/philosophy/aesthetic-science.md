# Definition

**Aesthetic science** (*nandana vijiṋána*) is the science of the inborn "love for
beautiful and subtle things," which "is the fundament of the aesthetic science
(nandana vijiṋána)" and which "uplifts humanity from the dust of this worldly abode to
taste and feel the subtler worlds." It has been shattered by dogma and must now be
refined "by the untiring and impartial cultivation of knowledge." (DNE 13, 16 June
1979.)

# Explanation

- **The fundament of the aesthetic science.** "Human beings, by their inborn instinct,
  have love for beautiful and subtle things. They appreciate and seek to cultivate what
  is beautiful and subtle in their internal and external lives, and in the world of
  ideas. This love for art is the fundament of the aesthetic science (nandana
  vijiṋána)."
- **Shattered by dogma.** "Yet we have shattered this science of aesthetics by stamping
  doctrines upon it. We could have very well utilized this aesthetic science for our
  welfare through our developed wisdom, and based on this aesthetic science we could
  have guided human beings to follow the path of synthesis which, alas, we did not do."
- **Its role and cultivation.** "This aesthetic science uplifts humanity from the dust
  of this worldly abode to taste and feel the subtler worlds. Therefore every effort
  must be made to develop aesthetic science – a task not at all beyond the capacity of
  human beings." "the cultivation of knowledge should go on side by side with aesthetic
  science"; "let all the various human scripts be studied and developed impartially, and
  let the result be utilized in the cause of aesthetic science."
- **Progress is only spiritual.** "I have said that no true progress can occur in the
  psychic or in the physical realm of the five elements (páiṋcabhaotika); progress
  occurs only in the spiritual sphere."
- **The slogan.** "'Caraeveti, caraeveti' – 'March forward, march forward'. This should
  be the slogan of today's humanity."

## Relationships

- the "nandana vijiṋána" is the aesthetic wing of [Art and Science](/philosophy/art-and-science.md), whose Science is "rational expression… with cause and effect"
- the impartial, dogma-free cultivation it requires is a demand of [Neohumanism](/philosophy/neohumanism.md)
- it uplifts the microcosm "to taste and feel the subtler worlds" along the path of [Mysticism](/practice/mysticism.md), toward [Cosmic Mind](/cosmology/cosmic-mind.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** the love for art is the fundament of nandana vijiṋána; the
  science has been shattered by dogma; no true progress occurs except in the spiritual
  sphere; "Caraeveti, caraeveti" is the slogan.
- "nandana vijiṋána" is kept in the source's transliteration; the fuller aesthetic
  science doctrine of [Art and Science](/philosophy/art-and-science.md) is treated in
  its own node.
- **Could not verify:** the supra-aesthetic-science layer (cf. *Supra-Aesthetic
  Science and Music*, and *Namami Krsnasundaram* 25–27) is not covered by this source;
  only the DNE 13 passage was consulted.
