# Definition

**Karma** (action) is the transmutation of [citta](/biopsychology/citta.md); its
**requital** (*karmaphala bhoga*) is the process by which the distorted citta
regains its original state. Ánanda Sútram 2-19:
*"Vikrtacittasya pūrvāvasthāprāptirphalabhogah."*

# Explanation

The mechanism of action and requital runs through the strata of the unit mind:

- The **[ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md)** (the "I") is the **doer** and
  the **direct enjoyer/endurer** of the fruits of action (2-17,
  *"Ahaṃ kartā pratyakṣaphalabhoktā"*).
- The **[citta](/biopsychology/citta.md)** takes the **form of the results** of
  actions (2-18, *"Karmaphalaṃ cittam"*).
- Action = a *simulative transformation* (*vikṛti*, distortion) of citta;
  **requital is citta re-attaining its previous state** — experienced as pleasure
  or pain (2-19).

There is **neither heaven nor hell** as a place (2-20,
*"Na svargo na rasātalah"*): when a person does a virtuous act and enjoys its
fruits, the surrounding environment is "heaven"; when one does an evil act and
endures its consequences, the environment becomes a "hell." Heaven and hell are
**states of one's own making, here**.

Behind requital lies a **divine longing for welfare** (*hitaiṣaṇā*), 3-7
(*"Hitaeṣaṇāpreṣito'pavargah"*): the punishment for an evil act teaches one to
keep away from evil, and the reward for a benevolent act teaches that such reward
will never follow an evil deed. Requital is therefore **corrective and
educative**, not merely retributive.

# In *Discourses on Neohumanist Education* (1958–1959) — the two karmas and the misuse of karmavāda

Two DNE chapters apply karma to science and to social justice:

- **Pratyayamūlaka and saṃskāramūlaka karma (dne-3, Synthesis and Analysis, 27 Jul
  1958).** Science can change "the flow of a person's pratyayamúlaka karma [original
  actions]," but "the flow of his or her saḿskáramúlaka karma [reactions to original
  actions] will not be affected" — "science can never change people's saḿskáras," so
  "there is no alternative for human beings other than to perform sadhana."
- **The misuse of karmavāda (dne-21, Social Justice, 1959).** "I heard a millionaire
  speak at a meeting. He was arguing that in modern society the karmaváda [doctrine of
  action and reaction of the Giitá] should be more widely propagated, because he
  thought that if people could understand this doctrine of action properly, the
  countless shrivelled-up human beings languishing in the dustbins of society would no
  longer blame the capitalists for their miserable condition." — "What a wonderful
  capitalistic argument!… God save humanity from such perverted philosophies!"
- **Atonement can occur in the psychic realm.** "If people do have to undergo torments
  in proportion to their original bad actions, then they may undergo their suffering in
  a different way in the psychic realm. Without being deprived of food and clothing or
  being impoverished due to social disparity, people can just as easily atone for their
  past misdeeds through psychic suffering."
- **Karma-based blame is a vested-interest argument.** "It is useless to blame the past
  misdeeds or fate of others. Actually, blaming people's suffering on their past
  misdeeds is merely an argument used by vested interests to justify their position."

# In *Neohumanism in a Nutshell* (1978) — karmayoga and jñānayoga as the physical and psychic phases of progress

*The Process of Human Progress* (nh-2-3, 31 Dec 1978, Patna) maps karma and jñāna
yoga onto the evolutionary phases of humanity:

- **True progress takes place "only in the spiritual sphere."** "Long ago, I said that
  true progress takes place in only in the spiritual sphere. What we generally call
  'progress' is not actual progress."
- **The physical phase is karmayoga — Ananda Marga's "real karmayoga."** When early
  humans "tried to establish their physical supremacy in the external world, great
  pressure was exerted on their nervous system, resulting in a mutual conflict between
  their innumerable cells. This inter-cellular clash caused a marked development in human
  consciousness which, in turn, led to corresponding changes in the external human
  structure" (morphological change). "This transformation is called 'karmayoga'.
  According to Ananda Marga philosophy this is the real karmayoga." Yet "this process of
  internal and external change through inter-cellular conflict does not represent true
  progress" — the advancement had "no absolute importance [but] had significant relative
  importance."
- **The psychic phase is jñānayoga.** Psychic change "resulted in considerable clash
  among the nerve cells and nerve fibres which led to subtle changes in both the psychic
  and physical structures." "According to Ananda Marga philosophy, change in the psychic
  world can be termed 'Jñāna yoga'. It is also a change, not of absolute significance,
  but of relative significance."
- **Karma and jñāna as the bright side of the path.** Because karma and jñāna yoga
  developed, humans "will realize more quickly than their ancestors the need to follow
  the path of devotion. This is the bright side of karma and jñāna yoga. That is, these
  two yogas will strengthen the path of devotion, will make it more solid" (cf.
  [Bhakti](/practice/bhakti.md)).

## Relationships

- doer & enjoyer = [Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) (see [Doership](/biopsychology/doership.md))
- citta records results → see [Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md)
- reframes heaven/hell as environments of one's own action (no external place)
- deferred requital is carried as [Saṃskāra](/biopsychology/samskara.md) into rebirth
- requital is guided by a divine longing for welfare (hitaiṣaṇā, 3-7)
- dne-3: pratyayamūlaka karma (original actions) vs saṃskāramūlaka karma (reactions) — science can change the former's flow, never the latter; hence [sādhana](/practice/sadhana.md) alone
- dne-21: karmavāda misused by vested interests ("God save humanity from such perverted philosophies") — the social-justice corrective of [PROUT](/prout/prout.md) / [Social Justice](/prout/social-justice.md)
- *NHN 2/3*: "true progress takes place in only in the spiritual sphere"; the physical phase = **karmayoga** ("According to Ananda Marga philosophy this is the real karmayoga") — inter-cellular clash → nervous-system pressure → consciousness development → morphological change, of relative (not absolute) importance; the psychic phase = **jñānayoga** ("change in the psychic world can be termed 'Jñāna yoga'"); both are the "bright side" that strengthens the path of [Bhakti](/practice/bhakti.md) (cf. [Buddhi](/biopsychology/buddhi.md))

# Notes — fidelity

- Requital is **psychological-energetic** (distortion → restoration of citta), not
  a juridical reward/punishment by a deity. Keep this framing.
- "No heaven, no hell" is a **direct teaching** (2-20), not an interpretation.
