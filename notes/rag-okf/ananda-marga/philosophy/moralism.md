# Definition

**Moralism** is the starting point of social life: "The ideological component of the
unit mind which provides the initial inspiration for the individual to attain that
Cosmic state, is called 'morality.'" **Society** (*samāja* — "to move together") is
"the collective body of those who are engaged in the concerted effort to bridge the
gap between the first expression of morality and establishment in universal
humanism." (1959, Human Society 1/1.)

# Explanation

- **Society moves on a systaltic force.** The vitality of society depends on its
  existence (a collective creation) and its inherent dynamism; the activating force
  moves "rhythmically or undulating… not monomorphic but **systaltic**" — through
  expansion and contraction. "Vested interests cannot hold back the progress of
  society"; every prativiplava (counter-revolution) lengthens the social contraction
  but then "helps to accelerate the speed of the period of social expansion."
- **Morality is distinct from religion.** *Niiti* derives from "that which contains
  the principle of leading"; it is the starting point of spiritual practices, a
  "living force" that cannot be limited to time, space and person, and must lead
  human beings to the state from which they cannot be led further. **Dharma** means
  "the attainment of bliss or the endeavour to attain bliss through regular sādhana
  in the subtler spheres" — Brahma to the wise, the soul to devotees.
- **The critique of religion.** "Dharma is often loosely used for so-called religion"
  — but religions claim "messages of God," never follow the path of logic, preach
  that only their followers are "God's chosen people," and confine "universal
  fraternity within the confines of their own community." "Religion tries to transform
  the human mind into a state of staticity, because anything static is easily
  exploited." Religions have "proved to be flagrantly unworthy institutions, incapable
  of providing even the physical necessities of life." Directly or indirectly,
  "religion encourages communalism" — a group psychology based on religion.
- **The sages' test:** *"Yuktiyuktamupādeyam vacanam bālakādapi"* — "If a child says
  something rational, it should be accepted, and if the Supreme Creator says something
  irrational, it should be totally rejected"; and *"Kevalam śāstramāśrityam na
  karttavyo vinirṇayah"* — accepting scripture blindly brings the decline of dharma.
- **Morality and materialism.** The mind must be given its due importance; a materialist
  cannot judge efficacy because "the mind has to be kept above matter" at the moment of
  judgement — materialism "capitulates to idealism" if the mind judges.
- **Education in morality.** "The spirit of morality will have to be instilled in human
  beings from the moment that they first start to learn the lessons of social
  interaction… the mind of a child is the best receptacle for morality." The key
  responsibility lies with parents; teachers and well-wishers must help when parents are
  unfit.
- **Social progress** is "the concerted effort to bridge the gap between the first
  expression of morality and establishment in universal humanism."
