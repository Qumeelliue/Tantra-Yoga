# Definition

**"Without symbolization expression is not possible."** This holds "as much in the world
of the five rudimental factors as it is in the psychic world" — in the expressed
universe all expression happens through symbols. The horizon of what can ever be
expressed is tiny: "it has, therefore, been said that the expressed universe is a very
small island in the ocean of the unexpressed universe." (22 May 1971, DNE 37.)

# Explanation

- **The unexpressed universe.** The universe is "an combination of different kinds of
  waves"; the organs "are incapable of catching waves that are cruder or subtler," and
  "not only the majority of the waves… but indeed almost all of them, are beyond the
  capacity of human perception." Hence "the expressed universe is a very small island in
  the ocean of the unexpressed universe. This universe itself is such a small island,
  how very much less is the capacity of a human being."
- **Three properties of relative symbolization.** (1) **Never complete** — "between an
  idea and its expression a gap remains. There is never complete expression, and a
  feeling of insufficiency, of dissatisfaction, remains." (2) **Not identical across
  individuals** — "feelings and ideas never find identical expression and identical
  symbolization… This is because of differences in the individual psyches." (3) **Changes
  with time and space** — "symbolization has changed with the change in the country.
  Hence there is change according with time and space… Symbolization cannot remain
  static."
- **Unexpressed feelings become latent symbols — samskaras.** "The mental feelings or
  thoughts, before getting expressed in a symbolized form, really get symbolized in the
  mental stuff. If these do not find outwards expressions, or if an effort is not made
  for such an expression, then these latent symbols constitute the Samskaras or the
  reactive momenta… This is the reason why people take rebirths until such time when all
  their mental symbols get converted into physical expressions." These samskaras,
  carried over from previous lives or acquired in this one, are called **prarabdha**.
- **Parama Puruṣa cannot be symbolized.** "You are not able to express that which you
  experience. You can do it only partially – 99 per cent remains unexpressed." Hence:
  *"Yato vaco nivartante aprapyamansa saha…"* — "Words cannot be uttered because the
  vocal organ itself fails. The mind fails in symbolizing Him." "When because of the
  depth of the inner feelings, your mental waves seek symbolization but do not succeed,
  the mind itself ceases working. Such a state is called **Samādhi**."
- **The Guru dumb, the disciple deaf.** Because neither speaking nor listening can
  symbolize the Absolute, "the Guru becomes dumb and the disciple becomes deaf" —
  Krishnacharya: *"Já teni boli te tabi tál, Guru bob se shiisá kál…"* The remedy is the
  subtler symbolization of the deaf-and-dumb: "keeping silent and taking recourse to
  subtler symbolizations."
- **Liberation by converting symbolization.** "Direct your mental symbolization so that
  it finds shelter in the spiritual flow. In this way your symbols will be converted into
  psycho-spiritual waves… since the Ātman, the soul, is beyond the sphere of
  symbolization, the problem of symbolization ends. It will mean final liberation,
  **Mokṣa**." "In this very life convert your psychic symbolization into psycho-spiritual
  symbolization and become and emancipated being."

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** "without symbolization expression is not possible"; the three
  properties of relative symbolization; "the expressed universe is a very small island
  in the ocean of the unexpressed universe"; liberation = converting psychic
  symbolization into psycho-spiritual symbolization "in this very life." Keep the
  shlokas (*Yato vaco nivartante…*, the Krishnacharya lines) exact.
- **"Combination" and other oddities are the source's own** ("an combination of
  different kinds of waves"); not silently corrected.
- **Samskaras as symbols is Sarkar's distinctive formulation** — latent symbols *constitute*
  the samskaras, not merely accompany them; preserved as stated.
- **Could not verify in the cited passage:** a full catalogue of "symbols"; the chapter
  gives the principle of symbolization, not a typology of symbols.
