# Definition

**Pseudo-humanism** is ordinary/general humanism — "socio-sentiment maximitis," an
enlarged form of nationalism — plus the **pseudo-humanistic strategy** that
"outwardly preach[es] the gospels of humanistic idealism, but in actuality stabs
others in the back." Its corrective is the recognition of every creature's
**existential value**. (1982, *The Liberation of Intellect: Neohumanism* 8.)

# Explanation

- **Socio-sentiment and its radii.** Sentiment expanded beyond the individual to
  embrace others is socio-sentiment. Its radii grade: family (smallest — *socio-
  sentiment minimitis*), caste, community, national, international — up to
  *socio-sentiment maximitis* or *excellencio*. "The narrowest… is technically called
  'socio-sentiment minimitis', and the greatest… 'socio-sentiment maximitis' or
  'socio-sentiment excellencio'."
- **Humanism = socio-sentiment maximitis.** Enlarging the circumference beyond
  nationalism/internationalism "and [embracing] all people within one fold… is called
  'humanism', or… 'ordinary' or 'general' humanism." But it is NOT the panacea: (1)
  there is still **intra-humanistic clash** (the starving group fed so it becomes a
  supplier of raw materials and a purchaser of finished products; the "literacy drive"
  that paralyses minds to govern colonized people); (2) humans are not the only living
  beings — destroying the plant/animal world "will upset the balance among the plant,
  animal, and human worlds and result in the catastrophic ruin of human life as well."
- **Pseudo-humanistic strategy.** "Outwardly we preach the gospels of humanistic
  idealism, but in actuality we stab others in the back"; it has "some affinity with
  pseudo-reformist strategy" ("externally we may speak of reform, but in our heart of
  hearts, we have decided not to allow any change"). It brings aided groups "within the
  scope of one's own socio-sentiment," hence into later exploitation, "more intense in
  the economic sphere."
- **Existential vs utility value.** "Each and every living entity, whether plant or
  animal, has two types of value: one, its utility value, and the other, its
  existential value." Horses lost their utility value; synthetic milk would end cattle
  breeding. "Who says that those creatures who have lost their immediate utility value
  have no right to exist? No one has the moral right to say this… All are the children
  of Mother Earth; all are the offspring of the Supreme Consciousness." Even creatures
  with negative value must be preserved "by creating a congenial environment for them."
- **"Not here, not here."** "Thus humanism cannot be accepted as an ideal path to
  perfection. Human beings will have to progress further towards perfection": *"Hethā
  nay hethā nay, anya kothā, anya konakhāne"* ("Not here, not here, somewhere else,
  somewhere else") — beyond the confines of sentiment.

# In *Discourses on Neohumanist Education* (1982) — the metamorphosed sentimental strategy

*An Ideology for a New Generation (Discourse 10)* (dne-23, 28 Mar 1982) develops the
strategy of the pseudo-humanists into a full typology:

- **The metamorphosed sentimental strategy.** When a sentiment-based deception "is about
  to be detected," the exploiter "switches over to a sentiment different from the one
  which they were previously using" — "maybe from communalism to nationalism or from
  nationalism to communism — this metamorphosis of sentiment is called 'metamorphosed
  sentimental strategy.'"
- **Human chameleons and peaceful transfer of power.** "It is very hard to identify such
  gilded human chameleons" (cf. dne-7); they seek the "peaceful transference of
  political power" while retaining "the same exploitative machinery" — "the only
  difference will be that the white rulers will have been replaced by black rulers."
- **Demons in human framework.** The exploiters are "exceptionally clever and cunning";
  they "inflate the petty innate geo-sentiments or socio-sentiments of people, take
  them into their fold and guide them in any way they like… just for some petty social
  status." They are "veritable demons in human framework."
- **Two scriptural somersaults.** Kapila, after his *Niriishvara-Saṃkhya* (Atheistic
  Sāṃkhya) "was likely to be discarded by the people," "quickly invented a new concept
  of Janya-Iishvara [Proto-God]." Shankaracharya preached all-is-Māyā yet "composed
  hymns to the River Ganges" — "a case of somersaulting to save oneself from a very
  embarrassing position, just like a harmless snake pretending to be poisonous."
- **Truth surfaces.** "According to natural law, no truth is suppressed forever. It
  must come to light some day – it comes out indirectly from the sinners' mouths."
