# Definition

Sarkar derives **yuga** from the root *yunj* ("to add" in the merging sense) and
defines it functionally: *"When a particular period of time ends, when a particular
epoch ends, when a particular flow of life ends and another begins or emerges, it is
called yuga"* (AV 9). A yuga is therefore a **finite human/social age, not a cosmic
span** of hundreds of thousands of years. He is explicit: *"Personally, I do not
believe in this division of time into the Satya, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali Yugas,
although I do admit that there is some truth in the underlying spirit"* (AV 7).

# Explanation

**Etymology over astronomy.** In *The Significance of the Word "Yuga"* (AV 9) Sarkar
reads the four yugas not as fixed astronomical epochs but as **psychological and
collective states of action**, glossing the Vedic verse *Kalih shayāno bhavati…*:
one asleep in ignorance is in Kali; one awakened but inactive is in Dvāpara; one
risen and ready is in Tretā; and one who has begun concrete work has reached Satya
(*Krta*, "the age of practical action"). "Satya, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali yugas have
no significance apart from such psychological significances."

**Demythologisation.** He affirms the *spirit* of the sequence as a graded decline —
soul-oriented (Satya), mind-oriented (Tretā), body-oriented (Dvāpara),
food-oriented/grossly materialistic (Kali) — while rejecting the literal Purāṇic
time-scheme (AV 7).

**Yuga-sandhi.** In *The Transitional Period* (AV 7) Sarkar defines *yugasandhi* as
the "juncture between ages" (*yuga* = age + *sandhi* = joint) — the point of acute
social staticity, recurring "every three to four thousand years," when ordinary
effort fails and the advent of a **Mahāsambhūti / Tāraka Brahma** becomes inevitable
to re-energise the social body. He locates the *present* as exactly such a
yuga-sandhi (AV 9, AV 7).

**The present Kali Yuga's demand.** Because Kali is materialistic and fragmented,
*"Saṃghe shaktih Kalao Yuge"* — strength lies in an organised body; its acute
problems "require a collective, organised effort," and a new Satya Yuga begins "as
soon as you implement the socio-economic ideology" (AV 7). Ánanda Sútram 5-9 frames
the changing baseline of an age: *"Yugasya sarvanimnaprayojanaṃ sarveṣāṃ vidheyam"* —
the minimum requirements of an age must be guaranteed to all (and they change from
age to age).

## Relationships

- the four-fold yuga sequence is distinct from but historically aligned with the rotating varṇa-ages of the [social cycle](/prout/samaja-cakra.md); AV 9 names "the shudra yuga … kṣatriya yuga … vipra yuga … vaeshya yuga," so a *yuga-sandhi* is the transition between two social orders
- at each *yuga-sandhi* of acute staticity, when even sadvipras cannot lead, a [Tāraka Brahma / Mahāsambhūti](/cosmology/tarakabrahma.md) advents to infuse dynamism (AV 7, AV 9, SS 9)
- those who step forward to lead in the transitional age are the [sadvipras](/prout/sadvipra.md) (AV 9)
- [Krṣṇa](/people/krsna.md) is cited as a past Mahāsambhūti (~3,500 years ago) advening at such a junction
- the shift from one yuga to the next is the mechanism of [social change](/prout/social-change.md); a new Satya Yuga is established by collective implementation of the socio-economic ideology (AV 7)

# Notes — fidelity

- **Direct teaching:** the redefinition of *yuga* (from √yunj; the psychological gloss
  of the Vedic verse) and Sarkar's first-person rejection of the literal
  Satya/Tretā/Dvāpara/Kali time-division ("Personally, I do not believe…", AV 7).
- **Editorial, not Sarkar:** the Golden/Silver/Copper/Iron correspondences and the
  literal Purāṇic age-lengths are TRANSLATOR/editor footnotes labelled "mythology"
  (AV 17/8, SS 9 footnotes) — not Sarkar's affirmation. Attribute accordingly.
- The "every three to four thousand years" cadence (AV 7) and the ~3,500 / ~7,000-year
  Mahāsambhūti figures (SS 9) are Sarkar's stated **social/historical recurrence**
  intervals — NOT the multi-hundred-thousand-year Purāṇic yuga lengths he rejects.
- **Dedup:** the shudra→kṣatriya→vipra→vaeshya rotation belongs to
  [samāja-cakra](/prout/samaja-cakra.md); recorded here only as a cross-link.
- **Could not verify:** any fixed duration (in years) Sarkar assigns to an individual
  yuga (he defines it functionally); a *Mahāyuga* / larger cyclic super-structure.
