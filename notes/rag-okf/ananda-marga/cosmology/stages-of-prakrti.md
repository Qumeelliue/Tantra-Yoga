# Definition

As creation proceeds through the [triangle of forces](/cosmology/srstimatrka.md),
[Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) takes **three named forms**, each with its
witnessing [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md):

| Phase | Prakṛti | Puruṣa | State |
|-------|---------|--------|-------|
| **1 — pre-manifest (avyakta)** | Shivānī (Kaoṣikī) | Shiva / [Paramaśiva](/cosmology/paramashiva.md) | triangles in equipoise — theoretical stage |
| **2 — germ sprouts (sakala)** | Bhairavī | Bhairava | manifested; Puruṣa is now *saguṇa* |
| **3 — vibrational / manifest** | Bhavānī (Bhavadārā) | Bhava | the mental & physical worlds |

# Explanation

- **Shivānī / Shiva** (4-3): so long as the triangles keep their equipoise, this
  is the *pre-evolutional, purely theoretical* stage. Prakṛti the creatress of
  these initial receptacles is **Shivānī** (or Kaoṣikī); the witnessing Puruṣa is
  **Shiva**.
- **Bhairavī / Bhairava** (4-4): with the **loss of equipoise**, the germ of
  evolution sprouts from a vertex and moves as a straight line per the degrees of
  the guṇas — the *manifested state*. Here Puruṣa is **saguṇa** (see
  [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)), Prakṛti having
  the opportunity to express Herself.
- **Bhavānī / Bhava** (4-5): internal clashes betray **curvatures** (*kalā*); the
  density of *Puruṣabhāva* decreases; successive kalās arise, each *similar but not
  identical* to the last (**kalā pravāha**) — this is **sadṛśa**
  [pariṇāma](/cosmology/parinama.md) (homogenesis). It is why "human begets human,
  tree begets tree," yet a human of a million years ago differs vastly from one
  today. *Bhava* means "creation."

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — the creation of the universe

The 1959 cosmology (*The Creation of the Universe*, II-8) gives the fullest
sequential account of these phases:

- **Kaosikī is the cause of the kośas.** Prakṛti is called **Kaoṣikī** "because She
  is the cause of origin of different kośas which are formed later on in the
  process of evolution" — she is the Shivānī phase (Prakṛti as Shiva's counterpart
  in the triangle).
- **The bija.** The point at which the resultant force bursts out is the **bīja** —
  in Tantra, *kāmabīja* — "Here lies the innate desire of creation"; also called
  *icchābīja*, "seed of desire." The verse: *"Shiva Shakti vibhāgena, jāyate srṣṭi
  kalpanā"* (Shiva encircled in the triangle of forces, and Shakti/Prakṛti acted and
  reacted within themselves, are the cause of the divine desire of creation).
- **Nāda → kalā.** From the bīja the force expresses in a straight line
  (*jñānashakti nāda*); then, as Prakṛti gradually merges into Puruṣabhāva and
  internal friction (*guṇakṣobha*) grows, the flow curves — the first curvature is
  the **kalā** (*kriyā shakti*). Prakṛti active from kāmabīja to kalā is called
  **Bhairavī** (Puruṣa: **Bhairava**) in the nāda stage; the static-dominated
  mutative Prakṛti producing the sequence of curvatures is **Bhavānī**, "the energy
  of evolution," and the metamorphosed Puruṣa is **Bhava** ("to become") — Puruṣa
  has *become* the universe.
- **Kulakuṇḍalinī as fundamental negativity.** Because the mental body must retrace
  "against the singular positive force," the path of non-qualified practice toward
  mokṣa is "always the path of negativity" — the theoretical negative force tending
  toward final merger is **kulakuṇḍalinī**, the "coiled serpentine." Its starting
  point is in the **mūlādhāra** (controlling the crudest matter of the body); its
  arena is the **kāma-pīṭha**; the point of the Cosmic kāmabīja is on the back of
  **Shambhūliṅga** and that of negativity on the back of **Svayambhūliṅga**. The
  yogic raising of this "non-compromising aggressive spirituality" is "the only way
  to ultimate oneness with Infinite Consciousness."

## Relationships

- the named phase-forms of [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) and witnessing [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md)
- unfold within the [Sṛṣṭimātṛkā triangle](/cosmology/srstimatrka.md)
- at phase 2 Puruṣa becomes qualified — [Saguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)
- phase 3 proceeds by **sadṛśa** [pariṇāma](/cosmology/parinama.md), driving [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md)
- *Idea and Ideology*: Kaoṣikī = cause of the kośas; the bīja/kāmabīja point; nāda → kalā (guṇakṣobha); kulakuṇḍalinī as the negative force at [mūlādhāra](/biopsychology/cakra.md) / kāma-pīṭha, polarized by [Shambhūliṅga & Svayambhūliṅga](/cosmology/shambhu-svayambhu-linga.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- Shivānī, Bhairavī, Bhavānī (and Shiva, Bhairava, Bhava) are **functional names
  of one Prakṛti / one Puruṣa across creation phases**, not separate goddesses or
  gods. Preserve this structural reading; do not import Purāṇic mythology.
- The II-8 names **Kaoṣikī** (Shivānī phase) and the **kāmabīja / kalā** sequence
  are Tantric vocabulary cited from the prose cosmology — keep them as Sarkar's own,
  with the shloka *"Shiva Shakti vibhāgena…"* reproduced exactly.
- **Tension preserved:** the cosmological "fundamental negativity" of the
  kulakuṇḍalinī here (a force of the unit body) coexists with the [Kuṇḍalinī](/practice/kundalini.md)
  concept's sūtra-framing — cross-referenced, not merged.
