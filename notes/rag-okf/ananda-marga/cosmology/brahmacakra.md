# Definition

The **Brahmacakra** (also *Sṛṣṭi Cakra*, the Cosmic Cycle) is manifested through
the combination of the centrifugal ([saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md)) and
centripetal ([pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md)) actions of
[Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) upon [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md). Ánanda
Sútram 1-4 (and 1-3: *"Tayoh siddhih saiñcare pratisaiñcare ca"*).

# Explanation

Its nucleus is the *svabhāva* (characteristic bearing) of Puruṣa —
[Paramaśiva / Puruṣottama](/cosmology/paramashiva.md); the material cause of the
entire cycle is Puruṣa (Shiva). Creation emanates outward (crudification) and
returns inward (subtilization) in a beginningless, endless cycle that frames
mind, kuṇḍalinī, and sādhanā.
