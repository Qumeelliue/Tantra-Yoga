# Cosmology

The structure and cycle of creation — Brahmacakra, Puruśa / Prakŕti, the
movement of saiṋcara / pratisaiṋcara, and the guṇas. Entries use `type: Cosmology`.

# Entries

* [Brahma](brahma.md) - The Cosmic Entity: composite of Shiva (Puruṣa) and Shakti (Prakṛti).
* [Puruṣa](purusa.md) - Consciousness / Shiva — the witnessing entity and cause of creation.
* [Prakṛti](prakrti.md) - The Operative Principle (Shakti) — the force of Shiva.
* [Guṇa](guna.md) - The three binding principles: sattva, rajas, tamas.
* [Saiñcara](saincara.md) - Extroversial phase: subtle → crude, under increasing guṇas.
* [Pratisaiñcara](pratisaincara.md) - Introversial phase: crude → subtle, under waning guṇas.
* [Brahmacakra](brahmacakra.md) - The Cosmic Cycle around the nucleus Paramaśiva.
* [Paramaśiva](paramashiva.md) - Supreme Consciousness at the nucleus (Puruṣottama).
* [Five Fundamental Factors](five-fundamental-factors.md) - The bhūtas (ethereal → solid) and matter's crudification.
* [Tārakabrahma](tarakabrahma.md) - The bridge between Saguṇa and Nirguṇa Brahma.
* [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](saguna-nirguna-brahma.md) - Brahma within vs. beyond the guṇas; creation originates from Saguṇa.
* [Ānanda](ananda.md) - Infinite happiness beyond weal and woe, identified with Brahma itself.
* [Ātman](atman.md) - The one Soul; its reflection on mind is the jīvātman, the Reflector-Soul is the Paramātman.
* [Ota & Prota Yoga](ota-prota-yoga.md) - Puruṣottama's twofold association: individual (ota) and collective (prota).
* [Relative Truth & the Three Relative Factors](relativity.md) - Brahma is Absolute; the world is relative truth under time, space, person.
* [Saptaloka](saptaloka.md) - The Cosmic Mind's seven worlds; macrocosmic counterpart of the five koṣas, apex = Satyaloka (Puruṣottama).
* [Sṛṣṭimātṛkā & the Triangle of Forces](srstimatrka.md) - The tri-attributional primordial force flowing in endless guṇa-triangles.
* [Stages of Prakṛti](stages-of-prakrti.md) - Shivānī/Shiva → Bhairavī/Bhairava → Bhavānī/Bhava across the creation phases.
* [Pariṇāma — Svarūpa & Sadṛśa](parinama.md) - The two evolutions: homomorphic guṇa-churning vs. homogenetic kalā-flow.
* [Shambhūliṅga & Svayambhūliṅga](shambhu-svayambhu-linga.md) - The poles of fundamental positivity (source of saiñcara) and negativity.
* [Microvita](microvita.md) - Subtle entities (positive/negative) through which grace assists spiritual progress; distinct from vital energy.
* [Praṇava (Oṃkāra)](omkara.md) - The primordial cosmic sound (a-u-ma) produced in creation; Brahma expressed as sound (Shabda Brahma).
* [Vidyā & Avidyā (Māyā)](vidya-avidya.md) - The two momenta of manifested Prakṛti — introversive (centripetal) Vidyā and extroversive (centrifugal) Avidyā/Māyā.
* [Liilā & Kriidā (Divine Play)](lila.md) - The divine play (cause unknown to beings) vs the jīva's kriidā (cause known); Liilānanda & Nityānanda.
* [Cosmic Mind & Macropsychic Conation](cosmic-mind.md) - The Macrocosmic mind of Saguṇa Brahma; creation as His thought-projection; macropsychic conation vs the unit's micropsychic longing.
* [Yuga (Age / Era)](yuga.md) - The age/epoch reinterpreted psychologically (not literal Purāṇic spans); yuga-sandhi and the advent of Tāraka Brahma.
* [Acoustic Roots (Bīja Mantra)](acoustic-roots.md) - The seed-sound of every action; the fifty main sounds controlling the propensities; aum as their collective sound.
* [Nāma & Nāmī (the Name and the Named)](nama-nami.md) - Parama Puruṣa's countless names; the name as the activated power of the named; ground of kiirtana and the Iṣṭa mantra.
* [Shabda-Brahma (Sound as Brahman)](shabdabrahma.md) - The Supreme Cognitive Principle as sound — "shabdabrahma, praṇavātmakam brahma"; the primal sonic substrate of words, letters and languages.
* [Saṃvṛtti-Bodhicitta (the Manifest Universe)](samvrttibodhicitta.md) - Kāryya-brahma as the "physical transplantation of psychic idea"; the cross of Puruṣa × Prakṛti and its seed-glyph ka.
