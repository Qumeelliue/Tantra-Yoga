# Definition

**Microvita** (positive or negative) are subtle entities/agencies — **distinct
from vital energy** — through which [Parama Puruṣa's](/cosmology/brahma.md) grace
assists a spiritual aspirant's movement toward the Supreme. As Sarkar puts it,
**"even microvitum is nothing but the grace of Parama Puruṣa."**

> Scope note: the discourses cited here treat microvita in their **role within
> spiritual practice and grace**. Their broader cosmological role (as the subtlest
> entities between idea and matter) is developed in other works — see the fidelity
> note.

# Explanation

- Microvita are explicitly **not** vital energy: the doer-"I" performs sādhanā
  with the help of [prāṇa / vital energy](/biopsychology/prana.md), not with
  microvita; vital energy *accelerates* progress, while microvita *help* the
  aspirant move — yet the aspirant "cannot utilize the microvita."
- They come in two polarities — **positive and negative**.
- When proper spiritual practice ("cult") subtilises the mental flow, the aspirant
  receives help from **positive microvita**; the **eight occult powers** (aṇimā,
  laghimā, mahimā, iishitva, vashitva, prakāmya, prāpti, antaryāmitva) are each
  acquired through positive microvita.
- They have a **ceiling**: microvita "cannot function above the psycho-spiritual
  level, beyond the pituitary gland." In the final pure-spiritual phase neither
  microvita nor any vibration helps — only grace. Omniscience cannot be acquired
  through them.
- They are the **medium of grace and effort**: pleased by the aspirant's effort,
  Parama Puruṣa "will get the work done with the help of positive microvita"; the
  aspirant only continues effort and the credit goes to Him.

# In *Microvitum in a Nutshell* (1986–1989) — the cosmological doctrine

The *Microvitum in a Nutshell* volume supplies the cosmological definition the SS
discourses presuppose:

- **Definition (mv-1).** Microvita are entities "smaller or subtler than atoms,
  electrons or protons, and in the psychic realm may be subtler than ectoplasm";
  "as far as physicality is concerned, the position of these microvita is just
  between ectoplasm and electron, but they are neither ectoplasm nor electron."
  They are "not of protoplasmic order" — not carbon-based. They are "the mysterious
  emanation of cosmic factor."
- **Three categories** by perceptibility: (1) within a microscope's range (popularly
  "virus" — "the better term will be microvitum, and not virus"); (2) beyond the
  microscope but within perception by their actional vibration; (3) within a special
  perception "which is actually the reflection of conception within the periphery of
  perception" — felt by spiritually oriented minds.
- **The root cause of life.** "The root cause of life is not the unicellular protozoa
  or unit protoplasmic cell, but this unit microvitum" — microvita are "the carriers
  of life in different stars, planets and satellites," moving unbarred through the
  universe, through sound, tactuality, form, smell and ideas.
- **Crude and subtle; the seven types (mv-3).** Crude microvita emanate life in the
  cosmos (emergence of dinosaurs → mammals → humans); subtle microvita divide into
  those within the world of perception (via tanmātras) and those acting directly in
  the human mind. The **seven types** (collective names, per the ancient ṛṣis):
  *yakṣa* (the urge to accumulate wealth/capitalism — a psychic ailment), *gandharva*
  (love of the fine arts), *kinnara* (thirst for beauty — *kinnari vṛtti*),
  *vidyādhara* (urge to attain good qualities), *prakṛtiliina* (rushing toward crude
  pleasures), *videhaliina* (mind rushing restlessly, oblivious of the supreme
  purpose), and *siddha* (helping in the domain of spirituality — "the Sudharsan
  Cakra is nothing but the symbolic name of siddha microvita"). (The devayonis:
  yakṣa, gandharva, kinnara, vidyādhara, videhaliina, prakṛtiliina, siddha.)
- **Matter and abstract (mv-18).** "Matter is not bottled-up energy" — matter is the
  "known 'I'" in the cosmic arena; energy and microvita lie "on this silver line of
  demarcation between matter and abstract." **Energy is a blind force** (no
  conscience), **microvita are not blind** — "they have the support of conscience
  behind them." Negative microvita function better in the physical/physico-psychic
  strata, positive in the psychic/psycho-spiritual. Microvita can change bodily
  temperature, psychic wavelength, and hormone secretion.

# In the Discourses on Neohumanist Education (1987) — neutrality, disease, and the glands

**DNE 17 (Prámā – 4, Feb 1987) — microvita are neither friends nor enemies by
nature.** "Some microvita are, in effect, your friends and some are your
enemies. **By nature, no microvitum is either a friend or enemy of human
beings.**" The shloka is cited:

> Na kashcit kasyacit mitraḿna kashcit kasyacit ripuh
> Vyavaháreńa mitráńi jáyante ripavastathá.
>
> [No one is the enemy of anyone by nature. By one's behaviour a person may
> become friendly or inimical to others.]

"By nature inimical microvita may also become your friends if controlled by good
people. Though poison is fatal for humans, it is also a life-saving drug." And
microvita can cause disease: "Microvita may create physico-psychic diseases also
– diseases starting from the physical level and coming to the psychic level."

**DNE 29 (Bio-Psychology, 3 Jun 1987) — Parama Puruṣa acts on the glands through
microvita.** "Parama Puruṣa applies microvita to different glands and
sub-glands." The polarity rules the plexi: "The grace of Parama Puruṣa influences
all plexi … Positive microvita function through all plexi. Negative microvita do
not function in the pituitary and pineal glands." "As a general rule the grace
of Parama Puruṣa is expressed through positive microvita in different plexi."
Propensities are also managed microvitally: "Good propensities may be enhanced
with positive microvita and diminished with negative microvita. Bad propensities
may be enhanced with negative microvita and diminished with positive microvita."
Thus the Guru's grace: "When the Guru is pleased with the disciples' efforts,
the Guru graces them by encouraging and enhancing their good propensities and by
reducing their bad ones by microvita."

# In *Varna Vijinana* (1983) — the vibration-based bridge between the physical and the psychic

*Varna Vijinana* describes the same idea↔matter bridge that microvita occupy,
from the sound/vibration side:

- **Emanation is vibrational.** (vv-20) "This manifestation or emanation is
  vibrational, and this vibration moves in a contractive-expansive flow, a
  systaltic order which moves in crests and troughs." The "wave impulse that is
  present in the first phase of emanation wanes in the subsequent phase due to
  the different blows and counterblows" — a mechanical picture of how vibration
  transmits from the subtle to the crude, the stratum microvita are said to
  occupy ("between ectoplasm and electron").
- **Vibration bridges the nerves and the mind.** (vv-9) "First a vibration arises
  in the nerves when an object is seen, then a mental sound is created and finally
  a word is made based on that sound" — an example of the physical→psychic
  transmission through which a crude contact produces a mental (and then acoustic)
  expression, paralleling microvita's role in transmitting impulses between the
  physical and psychic strata.

# Notes — fidelity

- **Anti-syncretism:** in these passages microvita are **subtle spiritual/occult
  agencies of grace**, NOT microbes, viruses, or germ-theory entities. Do not
  normalise toward biology. (The "virus" identification in mv-1 is Sarkar's own
  preference — "the better term will be microvitum, and not virus.")
- Sarkar subordinates them to grace ("nothing but the grace of Parama Puruṣa"), so
  they are not autonomous physical agents here. All cited discourses are **late
  (1986–1989)** — microvita is a late-period teaching.
- **Microvitum in a Nutshell now grounds the cosmological role** (root cause of life,
  seven types, matter/abstract) previously could-not-verified — this note supersedes
  the earlier scope caveat; the *grace* framing of the SS passages remains the primary
  spiritual reading.
- **The seven names are Sarkar's own** (per the ancient ṛṣis, as he states) — keep
  them exact; the Sudharsan Cakra identification is his symbolic claim.
- **VV (1983) connection — interpretive, not direct microvita teaching.** VV 20 and
  VV 9 describe the *vibration-based bridge* between the subtle and the crude (the
  "vibrational" emanation; the nerve-vibration → mental-sound → word mechanism).
  These do not mention microvita; the link to the microvita doctrine (the stratum
  "between ectoplasm and electron," "on this silver line of demarcation between
  matter and abstract") is a connecting observation drawn here, flagged as
  interpretation rather than a direct teaching.
- **DNE corroboration (1987):** DNE 17's "neither friend nor enemy" neutrality and
  DNE 29's glandular microvita-grace are direct teaching, consistent with the SS 18
  grace-framing; the shloka is quoted as printed in the source (with the source's
  "mitraḿna" conflation preserved). The "disease" claim (DNE 17) extends the
  doctrine into physico-psychic pathology — keep it alongside the grace framing,
  not as a germ-theory reading.
