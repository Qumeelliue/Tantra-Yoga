# Definition

The **sṛṣṭimātṛkā** is the *tri-attributional primordial force* — the progenitrix
of creation — flowing "in endless triangular forms." Ánanda Sútram 4-1:
*"Triguṇātmikā srṣṭimātrkā asheṣatrikoṇadhārā."*

# Explanation

In Parama Puruṣa, **countless linear waves** arise in the flows of *sattva*
(sentient), *rajah* (mutative) and *tamah* (static) — the three
[guṇas](/cosmology/guna.md). Their triple-attributional flows evolve **triangles**
and other multi-conical or polygonal diagrams; but the polygons are gradually
reduced back to triangles through **svarūpa pariṇāma** (homomorphic evolution).
This triple-qualitative *mātṛkāśakti* is endless.

Within the triangle (4-2, *"Tribhūje Sā svarūpapariṇāmātmikā"*) the three
attributes are locked in **endless mutual transformation** —
sattva → rajah → tamah, then tamah → rajah → sattva, back and forth. The thread
interweaving the mid-points of these triangles is **Puruṣottama /
[Paramaśiva](/cosmology/paramashiva.md)**.

# In *Varna Vijinana* (1983) — kāryabrahma and the seed-letter ka

*Varna Vijinana* 20 (*Emanation*, 30 October 1983, Calcutta) names the manifest
universe that the sṛṣṭimātṛkā generates and traces its symbol:

- **The manifest universe — saṃvrttibodhicitta / kāryabrahma.** "the manifest
  universe is a result of the influence of the guńas of prakrti over consciousness
  – the supremacy of the binding faculty over the cognitive faculty. … This
  manifest universe is called saḿvrttibodhicitta in the Buddhist nomenclature and
  káryabrahma (expressed universe) in Aryan nomenclature."
- **The cross and the letter ka.** "In order to indicate this state of puruśa and
  prakrti a vertical line is used for puruśa and a horizontal line is used for
  prakrti. Together the two form a cross (+) which signifies káryabrahma or the
  created universe. The biija [seed] mantra of káryabrahma is ka so this cross
  became ka in the Bráhmii script. Through the process of writing the Bráhmii ka
  quickly it became transformed through distortion into today's letter ka."
