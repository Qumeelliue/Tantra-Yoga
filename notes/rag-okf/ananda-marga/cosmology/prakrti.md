# Definition

**Prakṛti** is the **Operative Principle** — "the shakti (force) of Shiva."
Ánanda Sútram 1-2: *"Shaktih Sā Shivasya Shaktih."*

# Explanation

Prakṛti is the conjunctive force linking the material cause to the efficient
cause. She is not all-pervading and is "sheltered in [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md)",
acting only as much as she is authorized. Her worldly manifestations are
effected by the three [guṇas](/cosmology/guna.md). Her ever-increasing influence
is [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md); her waning influence,
[pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md). Ultimately "Prakṛti is but the
characteristic of Puruṣa himself."

## Relationships

- pole-of [Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md) (with [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md))
- force-of [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md)
- operates-via [Guṇa](/cosmology/guna.md)
- drives [Saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) and [Pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- Shakti = Prakṛti here (the Operative Principle) — subordinate to, yet
  inseparable from, Puruṣa; not an independent goddess.
