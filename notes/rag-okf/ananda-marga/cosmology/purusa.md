# Definition

**Puruṣa** is Consciousness — the witnessing entity, "the witness-ship that lies
quiescent in every entity" (*Pure shete yah sah puruṣah*). It is equivalent to
**Shiva** and is also called **Citishakti** (Cognitive Principle).

# Explanation

Puruṣa is all-pervading, and so the only possible **material cause** of creation;
as the doer-witness it is also the primary **efficient cause**.
[Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) acts only to the extent Puruṣa authorizes her.
As the [guṇas](/cosmology/guna.md) bind it, Puruṣa appears to undergo
crudification (→ Mahattattva, Ahaṃtattva, citta), yet it remains the witness in
all states and conditions — the constant ground of the
[Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md).

## Relationships

- pole-of [Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md) (with [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md))
- authorizes [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md)
- nucleus-form is [Paramaśiva](/cosmology/paramashiva.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- Shiva / Puruṣa / (in common usage) ātman denote the same witnessing
  consciousness here. Distinguish from the sectarian deity "Shiva."
