# Definition

**Ota** and **prota yoga** are the two modes by which
[Puruṣottama](/cosmology/paramashiva.md) — the Nucleus of the universe — is
associated with creation. Ánanda Sútram 2-10: *"Otahprotah yogābhyāṃ saṃyuktah
Puruṣottamah"* — "Puruṣottama is linked to each entity individually and to all
entities collectively."

# Explanation

- **Ota yoga** (the yoga of *individual* association): Puruṣottama is the witness
  of, and directly concerned with, **every unit entity** — one-to-one.
- **Prota yoga** (the yoga of *pervasive* association): Puruṣottama is the
  collective entity of the universe and the witness of the **collective mind** —
  one-to-all.

The one who is associated with His objects through **both** ota and prota yoga at
the same time is, by definition, Puruṣottama.

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — prota in saincara, both in pratisaincara

The 1959 cosmology (*Ātman, Paramātman and Sādhanā*, II-6) gives the phase-wise
distribution:

- **Association of Puruṣottama with the Cosmic Mind** — where He functions as the
  witness of the *collective* mind — is called **prota yoga**; His association
  with the unit minds **separately and individually**, witnessing each unit mind,
  is **ota yoga**; His association with unit minds *in collectivity* is also prota
  yoga.
- Hence **in saincara there is only prota yoga** (no units yet — only the Cosmic
  Mind); **in pratisaincara both ota yoga and prota yoga** are present.
- The reflection involved "is not like a reflection in a mirror but a reflection
  involving association like the rays of the sun" — through His ota yoga He
  associatively reflects on the unit psychic plate; through prota yoga He
  witnesses the Macrocosm. **Paramātman** is the collective name for Puruṣottama
  together with His prota yoga in saincara and His ota and prota yogas in
  pratisaincara — "the consciousness in the Nucleus and the consciousness in the
  Macrocosm and microcosms," while the Macrocosm and microcosms themselves are
  **not** included in Paramātman.

## Relationships

- twofold association of [Paramaśiva (Puruṣottama)](/cosmology/paramashiva.md)
- witnesses each [unit mind](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md) (ota) and the collective mind (prota)
- complements the witness-role of [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md)
- phase-wise: only prota in [Saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md); ota + prota in [Pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md)
- ota-prota define the scope of [Paramātman](/cosmology/atman.md) vs Saguṇa Brahma (II-6)
