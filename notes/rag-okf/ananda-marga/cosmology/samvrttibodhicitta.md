# Definition

In *Varna Vijinana* 13 Sarkar ties the first consonant of the alphabet to the
expressed universe: *"The letter ka prepresents saḿvrttibodhicitta, that is, ka is
a physical transplantation of psychic idea, thus it occupies the first position in
the list of consonants. Ka is káryyabrahma."* In *Varna Vijinana* 20 he states the
general cosmology: the manifest universe is *"called saḿvrttibodhicitta in the
Buddhist nomenclature and káryabrahma (expressed universe) in Aryan
nomenclature."*

# Explanation

**The binding of consciousness (vv-20).** Manifestation is a loss of cognitive
freedom, not a neutral overflow: "the manifest universe is a result of the
influence of the guńas of prakrti over consciousness – the supremacy of the
binding faculty over the cognitive faculty. In other words, Parama Puruśa comes
within the jurisdiction of the binding power of prakrti."

**Two nomenclatures (vv-20).** Sarkar names the same reality twice: "This manifest
universe is called saḿvrttibodhicitta in the Buddhist nomenclature and
káryabrahma (expressed universe) in Aryan nomenclature." *Kāryya-brahma* — the
effect-Brahma, the expressed universe — is the manifested, Saguṇa side of Brahma
(cf. [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)).

**The cross as glyph (vv-20).** The state of Puruṣa-and-Prakṛti is diagrammed:
"In order to indicate this state of puruśa and prakrti a vertical line is used for
puruśa and a horizontal line is used for prakrti. Together the two form a cross
(+) which signifies káryabrahma or the created universe."

**ka, the seed-mantra of kāryya-brahma (vv-20).** The glyph and the letter
coincide: "The biija [seed] mantra of káryabrahma is ka so this cross became ka in
the Bráhmii script. Through the process of writing the Bráhmii ka quickly it
became transformed through distortion into today's letter ka."

**ka, first among consonants (vv-13).** Because it is the seed of the expressed
universe, *ka* leads the consonant series; the ordering of script follows the
ordering of creation. The same chapter explains why *a* leads the vowels — "The
reason is that a is the acoustic root of creation" (see
[Shabda-Brahma](/cosmology/shabdabrahma.md)) — so vowels and consonants alike open
with the acoustic seed of manifestation.

## Relationships

- the *binding* of consciousness is the Avidyā-momentum of Prakṛti — cf. [Vidyā & Avidyā (Māyā)](/cosmology/vidya-avidya.md)
- the product of [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md)'s guṇa-influence over [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md); the expressed universe is born in the extroversional [Saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) phase of the Cosmic Cycle
- the "psychic idea" physically transplanted is the seed-sound tradition of [Acoustic Roots](/cosmology/acoustic-roots.md); the seed-mantra *ka* lives within the acoustic cosmology of [Shabda-Brahma](/cosmology/shabdabrahma.md)
- the letter *ka* is one of the fifty letters of the [Sṛṣṭimātṛkā](/cosmology/srstimatrka.md) matrix (vv-8: the akṣamālā on Parama Puruṣa's breast)
- as the *expressed universe* it is the Saguṇa side — cf. [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)
- the ceaseless subtle-vibration backdrop of manifestation is the [Microvita](/cosmology/microvita.md) cosmology

# Notes — fidelity

- **"saṃvrttibodhicitta" is Sarkar's usage of a Buddhist term.** He borrows the
  (Sanskritized) Buddhist compound *saṃvṛtti-bodhicitta* — "conventional /
  veiled awakening-mind" — and applies it to his own doctrine of the manifest
  universe as the binding of consciousness by Prakṛti. Reproduce his exact framing;
  do not import Buddhist scholastic baggage (e.g. the two-truths
  saṃvṛti/paramārtha scheme) into it. He deliberately places it "in the Buddhist
  nomenclature" as a name, paired with the "Aryan" *kāryya-brahma*.
- **Printed text has a typo.** vv-13 prints "The letter ka prepresents
  saḿvrttibodhicitta" — the "pre-" is the source's misprint for "re-". The direct
  quote preserves the printed form.
- **Transliteration variants kept.** Direct quotes preserve the printed forms:
  "saḿvrttibodhicitta", "káryyabrahma" (vv-13), "káryabrahma" (vv-20), "Bráhmii",
  "biija", "guńas".
- **kāryya vs kārya.** vv-13 prints "káryyabrahma"; vv-20 prints "káryabrahma".
  Same term; keep each chapter's spelling where quoted.
