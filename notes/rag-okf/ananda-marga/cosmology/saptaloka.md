# Definition

The **Cosmic Mind** is held in **seven lokas** (worlds). Ánanda Sútram 3-2:
*"Saptalokātmakaṃ Brahmamanah."* These are the macrocosmic counterpart of the
five [koṣas](/biopsychology/pancakosa.md).

# Explanation

From crudest to subtlest, with the witnessing puruṣa and the cosmic koṣa each
expresses:

| Loka | Cosmic koṣa / mind | Witnessing puruṣa |
|------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **bhū / bhuvah** | kāmamaya — crude cosmic mind | **Iishvara** |
| **svah** (svarloka) | manomaya — subtle cosmic mind | **Hiraṇyagarbha** |
| **mahah** (maharloka) | atimānasa | Virāṭa / Vaeshvānara |
| **janah** (janarloka) | vijñānamaya | Virāṭa / Vaeshvānara |
| **tapah** (taparloka) | hiraṇyamaya — causal cosmic mind | Virāṭa / Vaeshvānara |
| **Satya** (Satyaloka) | Cosmic Mahat & Aham (Causal Cosmic Body) | **[Puruṣottama](/cosmology/paramashiva.md)** |

The three subtler koṣas (atimānasa, vijñānamaya, hiraṇyamaya) together form the
**causal cosmic mind** / subtle cosmic body. At the apex, the witness of the
Cosmic [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md) and
[Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) is **Puruṣottama Himself** — the
Satyaloka.

# In the Discourses on Neohumanist Education (DNE 36, 31 May 1959) — the loka-kośa-witness table

DNE 36 (*Kośa*, the same chapter as *Idea and Ideology* II-5) gives the full
correlation in tabular form:

| Loka | Macrocosmic kośa | Witnessing entity |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| **bhū** (bhurloka) | kāmamaya where "material structure has taken form" — "physical world" | **Iishvara** |
| **bhuvar** (bhuvarloka) | kāmamaya where material structure "has just begun taking form but has not actually acquired it" — "crude mental world" | **Iishvara** |
| **svar** (svarloka) | manomaya — "subtle mental world" | **Hiraṇyagarbha** |
| **mahaḥ** (maharloka) | atimānasa — "supramental world" | **Virāṭa or Vaeshvānara** |
| **janaḥ** (janarloka) | vijñānamaya — "subliminal world" | **Virāṭa or Vaeshvānara** |
| **tapaḥ** (taparloka) | hiraṇyamaya | **Virāṭa or Vaeshvānara** |
| **Satya** (satyaloka) | the causal body (Mahattattva/Ahamtattva) | **Puruṣottama** |

The microcosmic witnessing counterparts are **Prājña** (crude), **Taejasa**
(subtle) and **Vishva** (causal). Sarkar stresses: **"The term loka refers only
to the Macrocosm and not to the unit."** The macrocosmic witness order is the
mirror of the unit's: "The Puruṣottama witnessing the causal Cosmic mind,
subtle Cosmic mind and crude Cosmic mind, has been called Virāṭa or
Vaeshvānara, Hiraṇyagarbha and Iishvara, respectively."

# In *Neohumanism in a Nutshell* (1978) — the seven strata and the Gāyatrī's "Savitā"

*Proper Intellect* (nh-2-1, 24 Aug 1978, Patna) ties the saptaloka to the Gāyatrī
mantra and its address of the Creator:

- **"This universe is saptalokātmaka."** "This universe is saptalokātmaka, having seven
  strata – bhuh, bhuvah, svah, mahah, janah, tapah and satya."
- **The creator addressed as "Savitā".** "Its creator is addressed as 'Savitā'.
  'Savitā' means 'Progenitor'." The name is masculine: "Some people wrongly name their
  daughters 'Savitā'. The name of a girl should not be 'Savitā'. 'Savitā' means
  'father', (masculine gender)." "We meditate on the hallowed divine effulgence of the
  creator of the seven lokas."
- **The Gāyatrī's word-by-word gloss.** "'Dhī' means 'intellect', 'nah' means 'ours' and
  'pracodayāt' means 'to goad on the right track'." The purpose of the meditation is
  "that He might lead our intellect (buddhi, medhā) on the right track" (cf.
  [Buddhi](/biopsychology/buddhi.md)).

# Notes — fidelity

- *Iishvara*, *Hiraṇyagarbha*, *Virāṭa/Vaeshvānara* here are **functional names of
  the witnessing puruṣa at each cosmic stratum**, not separate deities. Preserve
  this structural reading.
- **DNE 36's bhū/bhuvar split:** the sūtra-layer table (above) groups bhū +
  bhuvah under one kāmamaya row; DNE 36 separates them (bhū = material
  structure taken form; bhuvar = structure "just begun taking form"). Both are
  the crude cosmic mind under Iishvara — keep both renderings, do not unify.
- The microcosmic witnessing counterparts (Prājña/Taejasa/Vishva) are the unit's
  counterparts in DNE 36's table; "lokas are only for the Macrocosm" is direct
  teaching.
