# Definition

**Guṇa** means "binding principle." [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) works through
three guṇas: **sattva** (sentient), **rajas** (mutative), and **tamas** (static).
Ánanda Sútram 1-8: *"Guṇabandhanena guṇābhivyaktih."*

# Explanation

The stronger the bondage, the cruder the result. Successive impacts of the guṇas
on [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md) give rise, in order, to *Mahattattva*
(existential "I", via sattvaguṇa), *Ahaṃtattva* (doer "I", via rajoguṇa), and
*citta* (mind-stuff, via tamoguṇa); intensifying tamasic bondage then yields the
five fundamental factors (ethereal → aerial → luminous → liquid → solid).
Increasing bondage drives [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md); waning bondage,
[pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md).

# In the prose discourses

**The prose develops the guṇas as a quantitative play of bondage, friction, and
attributional expression.** In *Concept of Guṇābhivyakti and Jaḍasphoṭa* (AV 33),
*guṇābhivyakti* ("attributional expression") means a growing **variety** of
attributional manifestations, never an increase in total attributional **capacity**:
fixing the ethereal factor's measure at one hundred, each crudification step
(ether → air → fire → water → solid) adds one new attribute — sound, then touch,
form, taste, smell — while the combined measure stays unenhanced. Firmer static
bondage shrinks inter-atomic distance, raising the internal friction that compels
this expression. The same dynamic appears in *Struggle and Progress* (SS 7): the
centrifugal force of [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) generates a reactive
centripetal force within structures, the nadir of crudification being where the two
balance in the solid factor.

**Jaḍasphoṭa — the "explosion of matter" — is the turning point from saiñcara to
[pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md).** A dedicated sūtra governs it
(Ánanda Sútram 1-9, *"Guṇādhikye jaḍasphoṭah bhūtasāmyābhāvāt"*): when the solid
element's tension becomes abnormally high relative to the others, balance among the
bhūtas is lost and the structure bursts — otherwise living organisms arise instead.
In *The Expansion of the Microcosm* (SS 5) jaḍasphoṭa reconverts all five factors
back to the **ethereal** factor, which then re-enters saiñcara ("negative saiñcara",
1-9) — an inexhaustible recycling that, Sarkar argues, makes total thermal death of
the universe impossible and renders the notion of *pralaya* (doomsday) baseless
(*The Divine Drama*, AV 7).

**Upstream, the guṇas' very expression begins with guṇakṣobha.** *Guṇayantraka and
Svarūpa Pariṇāma* (AV 33) traces how the balanced triangle of forces loses equipoise
— one guṇa inter-converting into another at its vertices — until the resultant bursts
out the *kāmabīja*, the noumenal seed, beginning Prakṛti's influence on Puruṣa.

## Relationships

- wielded-by [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md)
- drives [Saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) (increase) and [Pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md) (decrease)
- produces [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md), [Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md), [Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md) (mental strata)
- jaḍasphoṭa is the saiñcara → pratisaiñcara turning point: the crudest solid bursts back to the ethereal factor and re-enters the cycle (AS 1-9)
- operates within the [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md) — the mechanism preventing universal thermal death and refuting *pralaya*
- guṇakṣobha (loss of equipoise at the triangle of forces) initiates [Prakṛti's](/cosmology/prakrti.md) influence on [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md)
- the prāṇa/prāṇāh interplay at jaḍasphoṭa links the guṇa doctrine to the emergence of [Prāṇa](/biopsychology/prana.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- "Guṇa" here = **binding principle**, not "virtue/quality" in the colloquial
  sense.
- **Prose (AV 33): guṇābhivyakti = increase in the *variety* of attributes, NOT in
  capacity.** The "fix the measure at one hundred" point is load-bearing — do not
  restate as "more attributes = more capacity."
- **Anti-syncretism (svarūpa pariṇāma):** Sarkar uses *svarūpa pariṇāma* for the
  mutual inter-conversion of the three guṇas at the triangle's vertices, and
  explicitly rejects the Sāṃkhya sense (each guṇa changing within itself at
  dissolution). Do not collapse the two.
- **Could not verify:** no read passage gives numeric/temporal scales for jaḍasphoṭa,
  nor any "Big Bang/Big Crunch" expansion–contraction cycle beyond saiñcara /
  pratisaiñcara — any such gloss is unverified.
