# Definition

**Pratisaiñcara** is the introversial movement of the Cosmic Cycle — the return
from crude toward subtle under the **waning** influence of the
[guṇas](/cosmology/guna.md). Ánanda Sútram 1-6:
*"Nivrttimukhii pratisaiñcarah guṇāvakṣayeṇa."*

# Explanation

Centripetal (*nivṛtti*). As [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md) contracts
[Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md)'s power, the crude gradually re-subtilizes —
matter → vital energy → mind → the merger of the unit mind back into
[Paramaśiva](/cosmology/paramashiva.md). This is the arc of evolution and
spiritual progress; the second half of the [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md).

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — the return and its reversal

The 1959 cosmology (*Pratisaincara and Manah*, II-2) grounds and extends the
sūtra:

- **Mind born of matter.** Pratisaincara begins at the crudest solid: the clash
  within matter "powders down" portions into subtler factors, and the effect is the
  **unit mind** — a "chemical reaction of physical clash," whose stuff is identical
  to the Cosmic Mind's. This is the speciality of Ananda Marga philosophy: it
  "explains by a logical and analytical theory that mind is a creation of matter,"
  yet pierces deeper than materialism by giving matter's rudimental cause —
  [Puruṣottama](/cosmology/paramashiva.md), the noumenal cause.
- **Psychic dilation.** As Consciousness reflects associatively on the "unit mental
  plate" (the mirror analogy), the crude mind is converted into subtler mind —
  *psychic dilation* driven by three forces: physical (physical clash), psychic
  (psychic clash), spiritual (longing for the Great). Dilation increases as
  proximity to the goal decreases.
- **Samvega.** The unit mind receives *momentum (samvega)* from the Macrocosm; in
  early stages it moves by the will of the Cosmic "I"; once ego and will develop
  (the human stage, *mānusha*), the mind can direct that momentum itself.
- **Negative pratisaincara.** With will comes the capacity to choose "any direction
  according to its desire" — including backward: adopting cruder psycho-physical
  parallelism makes the mind take a cruder body (a tree, protozoan, or "even that of
  an inanimate object such as stone"). The mind-stuff "takes the form of the object
  it entertains"; the Macrocosm's mercy ever re-launches the lost mind on the path
  of evolution, though it may take "millions of years."
- **The culmination is yoga/mukti.** The return completes when the psychic gap
  between reflected consciousness and reflecting plate diminishes and the two merge
  — **yoga**: *"Saṃyogo yogo ityukto jīvātmā Paramātmanah"*, and **mukti**, the
  finality of association. It rests on the theory "Oneness in objectivity results in
  oneness in subjectivity" — the basis of Īishvara Praṇidhāna.
