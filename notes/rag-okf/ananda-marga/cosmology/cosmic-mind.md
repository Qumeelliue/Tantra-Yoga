# Definition

The **Cosmic Mind** is the mind of **Saguṇa Brahma** — the Macrocosm — arising when
the mutual interaction of [Prakṛti's](/cosmology/prakrti.md) three guṇas causes
awareness to dawn in Puruṣa, who then passes successively through **Mahattattva**
("I am"), **Ahaṃtattva** ("I do") and **Citta** ("done-I"), and the quinquelemental
factors (SS 7). Within this mind the whole creation exists as His mental
projection: *"everything is being created, maintained and dissolved within His
Cosmic Mind. This internal psychic world of Brahma appears to be an external world
to the finite entities"* (SS 5). **Macropsychic conation** is the vast,
all-embracing psychic movement of that Cosmic Mind — *"a conative expression of the
Supreme Self … as a result of this macropsychic conation we get this universe"*
(AV 30) — as distinct from the narrow *micropsychic longings* of the unit mind.

# Explanation

**Genesis of the Cosmic Mind.** In Nirguṇa Brahma the three guṇas hold the supreme
triangle of forces in balance and the Macrocosm "does not think at all" (SS 18).
When equilibrium is lost, a resultant force bursts out of one vertex and creation —
and His conation — begins. *"When the mutual interaction of the three fundamental
principles causes a kind of awareness to arise, Puruṣa is called Saguṇa. Later, as
Puruṣa comes under the greater influence of Prakṛti in successive stages, He is
called Mahattattva … Ahaṃtattva … Citta … and the quinquelemental factors"* (SS 7).
The Macrocosm is "the composite of Cosmic Citta and Cosmic Operative Principle";
the quinquelemental world "originated from Macrocosmic mind-stuff (citta) surrounded
by the static portion of His mind" (SS 5).

**Creation as macropsychic projection.** What the Macrocosm thinks is His
"auto-internal projection," which becomes "the attributional emanation of the
universe … He creates everything within; there is nothing without" (SS 18). For the
unit, this auto-internal projection of the Macrocosm *is* its outer reality;
created objects are "shadowy objects" dependent on the Macropsychic imagination
(SS 5). In *The Divine Drama*, "all the unit minds of the universe along with the
cosmic mind make the mind of Parama Puruṣa," so nothing can lie outside His mind —
heaven and hell alike are within Him (AV 7).

**Macrocosm ↔ microcosm and the ethical pull.** The unit mind is "a mere bubble, a
mere spark of the Macrocosmic Mind." Macropsychic conation has "the faculty of
greatness"; micropsychic longings have only "the faculty of desiring … something
from somebody else" — *"the attribution of the Cosmic Mind is greatness, and that
of the unit mind is smallness"* (SS 18). Macrocosmic attraction plus the four-fold
microcosmic urge keep "the equilibrium of the universe" (SS 24). The discourses
therefore urge the sādhaka to convert finite *preya* into cosmic *shreya* and to
move from self-centred *khud-parastii* to God-centred *khudā-parastii* (SS 18).

# In the Discourses on Neohumanist Education — circum-rotation, unity of substance, and the bubbles of ideas

**DNE 23 (An Ideology for a New Generation, 28 Mar 1982) — the circum-rotarian
universe.** "This universe of ours, as you know, is circum-rotarian (that is,
rotating around its own nucleus). Nothing is outside its circumference, so it is
not necessary to search for its nucleus outside." The Hub of the universe is the
hub of the individual also: "This circum-rotarian universe is controlled by one
Centre both collectively and on a unitary basis. Thus each and every grain of
dust, each and every blade of grass – all are being equally controlled by that
Supreme Hub."

**DNE 24 (Devotional Sentiment and Neohumanism, 21 Feb 1982) — one substance
everywhere.** "All molecules, atoms, electrons, protons, positrons and neutrons
are the veritable expressions of the same Supreme Consciousness." Those who keep
this realization alive "are said to have attained perfection in life. They are
the real bhaktas [devotees]."

**DNE 8 (A Scriptological and Linguistic Survey of the World, 20 Feb 1989) —
the bubbles of ideas.** "Between the points of no magnitude there is a flow of
cognition. In that fluidal flow of cognition, bubbles are created. These bubbles
are the bubbles of ideas." When, "in the Cosmic emanation of the Supreme," these
bubbles "touch the unit 'I feeling', then unit ideas are created as a result of
close proximity to the Cosmic Ocean. These are the reflections or refractions of
Cosmic ideas." The unit "I" then tries to express them through its own
psycho-physical structure. So far as the refraction of the bubbles is concerned,
"the language of the universe is the same, was the same and will remain the same
forever" — **"The language of the 'inner voice' is always one and indivisible."**

## Relationships

- the Cosmic Mind is the mind of [Saguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/brahma.md) — "the composite of Cosmic Citta and Cosmic Operative Principle" (SS 7)
- it arises through the guṇa-impact of [Prakṛti's three principles](/cosmology/guna.md) on Puruṣa, producing in succession [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md), [Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md) and Cosmic [Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md)
- its outflowing macropsychic conation is the extroversial first half of [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) within the [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md), crudifying Cosmic Citta into the [five fundamental factors](/cosmology/five-fundamental-factors.md)
- it stands in deliberate contrast to the [unit mind / microcosm](/biopsychology/unit-mind.md), a "bubble" whose micropsychic longings are merely an auto-internal projection
- the pull from micropsychic to macropsychic is the ethical core later universalised as [Neohumanism](/philosophy/neohumanism.md) (SS 18)
- *DNE 23*: the universe is **circum-rotarian**, "controlled by one Centre both collectively and on a unitary basis"
- *DNE 24*: all molecules, atoms, electrons, protons, positrons and neutrons are "the veritable expressions of the same Supreme Consciousness"
- *DNE 8*: the flow of cognition between "points of no magnitude" creates the "bubbles of ideas"; the unit "I feeling" touching them yields unit ideas — reflections/refractions of Cosmic ideas; "The language of the 'inner voice' is always one and indivisible"

# Notes — fidelity

- **Sarkar's exact framing:** creation is Brahma's "auto-internal projection" /
  "Macropsychic imagination" *within* the Cosmic Citta — the universe is internal to
  Him and external only to the unit. This is NOT generic panpsychism; do not soften.
- **Terminology variation (preserve):** for the "existential I," SS 5 prints
  *Buddhitattva* ("Cosmic existential I") while SS 7 and the biopsychology nodes use
  *Mahattattva*. Same stage, two terms across discourses — flagged, not silently
  unified.
- **Could not verify:** no read passage gives a single canonical Saṃskṛta compound
  for "macropsychic conation" / "Cosmic Mind" beyond the English coinages and
  "Cosmic Citta" (hence no `sanskrit` field). The microcosm–Macrocosm framing is
  Sarkar's English; the source `.txt` carries minor OCR slips (e.g. a "micro-/macro-"
  swap in one SS 18 line) preserved verbatim in the verbatim layer.
