# Definition

**Saiñcara** is the extroversial movement of the Cosmic Cycle — the flow of the
Cognitive Body (Puruṣadeha) from subtle toward crude under the **increasing**
influence of [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md)'s [guṇas](/cosmology/guna.md).
Ánanda Sútram 1-5: *"Pravrttimukhii saiñcarah guṇadhārāyām."*

# Explanation

Centrifugal and object-ward (*pravṛtti*). From the nucleus
([Paramaśiva](/cosmology/paramashiva.md)) the successive guṇic impacts produce
Mahattattva, Ahaṃtattva, and citta, progressively crudifying consciousness. It
is the first half of the [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md).

# In *Idea and Ideology* (1959) — the mechanics of saincara

Sarkar's 1959 cosmology (*Saincara and Prāṇāḥ*, II-1) details the movement the
sūtras compress:

- **Triangle of forces.** The three guṇas are "three belligerent forces" whose
  eternal flow yields "a triangle of forces"; Puruṣa (here called **Shivānī**'s
  counterpole **Shiva**) is encircled by this triangle. The resultant of their
  internal clash-and-cohesion bursts out from a vertex; the Puruṣa at that vertex
  is **Shambhū**, the centre of the triangle is **Puruṣottama** — the "nucleus of
  all the creative principles" (cf. [Sṛṣṭimātṛkā](/cosmology/srstimatrka.md)).
- **The three "I"s.** From the resultants: sentient Prakṛti inculcates the pure
  "I" (*Mahattattva*, the Cosmic "I"); mutative Prakṛti metamorphoses it into the
  Cosmic Doer "I" (*Ahaṃtattva*); static Prakṛti objectivates a portion into the
  done "I" (*citta*). Mind is the collective name of the three (cf.
  [Mahattattva](/biopsychology/mahattattva.md), [Ahaṃtattva](/biopsychology/ahamtattva.md),
  [Citta](/biopsychology/citta.md)).
- **Crudification to the five factors.** Under increasing static pressure citta
  grows cruder: ethereal → aerial → luminous → liquid → solid, with increasing
  chemical affinity and decreasing inter-atomic space. The external pressure of
  static Prakṛti on the five factors is **bala**; its clash yields two opposing
  forces — interial (centre-seeking, maintaining solidarity) and exterial
  (fissiparous) — whose collective name is [prāṇa](/biopsychology/prana.md)
  ("energy").
- **Jadāshpoṭa & negative saincara.** If the exterial forces win, or the
  environment cannot sustain the vital energy, the structure bursts —
  **jadāshpoṭa** ("explosion of matter", also treated under the
  [Five Fundamental Factors](/cosmology/five-fundamental-factors.md)) — dissociating
  back into the five factors. This retracing is **negative saincara**, but it can
  never ascend above the ethereal factor: that would mean Ahamtattva withdrawing
  its "eternally active thought-projection," i.e. the end of creation. Hence there
  is **no "thermal death" of the universe** — the journey continues eternally.

- phase-of [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md)
- driven-by increasing [Guṇa](/cosmology/guna.md) bondage
- opposite-of [Pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md)
- resultant-bursts-from the [Sṛṣṭimātṛkā](/cosmology/srstimatrka.md) triangle; nucleus = [Puruṣottama](/cosmology/paramashiva.md)

- the *bala* clash within it yields [Prāṇa](/biopsychology/prana.md)
