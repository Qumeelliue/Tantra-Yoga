# Definition

Creation runs between two poles. Ánanda Sútram 4-6:
*"Shambhūliuṅgāt tasya vyaktih"* — "creation starts from **Shambhūliṅga**"; and
4-8 locates the **Svayambhūliṅga** as the ultimate point of crudity where the
[kuṇḍalinī](/practice/kundalini.md) resides.

# Explanation

- **Shambhūliṅga** — Puruṣa at the **vertex of the triangle**, the *common point of
  the theoretical and the practical evolution* and the **source of
  [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md)**. It is the **root-point of fundamental
  positivity**. From it issues first the **nāda** (a flow without any curvature),
  followed by the **kalā** (curvature) — see
  [pariṇāma](/cosmology/parinama.md).
- **Svayambhūliṅga** — the **ultimate point of manifestation and of fundamental
  negativity** (4-8), at the fringe of the [Bhavānī
  phase](/cosmology/stages-of-prakrti.md), wherein the **kulakuṇḍalinī** lies
  quiescent and coiled like a serpent.

Thus Shambhūliṅga (positivity, source) and Svayambhūliṅga (negativity, crudest
terminus) bracket the whole saiñcara flow; nāda → kalā is the movement between.

## Relationships

- Shambhūliṅga = source of [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md), the vertex of the [Sṛṣṭimātṛkā triangle](/cosmology/srstimatrka.md)
- nāda → kalā issue from it — the substrate of [pariṇāma](/cosmology/parinama.md)
- Svayambhūliṅga = seat of the dormant [Kuṇḍalinī](/practice/kundalini.md)
