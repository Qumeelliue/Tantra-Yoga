# Definition

**Vidyā** and **Avidyā** are the two momenta of *manifested*
[Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md), arising from the disparity (loss of equilibrium)
among her three [guṇas](/cosmology/guna.md): where **sattva** predominates the
force is **Vidyāmāyā** — the introversive, **centripetal** pull toward the Nucleus
(the Subtle); where **tamah** predominates it is **Avidyāmāyā** — the extroversive,
**centrifugal** pull toward crudity. **Māyā** is the Creative Principle (Prakṛti in
disequilibrium) whose binding, Avidyā-dominant aspect tethers the unit to the
diversified, illusory world.

# Explanation

- In **equipoise** (Mūlā Prakṛti) there is no movement; manifestation begins with
  guṇa-disparity. "As you think so you become" — crude ideation crudifies the mind,
  and all complexes (fear, inferiority, hopelessness) are creations of Avidyā.
- **Avidyāmāyā** works through two sub-forces — *Vikṣepa* (the centrifugal,
  distancing force) and *Āvaraṇī* (the obstructive, covering force); **Vidyāmāyā**
  through *Saṃvit* (the introspective force awakening self-realization) and
  *Hlādinī* (the sublimative force propelling the sādhaka to the Supreme, which
  Sarkar identifies with Shrii Rādhā). Where the two balance, the equilibrium is
  the *tāṭasthā* ("coastal") force.
- **Prakṛti and Māyā are "almost synonymous"** — Prakṛti the equilibrated/unmanifest
  state, Māyā the disequilibrated Creative Principle, "the causal matrix of the
  creation of this disparate, illusory world."
- Māyā is named by **scope of operation**: *Mahāmāyā* (the sum of manifested Māyā),
  *Yogamāyā* (within the jīva, the urge "something is to be done"), *Viṣṇumāyā*
  (the all-pervading portion within creation), *Vishvamāyā* (makes the jīva see a
  pot as a pot, unable to see Him in it), *Aṇumāyā* (the unit's own
  world-creation). Liberation is specifically *vishvamāyā-nivṛtti*.
- **Sādhanā is the internal fight against Avidyā:** take the help of Vidyāmāyā, then
  ultimately forgo even Vidyā. Avidyā dominates during
  [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) and dwindles during
  [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md). One wins freedom **not by fighting**
  Māyā (suppression rebounds) but by **channelizing** her toward the Absolute (He is
  *Māyādhīṣa*); the final *Bhāvasāgar* is crossed only by devotion.
- **Parā vs Aparā knowledge:** *aparā* "subjectivizes the external objectivity"
  (temporary, imperfect — 49 of the 50 propensities); *parā* "subjectivizes the
  internal objectivity," its subject being Parama Puruṣa Himself (exact, permanent —
  the fiftieth propensity). The right course is maximum parā plus necessary aparā.

## Relationships

- the two momenta of [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md), arising from [Guṇa](/cosmology/guna.md) disparity
- contrasts with [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md) (Saguṇa needs active Māyā; Nirguṇa is Māyā-free)
- Avidyā dominates [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md); Vidyā prevails in [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md) — the centripetal/centrifugal movement of the [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md)
- overcome through [Sādhanā](/practice/sadhana.md); liberation (vishvamāyā-nivṛtti) is owned by [Mokṣa](/practice/moksa.md); the final gap crossed by [Bhakti](/practice/bhakti.md)
- parā/aparā knowledge relates to valid cognition — cf. [Pramā](/philosophy/prama.md)

# Notes — fidelity

- "Centripetal = Vidyā / centrifugal = Avidyā" is Sarkar's **direct teaching**.
- The five-fold Māyā list **varies between discourses** (AV 2/46 vs SS 19/4 differ
  on membership and on where Viṣṇumāyā sits) — complementary, not contradictory;
  preserve both. *Hlādinī = Shrii Rādhā* is Sarkar's stated equation. Core doctrine
  is stable across 23 years (1956 → 1979).
- No Advaita "neti neti" pedigree is imported — the framing is strictly
  Prakṛti-guṇa-based. The mokṣa **grades** are not defined here (they live in
  [Mokṣa](/practice/moksa.md)).
