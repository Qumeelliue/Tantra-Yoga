# Definition

**Paramaśiva** (also **Puruṣottama**) is Supreme Consciousness at the **nucleus**
of the universe / Cosmic Cycle. Ánanda Sútram 1-4:
*"Paramashivah Puruṣottamah vishvasya kendram."*

# Explanation

The nucleus of the [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md) is the *svabhāva* of
[Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md); the material cause of the whole cycle is
Puruṣa / Shiva, and this nuclear bearing is named Paramaśiva or Puruṣottama.
Both [saiñcara](/cosmology/saincara.md) and [pratisaiñcara](/cosmology/pratisaincara.md)
move with reference to it.

# In the *Discourses on Tantra* (1990) — Parashiva & Aparashiva

The chapter *The Balanced Triangle of Forces* (DT 1/6) develops the pre-creation
nucleus:

- **Parashiva = Guṇamaya.** In the balanced triangle of forces at the pre-creation
  stage — "when the unmanifested primordial Cognition has not yet come under the
  influence of the unmanifested primordial forces," purely subjective with nothing
  objective opposite — the Cognitive Faculty is called **Guṇamaya**; **Parashiva** is
  its philosophical equivalent.
- **Aparashiva.** When the three forces in manifestation give rise to an objective case
  opposite, that antithesis is called **Aparashiva** — "although Guṇamaya in the
  practical sphere, is actually not Guṇamaya in the theoretical sphere," because the
  alternation of manifestation/non-manifestation continues with the emanation and
  reflection "from the points of Shambhūliṅga and Svayambhūliṅga."
- **The concept is recognized across the Tantric schools** — Hindu, Buddhist
  (Vajrayāna, Mantrayāna, Tantrayāna, Kālacakrayāna), and Rājādhirāja Yoga; Nairātma
  Devii of Kālacakrayāna and Shūnyātma Devii of Kaṅkāla-mālinii are "simply
  alternative names of Parashiva."
- **The pure cult.** The purest part of Shaeva Tantra, beyond Dakṣiṇācāra and
  Vāmācāra, formerly learned in *sāmrājya dīkṣā* from a *kaola guru*, "is now included
  in Rājādhirāja Yoga" — some of Ananda Marga sādhanā's subtler processes bear "a
  close relation to Rājādhirāja" (introduced by Maharṣi Aṣṭāvakra at Vakreshvar tīrtha,
  first initiated to Prince Alarka). Later Shaeva Tantra "altogether lost its
  high-grade spiritual cult," leaving only distorted Vāmācāra remnants (the
  Aghorpanthā school).

# Notes — fidelity

- **Parashiva (Guṇamaya) and Aparashiva** are the two faces of the pre-creation
  nucleus — keep the pair; Aparashiva is Guṇamaya in practice but not in theory.
- The **Tantric-school survey** (Saora, Gāṇapatya, Shaeva, Shākta, Vaeṣṇava) and the
  Rājādhirāja history (Aṣṭāvakra, Alarka) are Sarkar's own — reproduce as his
  framing, dated 1990.
- **Could not verify in the cited passage:** any sūtra-level doctrine of Parashiva
  beyond 1-4's nucleus; the DT chapter is the elaboration.
