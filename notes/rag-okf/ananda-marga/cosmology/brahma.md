# Definition

**Brahma** is the Cosmic Entity — the composite of **Shiva** (Consciousness) and
**Shakti** (Operative Principle). Ánanda Sútram 1-1: *"Shivashaktyātmakaṃ
Brahma."*

# Explanation

[Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md) (Consciousness) and [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md)
(Operative Principle) exist in **inalienable concomitance** — like the two sides
of a single sheet of paper: removing one jeopardizes the other, and neither can
stand alone. Brahma is therefore not a deity but the non-dual cosmic totality.
The same witnessing entity is, in common usage, called *ātman* — "that which is
omni-telepathic" — by virtue of which all mundane objects find their factual
substantiation.

# In *Elementary Philosophy* (1955)

Chapters 2–3 expound Brahma at length in **Puruṣa / Prakṛti** vocabulary (not Shiva/Shakti),
using the analogy of **fire and its burning property** for their inseparability. Brahma is
the **collective term** for Puruṣa + Prakṛti, named *"Paramātman, Bhūmācaetanya, Universal
Consciousness"* (also Citishakti, Puruṣa). Being subtle, *"Brahma must be shapeless or
formless."* Consciousness is shown *"absolutely independent"* and hence **non-causal** —
per the Nāsadiiya Sūkta, *"even Brahma does not know the reason why It came into existence"*
— and **infinite** (*"if a part can be infinite, Brahma, the whole, is bound to be
infinite"*). **Bhagavān** (luminous, benevolent, omnipotent) is **qualified Puruṣa only** —
*"Thus Bhagavān is only Saguṇa Brahma"* — so to know the supreme one must step beyond
Bhagavān to Nirguṇa (developed in [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md)).

**"What is this world?" (ch.3): the world is Brahma.** Tracing any object back through the
five factors to consciousness, *"the entire creation is only made of Brahma"* — with no
external material, *"this creation is only Saguṇa Brahma metamorphosed,"* and *"Every speck
of dust is only Brahma."* The stuff projected into all forms is **Puruṣa** (Prakṛti, a mere
qualifying force, cannot take shape). Yet since subtle Puruṣa held no crude "seed," the
universe arises as a **thought-projection / imagination (kalpanā)** of the Cosmic Mind — so
it is neither wholly true nor false but **"relative truth"** (*"it is something between the
two"*); Brahma alone is Satya. (The Cosmic-Mind machinery and the subtle→crude→subtle cycle
are developed in [Cosmic Mind](/cosmology/cosmic-mind.md) and [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md).)

# In *Neohumanism in a Nutshell* (1979) — sourceless, all-pervading, all-covering

*Dogma – No More* (nh1-7, 23 September 1979) adds the non-causal and all-pervading
description of Parama Puruṣa:

- **Sourceless, non-causal.** "He is sourceless, He is non-causal. Everything in our
  universe is causal… His cause is known only to His devotees and to nobody else. That
  Supreme Entity who is beyond the periphery of the microcosm, is the creator of the
  microcosm."
- **Within every pore and without.** "Units exist within His jurisdiction, and He is within
  each and every pore of the unit. That is why He is all-pervading. And He is not only
  within, but without, outside. That is why He is within you, He is outside you, He is
  all-pervading and He is all-covering."
- **Coinciding nuclei.** "When one enlarges one's jurisdiction as a microcosm, when one's
  nucleus coincides with the nucleus of the Supreme, one becomes one with the Supreme."

## Relationships

- composite-of [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md) (Shiva) + [Prakṛti](/cosmology/prakrti.md) (Shakti)
- expresses-as [Brahmacakra](/cosmology/brahmacakra.md)
- *Elementary Philosophy*: the whole creation is **Brahma metamorphosed** ("every speck of dust is only Brahma"), a kalpanā / **relative truth**; Bhagavān = [Saguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md) only, not the supreme Nirguṇa state
- *nh1-7 (1979)*: Parama Puruṣa is "sourceless, He is non-causal" while "everything in our universe is causal"; "He is within each and every pore of the unit" and "within you, He is outside you… all-pervading and He is all-covering"; "when one's nucleus coincides with the nucleus of the Supreme, one becomes one with the Supreme" (cf. [Puruṣa](/cosmology/purusa.md), [Saguṇa & Nirguṇa Brahma](/cosmology/saguna-nirguna-brahma.md))

# Notes — fidelity

- **Do not conflate** Sarkar's *Brahma* (Cosmic Entity = Puruṣa + Prakṛti) with
  the Purāṇic creator-god *Brahmā* — distinct concepts.
- "Shiva" / "Puruṣa" are used as philosophical synonyms here; "ātman" is the same
  entity in common parlance.
- In *Elementary Philosophy* the term **ātman = unit consciousness** (the part reflected
  in beings), distinct from Bhūmācaetanya/Cosmic Consciousness (the totality) — see
  [Ātman](/cosmology/atman.md); and those chapters use **Puruṣa/Prakṛti** (never
  Shiva/Shakti) with the **fire-and-burning-property** analogy, not the two-sides-of-paper image.
- **nh1-7 (1979)** states the non-causal, all-pervading/all-covering nature of Parama
  Puruṣa and the "nucleus coincides" oneness — direct teaching, compatible with the
  *Elementary Philosophy* "non-causal" claim but in the vocabulary of "sourceless /
  non-causal."
