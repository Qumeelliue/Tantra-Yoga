# Ananda Marga — Philosophy Knowledge Base

A curated OKF bundle mapping the philosophy of Shrii Shrii Anandamurti
(P. R. Sarkar) — spiritual philosophy, biopsychology, cosmology, practice, and
the social theory PROUT — into atomic, cross-linked, source-cited concepts.
It is designed as a high-fidelity ontology that can later feed a
retrieval / GraphRAG layer. Read [About & conventions](meta/about.md) and the
[Parsing protocol](meta/parsing-protocol.md) before adding concepts.

> **Status:** pilot underway. The Ánanda Sútram verbatim layer is complete (85
> passages in [works/ananda-sutram/](works/ananda-sutram/)); the concept layer is
> growing (ch. 1 cosmology done). The taxonomy below is a working draft — extensible.

# Categories

* [Cosmology](cosmology/) - Brahmacakra, Puruśa / Prakŕti, saiṋcara / pratisaiṋcara, the guṇas.
* [Biopsychology](biopsychology/) - Kośas, cakras, vṛttis (propensities), kuṇḍalinii, saṃskāra.
* [Practice](practice/) - Sādhanā, Tantra, kiirtana, yama-niyama, the Sixteen Points.
* [Philosophy & Ethics](philosophy/) - Neohumanism, microvita, dharma, ethics, Ánanda Sútram.
* [PROUT](prout/) - Progressive Utilization Theory: the social cycle, sadvipra, economic democracy.
* [Glossary](glossary/) - Saṃskṛta terms as controlled-vocabulary anchors.
* [Works](works/) - Source texts and discourses (provenance targets).
* [People](people/) - The author and key figures.
